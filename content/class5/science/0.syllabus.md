# 📖 Syllabus map — The World Around Us

Class 5 doesn't have a separate science book yet — **The World Around Us** (book: *Our Wondrous World*) blends science with the world we live in. Ten chapters in five units:

## Unit 1 — Life Around Us

1. **Water — The Essence of Life** — why every living thing needs water, where our water comes from, saving it.
2. **Journey of a River** — a river from source to sea, and everything it feeds along the way.

## Unit 2 — Health and Well-being

3. **The Mystery of Food** — what's in our food and why the body needs it.
4. **Our School — A Happy Place** — health, safety and working together at school.

## Unit 3 — Incredible India

5. **Our Vibrant Country** — India's land, people and diversity.
6. **Some Unique Places** — special natural and cultural places of India.

## Unit 4 — Things Around Us

7. **Energy — How Things Work** — what makes things go: fuels, electricity, the Sun.
8. **Clothes — How Things are Made** — from fibre to fabric to clothing.

## Unit 5 — Our Amazing Planet

9. **Rhythms of Nature** — day & night, seasons, and nature's repeating cycles.
10. **Earth — Our Shared Home** — caring for the planet we share with all living things.
