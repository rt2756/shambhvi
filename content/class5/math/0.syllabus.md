The whole year at a glance — NCERT's **Maths Mela** (Class 5). Notes for each chapter will appear as their own panels here as they're written.

1. **We the Travellers — I** — big numbers: place value up to **ten-thousands**, reading and writing numbers beyond 10,000.
2. **Fractions** — parts of a whole, equal shares, comparing simple fractions.
3. **Angles as Turns** — angles seen as **turning**: quarter, half and full turns, right angles.
4. **We the Travellers — II** — the journey continues: comparing and calculating with the big numbers from Chapter 1.
5. **Far and Near** — units of distance (m, km) and converting between units of length.
6. **The Dairy Farm** — multiplication: different ways to multiply, and multiplication in real situations.
7. **Shapes and Patterns** — 2D shapes and the patterns they make.
8. **Weight and Capacity** — grams & kilograms, millilitres & litres — measuring how heavy and how much.
9. **Coconut Farm** — division: splitting into groups, and how division and multiplication are the same fact family.
10. **Symmetrical Designs** — mirror halves and symmetric patterns.
11. **Grandmother's Quilt** — covering surfaces with shapes (tiling) — the seed of the area idea.
12. **Racing Seconds** — time: seconds, 12-hour and 24-hour clock formats.
13. **Animal Jumps** — number patterns from equal jumps: multiples and hidden-number puzzles.
14. **Maps and Locations** — reading maps, directions and routes.
15. **Data Through Pictures** — collecting data and showing it in pictures (pictographs).

> [!NOTE]
> Class 5's new book stops at **ten-thousands** — no decimals, integers or degree-measurement of angles yet; angles are *turns* and symmetry is folding & turning. That's by design: the deep versions arrive in Classes 6–7.
