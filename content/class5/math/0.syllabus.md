The whole year at a glance — NCERT's **Maths Mela** (Class 5).

**Every chapter has detailed notes (with the book's activities worked out) and a matching 4-level test.**

1. ✅ **We the Travellers — I** — big numbers: place value up to **ten-thousands**, comparing, rounding to the nearest 10/100/1,000, and number puzzles.
2. ✅ **Fractions** — equivalent fractions, comparing fractions, fractions greater than 1, and comparing with 1 and ½.
3. ✅ **Angles as Turns** — angles seen as **turning**: quarter, half and full turns, acute/right/obtuse/straight, clockwise and anti-clockwise.
4. ✅ **We the Travellers — II** — adding and subtracting large numbers, quick sums to 100/1,000, consecutive-number sums, odd and even.
5. ✅ **Far and Near** — mm, cm, m and km — converting, comparing, adding, subtracting, multiplying and dividing lengths.
6. ✅ **The Dairy Farm** — multiplication: different ways to multiply, and multiplication in real situations.
7. ✅ **Shapes and Patterns** — 2D shapes and the patterns they make.
8. ✅ **Weight and Capacity** — mg, g, kg, quintal and tonne; ml and l — converting and calculating.
9. ✅ **Coconut Farm** — division: splitting into groups, and how division and multiplication are the same fact family.
10. ✅ **Symmetrical Designs** — mirror halves and symmetric patterns.
11. ✅ **Grandmother's Quilt** — perimeter and area: tiling, square units, length × breadth.
12. ✅ **Racing Seconds** — time: seconds, 12-hour and 24-hour clock formats.
13. ✅ **Animal Jumps** — number patterns from equal jumps: multiples and hidden-number puzzles.
14. ✅ **Maps and Locations** — reading maps, directions and routes.
15. ✅ **Data Through Pictures** — collecting data and showing it in pictures (pictographs).

> [!NOTE]
> Class 5's new book stops at **ten-thousands** — no decimals, integers or degree-measurement of angles yet; angles are *turns* and symmetry is folding & turning. That's by design: the deep versions arrive in Classes 6–7.
