# ➗ Fractions & decimals

## Fractions

- A fraction shows **part of a whole** or **part of a group**: `3/4` means 3 out of 4 equal parts.
- **Numerator** = top number; it tells how many total pieces we have
- **Denominator** = bottom number; it tells the total number of equal pieces.

- **Proper fraction:** numerator < denominator, so the value is less than 1 whole (`2/3`, `5/8`).
- **Improper fraction:** numerator >= denominator, so the value is 1 whole or more (`7/4`, `3/3`).
- **Mixed number:** a whole number and a fraction together (`1 1/2`, `2 3/4`).
- **Improper to mixed:** divide the numerator by the denominator; the **quotient** is the whole number and the **remainder** is the new numerator (`11/4` → 11 ÷ 4 = 2 remainder 3 → `2 3/4`).
- **Mixed to improper:** multiply the whole by the denominator, add the numerator, and keep the same denominator (`2 3/4` → (2 × 4) + 3 = 11 → `11/4`).
- **Equivalent fractions:** different names for the same amount. Multiply or divide the top and bottom by the same number (`1/2 = 2/4 = 4/8`).
- **Simplest form:** divide the top and bottom by a common factor until the fraction cannot be made smaller (`4/6 = 2/3`).
- **Fraction bar means division:** `6/2` is the same as `6 ÷ 2`, which equals 3.
- **"of" means Multiplication**: `1/2 of 10 = 1/2 x 10 = 10/2 = 5`.

> [!WARNING]
> A fraction only works when the whole is cut into **equal** parts. Unequal pieces cannot be counted fairly.

## Fraction operations

- **Add / subtract with the same denominator:** add or subtract only the numerators; keep the denominator the same (`1/5 + 2/5 = 3/5`).
- **Add / subtract with different denominators:** first change them into equivalent fractions with a common denominator, usually using the LCM.
- **Compare fractions:** if denominators match, compare numerators; if they do not match, make a common denominator first.
- **Multiply:** no matching needed; multiply straight across (`2/3 × 4/5 = 8/15`).
- **Divide:** use **Keep-Change-Flip**: keep the first fraction, change `÷` to `×`, then flip the second fraction (`1/2 ÷ 3/4 = 1/2 × 4/3 = 4/6 = 2/3`).

> [!TIP]
> After any fraction answer, check if it can be simplified.

> [!WARNING]
> Add or subtract only after the denominators match (`1/2 + 1/3 ≠ 2/5`). For division, flip only the **second** fraction.

## Decimals & money

- The point splits whole and part: 4.73 = 4 + 7/10 + 3/100 (tenths, hundredths, thousandths).
- **Compare:** line up the point and compare place by place; 0.5 = 0.50.
- **Add / subtract:** line up the decimal points.
- **Fraction ↔ decimal:**
  - **Fraction → decimal:** divide numerator ÷ denominator (`3/4` → 3 ÷ 4 = 0.75; `1/2` → 1 ÷ 2 = 0.5).
  - **Decimal → fraction:** write the digits over the place value (tenths, hundredths, …), then simplify (`0.25` = 25/100 = 1/4).
- **Money:** 100 paise = ₹1; write amounts with 2 decimal places (₹12.50).

> [!WARNING]
> 0.5 > 0.45 (line up the point, don't count digits) · keep 2 places after the point for paise.
