# 13. Exponents & powers

## Meaning

- `aⁿ` means **a multiplied by itself n times** — a is the **base**, n is the **exponent (power)**.
- `2⁴ = 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 = 16` — read "2 raised to the power 4".
- `10³ = 1000` (the power counts the zeros) · `10⁶` = 1 million.
- `a¹ = a` · **`a⁰ = 1`** for any non-zero number (`100⁰ = 1`).
- **Negative base:** even power → **positive**, odd power → **negative**: `(−2)⁴ = 16` but `(−2)³ = −8` · `(−1)ⁿ` = 1 when n is even, −1 when n is odd.

## Laws of exponents

- **Same base, multiplying → add the powers:** `aᵐ × aⁿ = aᵐ⁺ⁿ` → `2³ × 2⁴ = 2⁷ = 128`.
- **Same base, dividing → subtract the powers:** `aᵐ ÷ aⁿ = aᵐ⁻ⁿ` → `5⁶ ÷ 5⁴ = 5² = 25`.
- **Power of a power → multiply the powers:** `(aᵐ)ⁿ = aᵐⁿ` → `(3²)⁴ = 3⁸` (because 2 × 4 = 8).
- **Same power, different bases:** `aᵐ × bᵐ = (a × b)ᵐ` → `2³ × 5³ = 10³ = 1000` · `aᵐ ÷ bᵐ = (a ÷ b)ᵐ` → `6⁴ ÷ 3⁴ = 2⁴ = 16`.

> [!WARNING]
> `2³ × 2⁴ = 2⁷` — the base does **not** change · the laws need the **same base** (or the same power) · `a⁰ = 1`, not 0 · `2³ = 8`, not `2 × 3 = 6`.

## Expanded form with powers of 10

- `47561 = 4 × 10⁴ + 7 × 10³ + 5 × 10² + 6 × 10¹ + 1 × 10⁰`.

## Standard form (for very large numbers)

- Write the number as **(a number between 1 and 10) × (a power of 10)**.
- Steps: put the decimal point just after the **first digit**; the power of 10 = how many places the point moved.
- `8500 = 8.5 × 10³` · `597000000 = 5.97 × 10⁸`.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Distance from the Earth to the Sun ≈ 150,000,000 km = `1.5 × 10⁸` km.
