# 8. Lines & angles

## Angle pairs

- **Complementary angles:** two angles adding to **90°** (`35° + 55°`). Complement of x = `90° − x`.
- **Supplementary angles:** two angles adding to **180°** (`110° + 70°`). Supplement of x = `180° − x`.
- **Adjacent angles:** share a **common vertex** and a **common arm**; the other two arms sit on either side (no overlap).
- **Linear pair:** adjacent angles whose outer arms make a **straight line** — they always add to **180°**.
- **Vertically opposite angles:** two crossing lines make 2 pairs of opposite angles — opposite angles are always **equal**.

> [!TIP]
> **C**omplementary → **C**orner (90°) · **S**upplementary → **S**traight line (180°).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Two lines cross and one angle is 50°. The angle vertically opposite it = **50°**; each neighbour = `180° − 50°` = **130°** (linear pairs).

## Transversal across parallel lines

- A **transversal** is a line that cuts two lines at two different points — it creates **8 angles**.
- When the two lines are **parallel**:
  - **Corresponding angles** (F shape) are **equal**.
  - **Alternate interior angles** (Z shape) are **equal**.
  - **Alternate exterior angles** are **equal**.
  - **Interior angles on the same side** of the transversal (C shape, "co-interior") add to **180°**.
- **Test for parallel lines:** if any one of these pairs behaves as above, the two lines must be parallel.

> [!WARNING]
> Co-interior angles are **supplementary (180°)**, not equal · corresponding/alternate angles are equal **only when the lines are parallel**.
