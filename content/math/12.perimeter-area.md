# 12. Perimeter & area

## Formulas

| Shape | Perimeter | Area |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Square (side `s`) | `4 × s` | `s × s` |
| Rectangle (`l`, `b`) | `2 × (l + b)` | `l × b` |
| Parallelogram (base `b`, height `h`) | add all 4 sides | `b × h` |
| Triangle (base `b`, height `h`) | add all 3 sides | `1/2 × b × h` |
| Circle (radius `r`) | circumference = `2 × π × r` | `π × r²` |

- **π ≈ 22/7** (handy when r is a multiple of 7) or **3.14**.
- Circle: `diameter d = 2r`, so circumference also = `π × d`.
- The **height must be perpendicular (90°)** to the chosen base — never the slanted side.
- A triangle is **half** a parallelogram with the same base and height — that's where the `1/2` comes from.

## Reading the question

- **Perimeter** = length around → fencing, border, lace, frame — answer in **cm / m**.
- **Area** = surface covered → tiles, paint, carpet, field — answer in **cm² / m²**.
- **Cost = rate × amount:** rate per metre → use **perimeter**; rate per square metre → use **area**.
- **Path / border problems:** area of the path = **outer area − inner area**.

## Unit conversions

- `1 cm² = 10 × 10 = 100 mm²` · `1 m² = 100 × 100 = 10000 cm²`.
- **1 hectare = 10000 m²** — the area of a 100 m × 100 m square.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> A park is 60 m × 40 m. Perimeter = `2 × (60 + 40) = 200 m`, so fencing at ₹15 per metre costs `200 × 15` = ₹3000. Area = `60 × 40 = 2400 m²`.

> [!WARNING]
> Same perimeter does **not** mean same area · convert everything to **one unit before** using a formula · area units are squared (m²), perimeter units are not.
