# 📖 Syllabus map — Ganita Prakash

<!-- Chapter list verified against ncert_books/class_6/maths.pdf (NCERT Ganita Prakash, 2024). -->

The whole year at a glance — NCERT's **Ganita Prakash** (Class 6). **Every chapter has full notes and a matching practice set** on the Maths Questions page.

1. ✅ **Patterns in Mathematics** — maths as the search for patterns: number sequences, shape sequences, and why spotting the pattern *is* the maths.
2. ✅ **Lines and Angles** — points, lines, rays and segments; measuring angles in degrees for the first time.
3. ✅ **Number Play** — playing with numbers: arrangements, estimates, digit games and number puzzles.
4. ✅ **Data Handling and Presentation** — collecting data, pictographs and bar graphs.
5. ✅ **Prime Time** — factors and multiples, prime and composite numbers, divisibility.
6. ✅ **Perimeter and Area** — distance around vs surface covered, for squares, rectangles and shape combinations.
7. ✅ **Fractions** — fractions as equal shares and as numbers on the number line; comparing, adding, subtracting.
8. ✅ **Playing with Constructions** — drawing with ruler and compass: circles, perpendiculars, copying shapes.
9. ✅ **Symmetry** — line (folding) symmetry and rotational (turning) symmetry.
10. ✅ **The Other Side of Zero** — numbers *below* zero: negative numbers and the full number line.

> [!TIP]
> Chapters 5, 6, 7, 9 and 10 are the direct foundations of the Class 7 chapters on numbers, fractions, perimeter–area and symmetry — revising them from here is the fastest way to patch a Class 7 gap.
