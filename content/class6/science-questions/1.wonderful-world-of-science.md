What is **science**?

> [!ANSWER]
> A **way of thinking, observing and doing things** to understand the world we live in and to uncover the secrets of the universe.
>
> It is a **step-by-step process for answering questions** — not a pile of facts to memorise.

---

"Science is just memorising facts and figures." Why is this wrong?

> [!ANSWER]
> Facts are the *result* of science, not science itself.
>
> Science is the **process** — observing, questioning, guessing, testing and analysing. Someone who memorises every fact but can't investigate a new question isn't doing science.

---

Why is science compared to a **giant jigsaw puzzle**?

> [!ANSWER]
> Every discovery **adds a piece**, and there is **no limit** to how many pieces there are — each new piece of knowledge leads to **more questions**.
>
> And sometimes a piece turns out to be **in the wrong place** and must be moved: new discoveries **change** our earlier understanding.

---

Is it a problem when a new discovery shows that older scientific ideas were wrong?

> [!ANSWER]
> **No — that is science working properly.**
>
> Being willing to move a wrongly-placed piece is part of the method. Refusing to change your mind when the evidence says otherwise is what would break it.

---

List the **five steps** of the scientific method in order.

> [!ANSWER]
> **1. Observe** something interesting or puzzling.
>
> **2. Question** — form a question about it.
>
> **3. Guess** a possible answer.
>
> **4. Test** the guess through experiments or more observations.
>
> **5. Analyse** the results to see whether they actually answer the question.

---

Your pen stops writing. Walk through the scientific method to find out why.

> [!ANSWER]
> **Observe:** the pen has stopped writing. **Question:** "Why did it stop?"
>
> **Guess:** the ink is finished. **Test:** open the pen and check the refill.
>
> **Analyse:** if the refill is empty, the guess was right.

---

You check the refill and the ink is **not** finished. Has the method failed?

> [!ANSWER]
> **No.** A wrong guess **removes one possibility** — that's real progress.
>
> You simply **make a new guess** ("perhaps the ink dried up") and **test that one**. Repeating the cycle until something fits is exactly how science works.

---

Who counts as a **scientist**?

> [!ANSWER]
> Scientists are people who **follow the scientific method** to solve problems or discover new things.
>
> But **anyone** who follows it is doing science — including you, working out why your pen stopped.

---

Why does one science book cover things as different as food, water, magnets and stars?

> [!ANSWER]
> Because **the ideas are all connected**. As you discover more, you start to see how topics that look separate rely on the same underlying ideas.
>
> The book starts with **Earth** — the only planet we know that supports life, and one whose environment we must protect — before looking outward to the Sun, Moon and stars.
