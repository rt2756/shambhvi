## What science is

> **Science is a way of thinking, observing and doing things** to understand the world we live in and uncover the secrets of the universe.

- It is **not** about memorising facts and figures, and not only about doing experiments — it is a **step-by-step process for finding answers to questions**.
- It is **everywhere**: in the kitchen, on the playground, in the depths of the ocean and in outer space. Groundbreaking discoveries have often come from unexpected places.

> [!TIP]
> **Science is like a giant, unending jigsaw puzzle.** Every discovery adds a piece — and every new piece raises **more** questions. Sometimes we find a piece was **put in the wrong place** and has to be moved: new discoveries often **change** our understanding of the world. Being willing to move a piece is part of doing science well.

## The scientific method

You already use it. **Your pen stops writing:**

1. **Observe** — the pen has stopped working.
2. **Question** — "Why did my pen stop writing?"
3. **Guess** a possible answer — "the ink is finished."
4. **Test** the guess — open the pen and check the refill.
5. **Analyse** the result — if the refill is empty, the guess was right. If it's **not** empty, the guess was **wrong**, so make a **new** guess ("maybe the ink dried up") and test that one.

**The five steps:**

> **Observe → Question → Guess → Test → Analyse**

**Why the loop matters:** a wrong guess isn't a failure — it **removes one possibility** and sends you back to guess again. That repeating cycle is exactly how science makes progress.

- **Scientists** are people who follow the scientific method to solve problems and discover new things — but **anyone** who follows it is doing science.

## What this book explores

Everything connects, which is why one book covers such different-looking topics:

- **Living world** — the huge variety of plants and animals, and how they grow.
- **Food** — India's many cuisines, what food is made of, and eating well.
- **Water** — why it rains; how water freezes to ice and boils to steam.
- **Heat** — understanding hot and cold, and measuring temperature (of water, or of your body when you have a fever).
- **Materials** — paper, metal, plastic, rubber, cloth; what things are made of and how to separate them.
- **Beyond Earth** — the Sun, the Moon and the millions of stars.

> [!NOTE]
> **Earth is the only planet we know that supports life**, and it has an environment we must protect. That's why the book starts here before looking outward.
