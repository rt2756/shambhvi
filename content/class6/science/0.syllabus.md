# 📖 Syllabus map — Curiosity (Class 6)

<!-- Chapter list verified against ncert_books/class_6/science.pdf (NCERT Curiosity, 2024). -->

The whole year at a glance — NCERT's **Curiosity** (Class 6), the first book of the series that Class 7 science continues from. **Every chapter has full notes and a matching practice set** on the Science Questions page.

1. ✅ **The Wonderful World of Science** — what science *is*: observing, questioning, testing (intro chapter).
2. ✅ **Diversity in the Living World** — the huge variety of plants and animals, and grouping them by features and habitats.
3. ✅ **Mindful Eating: A Path to a Healthy Body** — food, nutrients and a balanced diet.
4. ✅ **Exploring Magnets** — magnetic and non-magnetic materials, poles, attraction and repulsion.
5. ✅ **Measurement of Length and Motion** — standard units, measuring correctly, and types of motion.
6. ✅ **Materials Around Us** — sorting materials by their properties (hard/soft, transparent/opaque, soluble…).
7. ✅ **Temperature and its Measurement** — hot and cold made precise: thermometers and the Celsius scale.
8. ✅ **A Journey through States of Water** — evaporation, condensation and the water cycle.
9. ✅ **Methods of Separation in Everyday Life** — sieving, winnowing, filtration, evaporation — the right tool for each mixture.
10. ✅ **Living Creatures: Exploring their Characteristics** — what makes something *living*: growth, respiration, reproduction, response.
11. ✅ **Nature's Treasures** — air, water, soil, sunlight and energy sources — nature's resources and using them wisely.
12. ✅ **Beyond Earth** — the night sky: Moon, stars and the Solar System.
