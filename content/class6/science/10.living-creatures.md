## A shell that isn't empty

On a morning walk, **Avadhi** tries to pick up some **shells**. Her mother stops her: the shell may be home to a **living snail** — it's **part of the snail's body**. Avadhi and **Aayush** wonder: how can something that **isn't even moving** be part of a living thing? Their teacher starts a class discussion: **what makes something living?**

## What sets the living apart?

**Activity 10.1:** list things around you — pencil, book, pigeon, car, plant — guess living or non-living, and give a reason.

> [!WARNING]
> **Movement alone doesn't prove life.** A **car moves** but isn't living. A **plant doesn't walk about** but is living. **Use yourself as the yardstick** — what can you do that a car can't? Grow, eat, breathe, have young ones…

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **All living beings:**
>
> | Characteristic | In animals | In plants |
> |---|---|---|
> | **Move** | walk, fly, swim | **flowers open**; **Drosera** curls hairs round insects; **climbers** wind round supports |
> | **Need food (nutrition)** | eat | make their own food |
> | **Grow** | your old clothes don't fit | seedling → tree |
> | **Respire** (breathing is part of it) | breathe in and out | air moves in and out through **stomata** — tiny pores on leaves |
> | **Excrete** (remove wastes) | **sweat** (water + salts), **urine** | give out extra water and minerals as **droplets on leaves** (grass, rose) |
> | **Respond to stimuli** | pull your foot off a thorn | **touch-me-not (chhui-mui)** folds its leaves; **amla** leaves fold together after **sunset** |
> | **Reproduce** | kittens, puppies, calves | seeds → new plants |
> | **Die** | | |
>
> **If something does NOT show these features, it is non-living.**

> [!NOTE]
> **Drosera** is an **insectivorous plant** — it gets nutrition from **insects**. Its saucer-shaped leaves have **hair-like projections with sticky ends**; when an insect walks in, the **hairs bend inward** and trap it.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **STIMULUS** (plural **stimuli**) = any thing or event that **makes a living being respond** — a thorn, a hot cup, touch, light, darkness.
>
> **REPRODUCTION** = producing **new ones of one's own kind**. **Why necessary?** For the **continuity of life** — without it, a kind of living thing would die out. (A pencil or a bulb never has "young ones".)
>
> **DEATH** — when a living being can **no longer show these characteristics**, even though food, air and water are available.

> [!TIP]
> **Breathing counts:** count your breaths per minute after a **walk**, a **run** and **dancing** — more activity → **faster breathing** (the body needs more air).
>
> **White patches under the arms of summer shirts** are **salts left behind from sweat** — evidence of **excretion**.

> [!TIP]
> **Is a seed living?** **Yes** — it looks lifeless, but it's **alive and resting**. Given water and air, it **germinates, grows, respires** — things only living things do.
>
> **Charan: "A wooden log is non-living because it can't move." Charu: "It's living because it came from a tree."** → **Both reasons are wrong.** The log **was** part of a living tree, but now it **doesn't grow, respire or reproduce** — so it's **non-living (dead)**. Not moving alone doesn't decide it.

## What does a seed need to germinate?

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **GERMINATION** = a seed **sprouting** into a young plant.

**Activity 10.2:** four pots of garden soil, four **bean seeds** in each, kept for 15 days:

| Pot | Conditions | Air | Water | Light | Germinates? | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | sunlight, **no water** | ✓ | ✗ | ✓ | **No** | no water |
| **B** | sunlight, **excess water** (always above the soil) | **✗** | ✓ | ✓ | **No** | water fills the soil spaces — **no air** |
| **C** | moist soil, **dark** | ✓ | ✓ | ✗ | **Yes** | light not needed |
| **D** | moist soil, sunlight | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | **Yes** | all conditions right |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Bean seeds need the RIGHT AMOUNT of WATER and AIR to germinate — light is NOT needed.**
>
> **Why water?** It **softens the SEED COAT** (the outer covering) and lets the tiny **EMBRYO** inside start growing.
> **Why air?** Seeds use the air in the **spaces between soil particles**. Those spaces also let **roots grow** easily. **Too much water fills those spaces** — that's why Pot B fails.
> **Light?** Most seeds **don't need light to germinate** — but the **seedling needs sunlight afterwards** to keep growing.

> [!NOTE]
> **Exceptions:** **Coleus and Petunia** seeds need **light** to germinate (don't bury them). **Calendula and Zinnia** need **darkness** (cover them well with soil).

> [!TIP]
> **Storing grains and pulses:** keep them **dry** — no moisture means they **can't germinate** (or rot).
>
> **Test whether temperature matters:** same seeds, same water and air — keep one set **in a fridge**, one at **room temperature**, one somewhere **warm**. Change **only temperature**.

## Growth and movement in plants

**Activity 10.3:** germinate bean or gram seeds on moist cloth. Tie each seedling onto **wet blotting paper on a glass plate** standing in a beaker of water:
- **A** — upright, light from **all sides**
- **B** — **upside down** (root up, shoot down), light from all sides
- **C** — upright, inside a **box with a hole** — light from **one side only**

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Results:**
> 1. **Upright:** root grows **down**, shoot grows **up**.
> 2. **Upside down:** the root **bends and grows DOWN**; the shoot **bends and grows UP**.
> 3. **Light from one side:** the shoot **bends TOWARDS the light**; the root still grows **down**.
>
> **So: shoots grow upward and move towards sunlight; roots grow downward** — whichever way the plant is placed.

> [!TIP]
> **A potted plant lying on its side for a week?** The **shoot curves up** towards the light and the **root curves down** into the soil.

> [!NOTE]
> **Jagadish Chandra Bose (1858–1937)** built the **crescograph**, which recorded how plants respond to **light, heat, electricity and gravity** and measured **how fast plants grow**. He showed that **plants sense and respond to stimuli**.

## Life cycle of a plant

**Activity 10.4:** grow a bean plant for three months and record every change.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Bean plant life cycle:**
> **I. Seed → II. Germination → III. Leaves appear → IV. Flowers appear → V. Fruits (pods with seeds)** → the plant **yellows, dries and dies** — and its **seeds start the next generation**.
>
> **LIFE CYCLE = the whole journey from seed → plant → the next generation of seeds.**

## Life cycles of animals

### Mosquito

**Female mosquitoes** suck blood and spread **malaria, dengue and chikungunya**. They lay eggs in **stagnant water** — **desert coolers, flower pots, open containers**.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Four stages: EGG → LARVA → PUPA → ADULT.**
> - Eggs are laid **on or near water**.
> - **Larvae and pupae live in water** but keep **coming up to the surface to breathe air**.
> - The adult **rests on the water briefly**, then flies off; it lives about **10–15 days**.
> - Each stage **looks completely different** from the next.

> [!TIP]
> **Why don't we let water stand?** No stagnant water → **nowhere to lay eggs** → no larvae → fewer mosquitoes.
>
> **Why spray kerosene on stagnant water?** It forms a **thin layer** that **cuts the water off from the air**. Larvae and pupae **can't get air** — they die. **The life cycle is broken.**

> [!TIP]
> **Which comes first, larva or pupa?** Put a few **larvae** in one container and a few **pupae** in another (same water) and watch daily: the **larvae turn into pupae**, and **adults come out of the pupae first** → order is **larva → pupa → adult**.

> [!NOTE]
> **Silk moths** also go **egg → larva → pupa → adult**. Before becoming pupae, the larvae wrap themselves in **thread** they make — the **fibre for silk**. The **Khadi and Village Industries Commission (KVIC)** runs silk-production centres in India.

### Frog

In the rainy season, near a pond's edge, you may see a **white jelly-like mass** on the water or stuck to plants — a cluster of frog's eggs called **SPAWN**.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Frog life cycle:**
>
> | Stage | Name | Time |
> |---|---|---|
> | **I A** | **Spawn** (eggs) | Day 1 |
> | **I B** | **Embryo** | Day 3–4 |
> | **II A** | **Tadpole with a tail** (no legs) | Day 7–10 |
> | **II B** | **Tadpole with legs** (hind legs, still a tail) | 8–10 weeks |
> | **III** | **Froglet** — looks like a little frog, starts going on land | 12 weeks |
> | **IV** | **Adult frog** — tail gone, strong legs; lives **in water and on land** | 14 weeks |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Each stage's features suit its life.**
> - **Tadpole's tail** → helps it **swim** in water, where it lives.
> - **Froglet** starts spending time **on land**; the **tail is lost**.
> - **Adult's strong legs** → **jumping** on land and swimming.
>
> **The HABITAT changes too** — from water only to water **and** land.

> [!TIP]
> **Mosquito vs frog:**
> - **Alike:** both start as **eggs in water**; both have young stages that **look nothing like the adult**; both change **body shape** a lot.
> - **Different:** mosquito: egg → larva → pupa → adult (adult **flies**); frog: egg → tadpole → froglet → adult (adult lives on **land and in water**).
>
> **Plants vs animals:** both **grow, reproduce and die**; plants start from **seeds** and grow all their lives, animals start as **eggs/newborns** and grow to a fixed adult size.

## Think-it-through questions

1. **Grows? Respires? Examples:**
   - **No / No** → a **stone**.
   - **No / Yes** → **not possible** — anything that respires is living, and living things grow (or did).
   - **Yes / No** → **a crystal** "grows" by adding layers, but doesn't respire — it's **non-living**.
   - **Yes / Yes** → **any living thing** — a plant, a dog.
2. **Why does a tadpole have a tail?** → to **swim**, because it lives **only in water**.
3. **Tara and Vijay's experiment with a plant in a box with a hole** → testing whether the **shoot grows towards light**. They'll know they're right if the shoot **bends towards the hole**.

## In a nutshell

- Living beings **move, need food, grow, respire, excrete, respond to stimuli, reproduce and die**; non-living things don't.
- Seeds need the **right amount of water and air** to germinate; most don't need light.
- **Shoots grow up and towards light; roots grow down.**
- **Plant life cycle:** seed → germination → leaves → flowers → fruit/seeds → new plants.
- **Mosquito:** egg → larva → pupa → adult. **Frog:** spawn → embryo → tadpole → froglet → adult.

> [!WARNING]
> **Top traps:** movement alone ≠ living · plants respire and excrete too · seeds are living · too much water stops germination (no air) · bean seeds germinate in the dark · larva comes before pupa · spawn = frog's eggs.
