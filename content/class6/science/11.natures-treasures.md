> *"Earth provides enough to satisfy every man's need but not for every man's greed."* — M. K. Gandhi

## Holidays with Ajji

**Bhoomi and Surya** visit their **Ajji** (grandmother) in a village at the edge of a forest in the **Western Ghats**. The air is **fresh and cool**; there are hills, streams and many plants, animals and birds. Ajji says the place is full of **treasures of nature** — **pure air, fertile soil, sunlight, trees, water** — and **without them no life on Earth is possible**.

## 1. Air

**Activity 11.1:** breathe in deeply, **hold your breath** as long as is comfortable, breathe out slowly. You can't hold it for long — you start to feel uncomfortable.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Why?** The air we breathe in has **OXYGEN**, which the body needs to work. Hold your breath and the body **runs short of oxygen**.
> **We can live a few days without food or water — but not even a few minutes without oxygen.** Most living beings need oxygen.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Air is a MIXTURE of gases:**
>
> | Gas | Share |
> |---|---|
> | **Nitrogen** | **78%** |
> | **Oxygen** | **21%** |
> | **Argon, carbon dioxide and others** | **1%** |
>
> (**Percent (%)** = parts out of **100** — picture a 10 × 10 grid: 78 squares nitrogen, 21 oxygen, 1 the rest.)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **WIND = moving air** — a **storm** when fast, a **breeze** when gentle. We notice it when leaves rustle, clothes sway, pages flutter.
>
> **Wind can do work:** it turns a **firki (paper pinwheel)** — and a **WINDMILL** works the same way. Windmills can **grind flour**, **pull up water** from wells, and **generate electricity**.

> [!NOTE]
> **Windmill farms** — areas with many windmills making electricity: **Muppandal (Tamil Nadu)**, **Jaisalmer Wind Park (Rajasthan)**, **Brahmanvel (Maharashtra)**.

## 2. Water

We need water for **drinking, cooking, bathing, washing, cleaning**, **growing crops** and **industry**.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Water covers about TWO-THIRDS of the Earth's surface — but most is in OCEANS and SEAS, and it's SALINE (salty).** Salty water is **not fit** for home, farm or factory use.
>
> **FRESHWATER** is in **ice sheets and snow**, **rivers and lakes**, and **underground**. Ice and underground water are **hard to reach** — only a **very small fraction** (ponds, rivers, lakes, wells) is **easy to use**.
>
> **That's why water is precious** — and why Ajji waters plants so that **no drop goes to waste**.

> [!WARNING]
> **Freshwater is limited and is being POLLUTED** — **plastic bags, wrappers**, **waste from homes and industries** are dumped in rivers and lakes. Polluted water is **unfit** for living beings. Many places face **shortages**; some people **walk long distances** for drinking water — **not everyone has the same access**.

**Saving water (Activity 11.3):** turn off taps while **brushing** and soaping hands; use a **bucket** instead of a shower; reuse water from **washing vegetables** for **plants**; **fix leaking taps**; **recycle** water; **harvest rainwater**.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **RAINWATER HARVESTING** = **collecting and storing rainwater** for later use — on buildings, homes, schools, housing societies.
>
> It's an **age-old practice in India**: **stepwells** — **Bawadi** in **Rajasthan** (e.g. Toorji ka Jhalra, Jodhpur), **Vav** in **Gujarat** — built because water was **scarce**. They store **rainwater** and water **seeping in** from nearby lakes, ponds and rivers through **stone-lined walls**.

> [!NOTE]
> **World Water Day — 22 March** every year.

## 3. Energy from the Sun

Ajji and the children **dry chillies in the sun** (like drying raw mango slices) — so they can be used when fresh ones aren't available.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **The SUN is the MAIN SOURCE OF ENERGY on Earth.** It gives **heat and light** to all living beings, and **sunlight helps plants make food**.
>
> **Uses of heat and light:** plants make food, **drying clothes**, drying chillies/grains, **solar cookers**, **solar water heaters**, **solar panels** (electricity — on rooftops, street lights, traffic signals).

> [!TIP]
> **Does a grazing cow get its energy from the Sun?** **Yes — indirectly.** The cow eats **grass**; grass needs **sunlight** to make its food. So the energy came from the Sun **through the grass**. (Just **standing in the sun** isn't how it gets energy.)
>
> **Sun → plants make food → animals eat plants → we eat plants and animals.** Every food chain starts with the Sun.

## 4. Forests

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **FOREST = a large area with a dense growth of many types of plants** — herbs, shrubs and trees.
> - **Home, food and shelter** for wild animals, birds and insects.
> - **Products:** wood, fruits, honey, medicinal herbs, gum, bamboo, leaves, paper.
> - **Roots hold the soil**, stopping it being **washed away**.
> - **Fallen leaves decay** and **enrich the soil** with nutrients for new plants — **recycling in nature**.

> [!WARNING]
> **Forest cover is shrinking**, mainly from **large-scale cutting of trees**. **It takes many years to grow a new forest** — so we must use forests **responsibly** and let them **regenerate**.

> [!NOTE]
> - Ajji's village **doesn't pluck fruit** like **nellikai (amla)** from trees — they take only **fallen** fruit and leave the rest for **animals and birds**.
> - **Van Mahotsav** — a **week-long forest festival in July**; people **plant trees** and spread awareness.
> - **Chipko movement** — early **1970s, Uttarakhand**: local **women hugged trees** to stop them being cut.
> - **Sacred groves** (from *Diversity in the Living World*) — another old tradition of protecting forests.

## 5. Soil, rocks and minerals

Preparing the garden, the children **loosen the soil** — air spaces and room for roots — and find **pebbles, roots and earthworms**. **Earthworms** are nature's helpers that **turn and loosen the soil**.

**Activity 11.4:** collect soils from different places; compare **colour and texture**, by eye and with a **magnifying lens**. (Wash hands afterwards — soil with garbage may carry **germs**.)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Soil contains** sand, small stones, **insects, worms**, **tiny organisms** you can't see, and **decayed plants and animals**. Soils from different places have **different colours** because they contain **different materials**.
>
> **How is soil formed?** By the **breaking apart (disintegration) of ROCKS** by the **Sun, water and living things** over **thousands of years**. That's why soil is **precious** — it can't be quickly replaced.
>
> Different soils suit different uses — some for **growing certain plants**, some for **making bricks**.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **ROCKS** — used for **houses, temples, roads, dams, table tops**:
> - **slate** for **roofing**; **laterite** as **bricks**; also **granite, sandstone, marble**
> - **stone tools** — **hand axes and arrowheads** — for thousands of years
>
> **Rocks are made of MINERALS.** Metals like **aluminium, gold, copper, iron** are extracted from minerals. Minerals go into **planes, cars, jewellery, cosmetics, electronics** — a basic **mobile phone has about a dozen** (gold, silver, copper, cobalt…).
>
> **Rocks take thousands to millions of years to form** → use them responsibly.

## 6. Fossil fuels

**Activity 11.5 — vehicle survey:** most vehicles run on **petrol or diesel**; some on **CNG**; some are **electric**.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **FOSSIL FUELS = COAL, PETROLEUM and NATURAL GAS.**
> **Formed from the remains of microorganisms and plants buried deep in the Earth over MILLIONS of years.**
> **Petrol, diesel and kerosene** are obtained from **petroleum**.
>
> | Fuel | Main use |
> |---|---|
> | **Petrol** | two-wheelers, cars |
> | **Diesel** | trucks, buses |
> | **Natural gas / CNG** | cooking, electricity, vehicles — **cleaner** than petrol/diesel |
> | **Coal** | mainly **making electricity** |
> | **LPG** | cooking — replaced **wood, coal and dung cakes**, which polluted more |

> [!WARNING]
> **Two problems with fossil fuels:**
> 1. **LIMITED** — we'll **run out** if we keep using them like this.
> 2. **POLLUTION** — burning them gives **smoke and carbon dioxide**. Over-use for transport and home fuel causes **large-scale air pollution** (the children **smell smoke** back in the city).
>
> **Save them:** **walk or cycle** short distances, use **public transport**, **car-pool**, switch off engines at red lights, use **electric vehicles** (no smoke) and **solar** energy.

## Natural resources — renewable and non-renewable

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **NATURAL RESOURCES** = resources we get **from nature** — air, water, sunlight, forests, soil, rocks, minerals, fossil fuels.
> **HUMAN-MADE RESOURCES** = things **people make** from natural resources — **bulbs, furniture, solar panels, bicycles, machines**.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> | | **RENEWABLE** | **NON-RENEWABLE** |
> |---|---|---|
> | Meaning | **renewed / replenished** by nature **within a reasonable time** | **limited**; **not replenished** in a reasonable time — once used, **exhausted** |
> | Examples | **air, water, forests**, sunlight, wind | **coal, petroleum, natural gas, minerals, rocks, soil** |
>
> **Why are forests renewable?** Fallen fruit → animals and birds spread the **seeds** → **new trees** grow (it takes **years**, but nature does renew them).
> **Why is petroleum non-renewable?** It takes **millions of years** to form — far longer than we can wait.

> [!WARNING]
> **Soil is NON-renewable** in this chapter — it takes **thousands of years** to form from rocks. Don't put it with air and water just because it seems everywhere.

> [!TIP]
> **Renewable doesn't mean "use carelessly".** Water and forests renew only if we **don't pollute or over-use** them faster than nature can restore them.

## Think-it-through questions

1. **True or false?**
   - *Nature has all the resources to meet human needs.* → **True** (needs, not greed).
   - *Machines are a resource found in nature.* → **False** — machines are **human-made**.
   - *Natural gas is non-renewable.* → **True.**
   - *Air is renewable.* → **True.**
2. **Fuel in scooters and bikes** → **petrol**. **A renewable resource** → **water**.
3. **Classify:** coal, natural gas, minerals → **non-renewable**; forests → **renewable**.
4. **Why is it hard to regrow forests?** → a tree takes **many years** to grow, and a forest is a whole community of plants and animals built up over **decades**.
5. **Four things possible because of air:** **breathing**, **burning** (fire needs oxygen), **flying a kite / birds flying**, **drying clothes**, **windmills**.
6. **Food cooking in a solar cooker** → energy from the **Sun (solar energy)** — **renewable** and **pollution-free**.

## In a nutshell

- **Natural resources:** air, water, sunlight, forests, soil, rocks, minerals, fossil fuels; **human-made resources** are made from them.
- **Air:** nitrogen 78%, oxygen 21%, others 1%; **wind** runs windmills.
- **Water:** most is salty; **freshwater** is scarce — save it, don't pollute it, **harvest rainwater**.
- **Sun** = main source of energy; **forests** shelter life, hold and enrich soil.
- **Soil** forms from rocks over thousands of years; **rocks** are made of **minerals**.
- **Fossil fuels** (coal, petroleum, natural gas) — **limited** and **polluting**.
- **Renewable:** air, water, forests. **Non-renewable:** fossil fuels, minerals, rocks, soil.

> [!WARNING]
> **Top traps:** nitrogen (78%), not oxygen, is most of the air · most of Earth's water is salty · soil and minerals are non-renewable · machines are human-made · the cow gets the Sun's energy through grass, not by standing in sunshine · CNG is cleaner, but still a fossil fuel.
