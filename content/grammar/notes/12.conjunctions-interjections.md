# 12. Conjunctions & interjections

*Shows up in: class 5, 7, 9.*

## Conjunctions — joining words

- *I was hungry. I ate a sandwich.* → *I was hungry, **so** I ate a sandwich.*

A **conjunction** joins words, phrases or sentences — and the one you pick **shows the link** between the ideas.

### Coordinating — joins two equal parts: FANBOYS

| Word | Shows | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **F**or | reason (formal) | *She stayed home, **for** she was ill.* |
| **A**nd | adding | *He sings **and** dances.* |
| **N**or | adding a negative | *He doesn't smoke, **nor** does he drink.* |
| **B**ut | contrast | *She is poor **but** honest.* |
| **O**r | choice | *Tea **or** coffee?* |
| **Y**et | surprising contrast | *He is rich, **yet** unhappy.* |
| **S**o | result | *It rained, **so** we stayed in.* |

### Subordinating — attaches a clause that depends on the main one

| Shows | Words | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **reason** | because, since, as | *I stayed in **because** it was raining.* |
| **time** | when, while, before, after, until, as soon as | *Call me **when** you arrive.* |
| **condition** | if, unless, provided that | ***Unless** you hurry, you'll miss the bus.* |
| **contrast** | although, though, even though, whereas | ***Although** he was tired, he kept going.* |
| **purpose** | so that, in order that | *He ran fast **so that** he could catch the bus.* |
| **result** | so … that, such … that | *It was **so** hot **that** we stayed in.* |
| **manner** | as if, as though | *She talks **as if** she knew everything.* |
| **precaution** | in case, lest | *Take an umbrella **in case** it rains.* |
| **comparing two facts** | whereas, while | *Rina is tall, **whereas** her brother is short.* |

**Why it matters:** *because* gives the reason, *so* gives the result — same two facts, opposite direction.
*I was tired, **so** I slept* = *I slept **because** I was tired.* Pick by asking: which half is the cause?

### Correlative — come in pairs

**both…and · either…or · neither…nor · not only…but also · whether…or · no sooner…than · hardly…when**

- Put each half **just before the matching words**: *She is **both** clever **and** hard-working* (two adjectives).
- *Not only…but also* — ✗ *He not only plays the piano but also the guitar* → ✓ *He plays **not only** the piano **but also** the guitar.*

**Why:** the pair is a balance — the words after each half must be the same kind, so the sentence stays even.

> [!WARNING]
> - **unless = if not** — never add another *not*: *Unless you work hard, you will fail* (not *unless you don't work*).
> - **although / though** don't take *but*: *Although it rained, we played* (not *although…but*).
> - **No sooner** goes with **than**; **Hardly / Scarcely** go with **when**.
> - **in spite of / despite** are prepositions, not conjunctions — they take a noun or *-ing*, not a clause:
>   *In spite of **working** hard, he failed* (not *in spite of he worked*). Never *despite of*.
> - *since* and *as* can show **time** (*since 2010, as I was leaving*) or **reason** (*since you're busy* = because).
> - **lest** = "so that … not", and is followed by **should**: *Walk carefully **lest** you **should** fall.*

## Interjections — feeling words

An **interjection** is a sudden word or sound showing **feeling**. It is not grammatically joined
to the sentence and is usually followed by **!**

| Feeling | Interjections |
|---|---|
| joy | Hurray! Wow! Yippee! |
| sorrow | Alas! Oh no! |
| surprise | Oh! What! Really! Wow! |
| pain | Ouch! Ow! |
| approval | Bravo! Well done! |
| greeting / calling | Hello! Hey! |
| disgust | Ugh! Yuck! Fie! |

- *Alas! The old man is dead.* · *Hurray! We won the match.* · *Bravo! Well played.*

**Why the comma or exclamation:** the interjection sits *outside* the sentence's grammar, so
punctuation separates it — *Oh, I forgot!* or *Oh! I forgot.*
