# 5. Articles & determiners

*Shows up in: class 5 (articles), 8–9 (determiners); a fixed part of the class 9–10 exam.*

A **determiner** comes **before a noun** and tells *which* or *how much* of it we mean.
*a pen, the pen, this pen, my pen, some pens, every pen.* The commonest ones are the
**articles**: **a, an, the**.

## a / an — "one, any one"

- **a** before a consonant **sound**: *a book, a university, a one-rupee coin, a European.*
- **an** before a vowel **sound**: *an apple, an hour, an honest man, an MLA, an X-ray.*

**Why it's about sound, not spelling:** *an* exists only to stop two vowel sounds bumping
together (*a apple* is awkward to say). *university* starts with a "yoo" sound (a consonant
sound), so it's *a university*; *hour* starts with a silent h — the first sound is "ow" — so *an hour*.

> [!TIP]
> Say it out loud. *MLA* is spoken "em-el-ay" → starts with "e" → **an** MLA. *UFO* is "yoo-ef-oh" → **a** UFO.

## the — "that particular one"

Use **the** when the listener knows **which one** you mean:
- already mentioned: *I saw **a** dog. **The** dog was barking.*
- only one exists: ***the** sun, **the** sky, **the** earth*
- superlatives & ordinals: ***the** best, **the** first, **the** last*
- rivers, seas, oceans, mountain ranges, groups of islands, deserts: ***the** Ganga, **the** Indian Ocean, **the** Himalayas, **the** Andamans, **the** Thar*
- famous books, newspapers, trains: ***the** Ramayana, **the** Hindu, **the** Rajdhani Express*
- a whole class of things (formal): ***The** cow is a useful animal.*
- musical instruments: *She plays **the** sitar.*
- **the + adjective** = all people of that kind: ***the** rich, **the** poor, **the** old* (so it takes a plural verb: *the poor **need** help*)
- a famous name used for "the one like that": *Rohan is **the** Einstein of our class.*

**Why it works:** *a* introduces something new; *the* points back at something both of you
can already identify. That is why the second mention switches from *a* to *the*.

## No article

- Names of people, cities, countries, single mountains, lakes: *Riya, Delhi, India, Mount Everest, Dal Lake.*
  (But *the USA, the UK* — because *States* and *Kingdom* are common nouns.)
- Languages, subjects, meals, games: *She speaks **Hindi**. We play **cricket**. **Lunch** is ready.*
- Uncountable / plural nouns in general: ***Honesty** is the best policy. **Dogs** bark.*
- Places used for their main purpose: *go to **school**, go to **bed**, go to **hospital*** (as a patient).
  *My father went to **the school** to meet my teacher* — he isn't a student, so it's just a building.

## Other determiners

| Group | Words | Use with |
|---|---|---|
| Demonstratives | this · that (singular) · these · those (plural) | near/far |
| Possessives | my · your · his · her · its · our · their | any noun |
| Quantifiers | some · any | *some* in positive, *any* in negatives & questions |
| | much · little · a little | uncountable |
| | many · few · a few · several | countable plural |
| | each · every · either · neither | singular countable |
| | all · both · enough · no | |

**Why some/any:** *some* assumes a thing exists (*I have some money*); *any* leaves it open (*Do you have any money? I don't have any*).
Offers expect "yes", so they use *some*: *Would you like **some** tea?*

> [!WARNING]
> **each / every** + singular noun + singular verb: *Every student **has** a book.*
> **both** = two together (plural); **either** = one of two; **neither** = not one and not the other.
