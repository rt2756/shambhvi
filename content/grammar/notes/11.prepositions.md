# 11. Prepositions

*Shows up in: class 5, 7, 8; tested in class 9–10 gap-filling.*

- *The cat is **on** the box. · **in** the box. · **under** the box. · **behind** the box.*

A **preposition** shows how a noun/pronoun is **related** to the rest of the sentence —
**where, when, how, by what**. *pre-position* = it is "placed before" a noun or pronoun.

**Why the object form follows:** a preposition always has an object — *with **him**, for **me***, never *with he*.

## Time: at / on / in

| | Use for | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **at** | a **point** — clock time, exact moment | at 6 o'clock, at noon, at night, at sunrise |
| **on** | a **day / date** | on Monday, on 15 August, on my birthday |
| **in** | a **longer period** | in May, in 2024, in summer, in the morning |

**Why:** think of size — **at** is a dot, **on** is a surface (one day), **in** is a container (a month, a year).

- **since** (point) / **for** (length): *since Monday, for three days* (see lesson 7).
- **by** = not later than: *Finish it **by** Friday.* · **until/till** = up to that time: *Wait **till** five.*
- **within** = before the end of: *Reply **within** a week.*

## Place: at / on / in

- **at** a point/place as a spot: *at the door, at the bus stop, at school.*
- **on** a surface or line: *on the table, on the wall, on the road.*
- **in** inside an area/container: *in the box, in the room, in Delhi, in India.*

## Movement and position pairs

| Pair | Difference | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **in / into** | *in* = position; *into* = movement inside | *He is **in** the room.* · *He walked **into** the room.* |
| **on / onto** | position / movement onto a surface | *The cat jumped **onto** the table.* |
| **between / among** | two / more than two | *between you and me* · *among the students* |
| **beside / besides** | next to / in addition to | *Sit **beside** me.* · ***Besides** maths, she likes art.* |
| **above / over** | higher than / higher and covering or across | *The plane flew **over** the city.* |
| **below / under** | lower than / directly beneath, covered | *The cat is **under** the bed.* |
| **across / through** | from one side to the other of a surface / inside a 3-D space | *across the road* · *through the tunnel* |

## by / with

- **by** the doer: *The book was written **by** Tagore.* · **with** the tool: *He cut it **with** a knife.*

## Fixed pairs (learn as one unit)

| Word + preposition | Example |
|---|---|
| afraid **of**, fond **of**, proud **of**, full **of**, aware **of** | *She is fond **of** music.* |
| good **at**, angry **with** (a person), angry **at/about** (a thing) | *good **at** maths* |
| interested **in**, believe **in**, succeed **in** | *interested **in** science* |
| depend **on**, insist **on**, congratulate **on** | *It depends **on** you.* |
| different **from**, suffer **from**, prevent **from** | *different **from** mine* |
| listen **to**, reply **to**, similar **to**, married **to** | *Listen **to** me.* |
| wait **for**, sorry **for**, famous **for** | *famous **for** its tea* |
| agree **with** (a person), agree **to** (a plan) | *I agree **with** you.* |
| die **of** (a disease), die **from** (a wound/cause) | *died **of** malaria* |
| prefer A **to** B | *I prefer tea **to** coffee.* |

> [!TIP]
> After a preposition a verb takes **-ing**: *insist on **paying**, good at **drawing**, prevent from **going***.
> **Why:** a preposition needs a noun-like object, and *-ing* turns a verb into a noun (a gerund — lesson 16).

> [!WARNING]
> **No preposition** after: *discuss, enter, reach, marry, resemble, attack, answer*.
> *We discussed **the problem*** (not *discussed about*). *He reached **home*** (not *reached to home*).
> **Why:** these verbs already contain the "about/into/to" idea inside them, so adding one repeats it.
