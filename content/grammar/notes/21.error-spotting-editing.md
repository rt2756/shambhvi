# 21. Error spotting & editing

*Shows up in: class 9–10 exam (editing, omission, gap-filling) — this lesson uses everything before it.*

In the exam you get a short passage with **one error per line** (editing) or **one missing
word per line** (omission). You don't need new rules — you need a **checklist** so you don't miss any.

## The 7-point checklist (check in this order)

| # | Check | Typical error | Fix | Lesson |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Subject–verb agreement** | *The list of names **are** long.* | is | 9 |
| 2 | **Tense** — does it match the time words and the rest of the passage? | *Yesterday he **go** to school.* | went | 7 |
| 3 | **Articles / determiners** | *He is **a** honest man.* | an | 5 |
| 4 | **Prepositions** | *She is good **in** maths.* | at | 11 |
| 5 | **Pronouns** — right form, right number | *Each boy brought **their** book.* | his | 3 |
| 6 | **Verb form** after modals, did, to, prepositions, has/have | *He can **sings**. I have **went**.* | sing · gone | 6, 8, 16 |
| 7 | **Adjective / adverb / word form** | *She sings **good**. He is **more taller**.* | well · taller | 4, 10, 19 |

**Why this order:** agreement and tense errors are the most common in CBSE papers, and both can
be spotted by looking at just the **subject** and the **verb** — so it's the fastest first pass.

## How to read a line

1. Find the **verb** first. Ask: *who is doing it?* (agreement) and *when?* (tense).
2. Look at every **small word** — *a, an, the, in, on, at, of, to, for* — these hide most errors.
3. Read the line **aloud in your head** — wrong grammar usually *sounds* wrong.
4. In **omission**, the missing word is almost always a **small word**: an article, preposition, helping verb, or *to*.

**Answer format (most boards ask for):** *incorrect word → correction*, e.g. *go → went*. For omission: *word before + missing word + word after*.

## Worked example — editing

> *Last Sunday we goes to the zoo. (1)*
> *We saw an elephant who was eating bananas. (2)*
> *The monkeys was jumping from tree to tree. (3)*

1. *Last Sunday* → past, *we* → plural: **goes → went**.
2. An animal → *which*, not *who*: **who → which** (or *that*).
3. *monkeys* is plural: **was → were**.

## Worked example — omission

> *My mother wakes up ___ six every morning. (at)*
> *She likes ___ drink tea first. (to)*
> *Then she goes ___ the market. (to)*

**Why small words:** the passage still "half makes sense" without them — that's why they're easy to miss
when reading quickly, and why the exam hides the blanks there.

## Gap-filling with tenses (class 10 style)

Read the **whole passage first** to decide the main tense (a story → past; a report of now → present),
then fill each gap — looking at time words (*yesterday, since, now, by*) and at the verbs on both sides.
