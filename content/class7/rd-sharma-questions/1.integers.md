# 1. Integers

<!-- Original RD Sharma-style practice. Auto-numbered on the page. -->

Find: **(−18) + 7 + (−9) + 25**.

> [!ANSWER]
> Add the negatives: −18 − 9 = −27. Add the positives: 7 + 25 = 32.
>
> 32 − 27 = **5**. Grouping same signs first is allowed because addition is commutative and associative.

---

Subtract **−35** from **−12**.

> [!ANSWER]
> "Subtract a from b" means **b − a**: −12 − (−35) = −12 + 35 = **23**.
>
> Taking away a negative is the same as adding its positive.

---

Find: **(−4) × (−5) × (−3) × 2**.

> [!ANSWER]
> Size: 4 × 5 × 3 × 2 = 120. Signs: three minus signs → odd → negative.
>
> **−120**.

---

Find: **(−144) ÷ 12** and **(−144) ÷ (−12)**.

> [!ANSWER]
> **−12** and **12**. Division follows the same sign rule as multiplication: different signs → −, same signs → +.

---

Use the distributive law to find **(−37) × 98**.

> [!ANSWER]
> (−37) × (100 − 2) = −3700 + 74 = **−3626**.
>
> Splitting 98 into 100 − 2 makes the multiplication easy.

---

Find **26 × (−48) + (−48) × (−36)**.

> [!ANSWER]
> −48 is a common factor: (−48) × (26 + (−36)) = (−48) × (−10) = **480**.

---

Which integer is the **additive inverse** of −29? What is the **multiplicative identity** for integers?

> [!ANSWER]
> Additive inverse of −29 is **29** (they add to 0). Multiplicative identity is **1** (a × 1 = a).

---

Is **(−8) − (−3) = (−3) − (−8)**? What does this show?

> [!ANSWER]
> Left: −8 + 3 = −5. Right: −3 + 8 = 5. Not equal.
>
> So **subtraction is not commutative** — order matters.

---

The product of two integers is **−180**. One of them is **15**. Find the other.

> [!ANSWER]
> −180 ÷ 15 = **−12**. Division undoes multiplication; different signs give a negative answer.

---

In a test, +4 marks for a right answer and −1 for a wrong one. Riya answered 30 questions and got 22 right. Her score?

> [!ANSWER]
> Right: 22 × 4 = 88. Wrong: 8 × (−1) = −8.
>
> 88 − 8 = **80**.

---

A lift is at 15 m below ground. It goes up 4 m every second. Where is it after 7 seconds?

> [!ANSWER]
> −15 + 4 × 7 = −15 + 28 = **13 m above ground**.

---

Find the value of **(−1)⁵⁰ + (−1)⁵¹**.

> [!ANSWER]
> (−1) to an **even** power = 1, to an **odd** power = −1.
>
> 1 + (−1) = **0**.
