# 10. Life processes in plants

<!-- NCERT Class 7 "Curiosity" Chapter 10 — Life Processes in Plants. -->

## Plants don't eat — they make their food

Animals get food from plants (directly, or by eating animals that ate plants). Plants **make their own**, using sunlight.

**Three-pot experiment** (same soil, same saplings):

| Pot | Sunlight | Water | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | ✅ | ✅ | grows best |
| **B** | ✅ | ✗ | may **die** |
| **C** | ✗ | ✅ | poor growth |

→ Plants need **both sunlight and water** to grow. Changing one thing per pot is what makes the conclusion trustworthy.

## The iodine test for starch

Plants store food as **starch**, made in the leaves.

**Method:** boil the leaf 5 min to soften → dip in **alcohol** (in a test tube standing in boiling water) until **colourless** → add dilute **iodine** → **blue-black means starch**.

- **Why decolourise first:** the leaf's own green would hide the colour change. Removing it makes the blue-black easy to see.

> [!WARNING]
> **Alcohol is highly flammable** — never heat it directly over a flame. That's why the test tube stands in a beaker of hot water.

## What photosynthesis needs — one experiment per ingredient

Each test removes **one** ingredient and looks for starch:

| Test | Set-up | Result | Conclusion |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Chlorophyll** | leaf with green + non-green patches, plant in sunlight | only **green** patches turn blue-black | **chlorophyll needed** |
| **Sunlight** | same leaf, plant kept in dark 36 h | **no** blue-black anywhere | **sunlight needed** |
| **Carbon dioxide** | half a destarched leaf sealed in a bottle with **caustic soda** (absorbs CO₂) | only the half **outside** turns blue-black | **carbon dioxide needed** |
| **Oxygen released** | water plant + funnel + inverted test tube in sunlight | gas collects; a lit matchstick **flares up** | **oxygen is released** |

- Note the plant is **destarched** (kept in the dark 2–3 days) before the CO₂ test — otherwise old stored starch would give a false positive.
- The non-green patches lack enough chlorophyll to make detectable starch.

## Photosynthesis — the summary

> **carbon dioxide + water → (sunlight, chlorophyll) → glucose + oxygen**

- Food is made as **glucose**, a simple carbohydrate — an instant energy source, later converted to **starch** for storage.
- **Leaves are the "food factories"** — broad and flat to catch light, green from **chlorophyll**, the pigment that captures sunlight.
- Other **green parts** of a plant photosynthesise too, since they also have chlorophyll.

> [!NOTE]
> Some leaves look red, violet or brown because other pigments **hide** the chlorophyll. Many still photosynthesise — the iodine test proves it.

> [!NOTE]
> **Rustom Hormusji Dastur (1896–1961)**, head of Botany at the Royal Institute of Science, Bombay, studied how **water, temperature and the colour of light** affect photosynthesis.

## Stomata — the gas doors

- **Tiny pores on the surface of leaves** (mostly the lower surface), seen by peeling a thin layer and viewing it under a microscope.
- They allow the **exchange of gases** — carbon dioxide in and oxygen out during photosynthesis, and the reverse during respiration.

## Transport in plants

Two separate pipe systems, running in opposite directions:

| Tissue | Carries | From → To |
|---|---|---|
| **Xylem** | **water and minerals** | roots → up to leaves and all parts |
| **Phloem** | **food** (made in leaves) | leaves → all parts, including seeds and roots for storage |

**The red-ink experiment:** a twig with white flowers stood in **red-inked water** shows red in the **stem, leaves and flowers** by the next day. Cut the stem above the water line and the red is visible **inside** — proof that water climbs through the **xylem**, and that dissolved minerals ride up the same way.

> [!TIP]
> **X**ylem carries **water** up from the roots · **Ph**loem carries **food** (**ph** for *phood*) from the leaves.

## Plants respire too

**glucose + oxygen → carbon dioxide + water + energy**

- **The germinating-seed experiment:** soaked moong seeds in a sealed flask, left in the **dark** for 24 hours. Air from the flask turns **lime water milky** → the seeds produced **carbon dioxide** → they were respiring.
- Kept in the **dark** deliberately, so photosynthesis can't happen and only respiration is being tested.
- **All parts of a plant — green or non-green — respire**, and the energy released is used for growth and development.

> [!WARNING]
> **Photosynthesis and respiration are opposites, and are not the same thing.** Photosynthesis: uses CO₂ + water, **makes** glucose, releases oxygen, needs **sunlight and chlorophyll**, happens only in green parts. Respiration: uses glucose + **oxygen**, **releases** energy and CO₂, happens **all the time in every part** of the plant.
