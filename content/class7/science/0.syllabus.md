The whole year at a glance — NCERT's **Curiosity** (Class 7). **Every chapter has full notes and a matching practice set** on the Science Questions page.

1. **The Ever-Evolving World of Science** — how all the other chapters connect, and science as a way of thinking. *(Non-evaluative intro chapter — no notes needed.)*
2. ✅ **Exploring Substances: Acidic, Basic, and Neutral** — indicators, the three families of substances, and neutralisation in daily life.
3. ✅ **Electricity: Circuits and their Components** — cells, batteries, lamps, switches, circuit diagrams, conductors and insulators.
4. ✅ **The World of Metals and Non-metals** — the five properties that spot a metal, rusting, and why metal oxides are basic.
5. ✅ **Changes Around Us: Physical and Chemical** — the new-substance test, combustion and the fire triangle, weathering and erosion.
6. ✅ **Adolescence: A Stage of Growth and Change** — puberty, the changes it brings, and staying healthy through them.
7. ✅ **Heat Transfer in Nature** — conduction, convection and radiation; land & sea breeze; the water cycle.
8. ✅ **Measurement of Time and Motion** — clocks and the pendulum, the SI second, speed, uniform vs non-uniform motion.
9. ✅ **Life Processes in Animals** — digestion through the alimentary canal, breathing and respiration, circulation.
10. ✅ **Life Processes in Plants** — photosynthesis and the experiments proving it, stomata, xylem & phloem, respiration.
11. ✅ **Light: Shadows and Reflections** — light travels straight, shadows, mirrors, and the pinhole camera.
12. ✅ **Earth, Moon, and the Sun** — rotation & revolution, why seasons happen, and eclipses.
