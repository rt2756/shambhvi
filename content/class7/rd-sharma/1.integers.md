# 1. Integers

## What an integer is

- **Integers** = the whole numbers and their negatives: …, −3, −2, −1, 0, 1, 2, 3, …
- **Positive** integers sit right of 0, **negative** integers sit left of 0. **0 is neither.**
- On the number line, **further right = bigger**. So −2 > −7, because −2 is to the right of −7.

**Why it works:** a negative number is an amount *below* zero — like 7 °C below freezing. Being 2 below is warmer (bigger) than being 7 below.

> [!WARNING]
> −7 is **smaller** than −2, even though 7 is bigger than 2. Think "owing ₹7 is worse than owing ₹2".

## Adding and subtracting

**Example:** −8 + 3. Start at −8, move 3 steps right → land on **−5**.

| Signs | What to do | Example |
|---|---|---|
| both + | add, answer + | 4 + 6 = 10 |
| both − | add, answer − | −4 − 6 = −10 |
| one + one − | subtract the smaller size from the bigger, keep the sign of the bigger | −9 + 4 = −5 · 9 − 4 = 5 |

- **Subtracting a negative = adding:** 6 − (−2) = 6 + 2 = 8.

**Why it works:** subtracting means "take away". Taking away a debt of ₹2 leaves you ₹2 better off — the same as being given ₹2.

## Multiplying and dividing

**Example:** (−3) × 4 = −12 · (−3) × (−4) = 12.

| Signs | Answer |
|---|---|
| + × + or − × − | **+** |
| + × − or − × + | **−** |

Division follows the **same** sign rule: −20 ÷ 4 = −5 · −20 ÷ −4 = 5.

**Why it works:** look at the pattern 3 × (−4) = −12, 2 × (−4) = −8, 1 × (−4) = −4, 0 × (−4) = 0. Each step the answer goes **up by 4**. Carry on: (−1) × (−4) = 4, (−2) × (−4) = 8. So negative × negative is positive. Division undoes multiplication, so it must obey the same signs.

- **Counting rule:** an **even** number of minus signs → +, an **odd** number → −. So (−1) × (−1) × (−1) = −1.

> [!WARNING]
> "Minus minus is plus" is for **multiplying**, not adding: −4 − 6 is **−10**, not +10.

## Properties (the names RD Sharma asks for)

| Property | Addition | Multiplication |
|---|---|---|
| **Closure** — answer is still an integer | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Commutative** — order doesn't matter: a + b = b + a | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Associative** — grouping doesn't matter: (a + b) + c = a + (b + c) | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Identity** — the "do nothing" number | 0 (a + 0 = a) | 1 (a × 1 = a) |
| **Inverse** | a + (−a) = 0 | — (½ is not an integer) |

- **Subtraction and division are not commutative:** 5 − 3 ≠ 3 − 5, and 6 ÷ 2 ≠ 2 ÷ 6.
- **Division is not closed:** 7 ÷ 2 = 3.5, not an integer.
- **Distributive:** a × (b + c) = a × b + a × c. Handy for mental maths: 15 × 98 = 15 × (100 − 2) = 1500 − 30 = **1470**.
- **Dividing by 0 is not allowed.** 0 ÷ 5 = 0 is fine.

**Why distributive works:** 3 × (4 + 2) means three bags of (4 apples and 2 apples). That is 3 × 4 apples plus 3 × 2 apples.

**Why no ÷ 0:** 12 ÷ 0 = ? would need ? × 0 = 12, but anything × 0 is 0. No number works.

## Word problems

**Example:** A submarine is at −120 m. It rises 45 m, then dives 30 m. Where is it? −120 + 45 − 30 = **−105 m** (105 m below sea level).

> [!TIP]
> Pick a direction as + (up, profit, above zero, to the right), and the opposite as −. Then just add.

## Quick practice

1. Arrange in ascending order: 3, −5, 0, −1, 4.
2. −15 + 7 − (−4) = ?
3. (−6) × (−5) × (−2) = ?
4. −72 ÷ 8 = ?
5. Use the distributive law: −23 × 101.
6. Which property is (−3) + 5 = 5 + (−3)?
7. Temperature is 5 °C at noon and falls 3 °C every hour. What is it after 4 hours?

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. −5, −1, 0, 3, 4
2. −15 + 7 + 4 = **−4**
3. 30 × (−2) = **−60** (three minus signs → odd → −)
4. **−9**
5. −23 × (100 + 1) = −2300 − 23 = **−2323**
6. **Commutative** property of addition
7. 5 − 3 × 4 = 5 − 12 = **−7 °C**

</details>
