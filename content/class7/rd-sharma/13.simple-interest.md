# 13. Simple Interest

## The words

**Example:** you lend a friend ₹1000 for 2 years at 5% a year. Each year they pay you 5% of 1000 = ₹50 extra. After 2 years: **₹100 interest**, and they return ₹1100.

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Principal (P)** | the money borrowed or deposited |
| **Rate (R)** | interest % **per year** (per annum, p.a.) |
| **Time (T)** | in **years** |
| **Simple interest (SI)** | the extra money paid for using P |
| **Amount (A)** | P + SI, the total paid back |

## The formula

**SI = P × R × T ÷ 100** · **A = P + SI**

**Why it works:** one year's interest is R% of P = P × R/100. "Simple" means it's the **same every year** (always on the original P), so for T years multiply by T.

**Example:** P = ₹4500, R = 8%, T = 3 years. SI = 4500 × 8 × 3 ÷ 100 = **₹1080**. A = **₹5580**.

## Time not in whole years

- Months → divide by 12: 9 months = 9/12 = ¾ year.
- Days → divide by 365: 73 days = 73/365 = ⅕ year.
- **Example:** ₹2400 at 10% for 6 months: SI = 2400 × 10 × ½ ÷ 100 = **₹120**.

> [!WARNING]
> R is per **year**, so T must be in **years**. Putting T = 6 for 6 months makes the answer 12 times too big.

## Finding P, R or T

Turn the formula around (it's an equation — Chapter 8):

- **P = SI × 100 ÷ (R × T)**
- **R = SI × 100 ÷ (P × T)**
- **T = SI × 100 ÷ (P × R)**

**Example:** at what rate does ₹5000 earn ₹1200 in 4 years? R = 1200 × 100 ÷ (5000 × 4) = **6%**.

**Example:** in how many years will ₹800 become ₹1000 at 5%? SI = 200. T = 200 × 100 ÷ (800 × 5) = **5 years**.

> [!TIP]
> If the question gives the **amount**, first find SI = A − P.

## "Doubles itself" questions

- Money doubles means SI = P. Then R × T = 100.
- At 10% a year, money doubles in 100 ÷ 10 = **10 years**.

**Why R × T = 100:** P = P × R × T ÷ 100. Divide both sides by P: 1 = R × T ÷ 100.

## Quick practice

1. SI on ₹6000 at 7% for 3 years?
2. Amount on ₹1500 at 12% for 2 ½ years?
3. SI on ₹3650 at 10% for 146 days?
4. ₹750 earns ₹180 in 4 years. Rate?
5. What sum earns ₹360 at 6% in 5 years?
6. In how many years will ₹2000 amount to ₹2600 at 5%?
7. At what rate will money double in 8 years?

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. 6000 × 7 × 3 ÷ 100 = **₹1260**
2. SI = 1500 × 12 × 5/2 ÷ 100 = 450 → **₹1950**
3. 146/365 = 2/5 → 3650 × 10 × 2/5 ÷ 100 = **₹146**
4. 180 × 100 ÷ 3000 = **6%**
5. 360 × 100 ÷ 30 = **₹1200**
6. SI 600 → 600 × 100 ÷ 10 000 = **6 years**
7. 100 ÷ 8 = **12.5%**

</details>
