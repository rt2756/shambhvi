# 20. Mensuration I — Perimeter and Area of Rectilinear Figures

## Perimeter vs area

- **Perimeter** = distance **around** the edge (a length: cm, m).
- **Area** = space **inside** (square units: cm², m²).

**Why square units:** area counts how many 1 cm × 1 cm squares fit inside.

## Rectangle and square

| Shape | Perimeter | Area |
|---|---|---|
| Rectangle (l, b) | 2(l + b) | l × b |
| Square (side a) | 4a | a² |

**Why l × b:** a 5 cm × 3 cm rectangle holds 3 rows of 5 unit squares = 15 cm².

**Example:** a park 40 m × 25 m. Fencing (perimeter) = 2 × 65 = **130 m**. Grass (area) = **1000 m²**.

- **Diagonal of a square** with side a = a√2; area = d²/2.

## Paths and borders

**Example:** a garden 30 m × 20 m has a 2 m path **outside** it. Outer rectangle = 34 × 24 = 816 m². Path = 816 − 600 = **216 m²**.

- **Rule:** area of path = outer area − inner area.
- Path **outside** → add 2 × width to each side. Path **inside** → subtract 2 × width.
- **Cross roads** through the middle: add both roads, then subtract the square where they overlap (it was counted twice).

**Why add 2 × width:** the path sticks out on **both** ends — left and right, top and bottom.

## Parallelogram

- **Area = base × height** (height is perpendicular to the base, not the slanted side).

**Why it works:** cut the triangle off one end and slide it to the other — it becomes a rectangle with the same base and height.

## Triangle

- **Area = ½ × base × height**.

**Why it works:** two copies of the same triangle fit together into a parallelogram. So a triangle is half of base × height.

- **Right triangle:** the two shorter sides are base and height: ½ × 6 × 8 = 24.

> [!WARNING]
> Height must be **perpendicular** to the base. Using a slanted side as the height is the most common mistake.

## Rhombus

- **Area = ½ × d₁ × d₂** (product of diagonals). Or base × height (it's a parallelogram).

**Why ½ d₁ d₂:** the diagonals cross at right angles and split it into 4 right triangles. Together they make half of the rectangle d₁ × d₂ drawn around the rhombus.

## Converting units

- 1 m = 100 cm → **1 m² = 100 × 100 = 10 000 cm²**. 1 hectare = 10 000 m².

**Why 10 000, not 100:** a 1 m square is 100 cm along **each** side, so it holds 100 rows of 100 unit squares.
