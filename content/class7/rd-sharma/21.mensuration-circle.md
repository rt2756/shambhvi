# 21. Mensuration II — Area of a Circle

## Parts and π

- **Radius (r):** centre to edge · **Diameter (d):** edge to edge through the centre, **d = 2r**.
- **Circumference:** the distance around the circle.
- **π (pi):** circumference ÷ diameter is the **same for every circle**, about **22/7 or 3.14**.

**Why π is a constant:** every circle is a scaled copy of every other. Double the diameter and the distance around doubles too, so the ratio never changes.

## Circumference

**C = πd = 2πr**

**Example:** r = 7 cm → C = 2 × 22/7 × 7 = **44 cm**.

**Why:** this is just the definition of π turned around: C ÷ d = π, so C = π × d.

## Area

**A = πr²**

**Example:** r = 14 cm → A = 22/7 × 14 × 14 = **616 cm²**.

**Why it works:** cut the circle into many thin pizza slices and lay them alternately point-up and point-down. They form a shape close to a rectangle. Its length is half the circumference (πr) and its height is r. Area = πr × r = πr².

> [!WARNING]
> Square the **radius**, not the diameter. If given d = 14, use r = 7.
> Circumference is a length (cm); area is in square units (cm²).

## Rings and parts of circles

- **Ring (annulus):** area = πR² − πr² = π(R² − r²) — big circle minus the hole.
- **Semicircle:** area = ½πr²; perimeter = πr + 2r (the curve **plus** the straight diameter).
- **Quadrant:** ¼πr².

## Wheels and distance

- One full turn of a wheel moves it forward by **one circumference**.
- Turns = distance ÷ circumference.

**Example:** a wheel of radius 35 cm. C = 2 × 22/7 × 35 = 220 cm. To cover 1.1 km = 110 000 cm, it turns 110 000 ÷ 220 = **500 times**.

## Quick practice

1. Circumference of a circle of diameter 21 cm?
2. Area of a circle of radius 3.5 cm?
3. Circumference is 88 cm. Find the radius and the area.
4. Area is 154 cm². Find the radius.
5. Ring: outer radius 10 cm, inner 4 cm. Area (use 3.14)?
6. Perimeter of a semicircle of radius 14 cm?
7. A wheel of diameter 70 cm makes 400 turns. Distance covered?

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. 22/7 × 21 = **66 cm**
2. 22/7 × 3.5 × 3.5 = **38.5 cm²**
3. 2πr = 88 → **r = 14 cm**, A = **616 cm²**
4. r² = 154 × 7/22 = 49 → **r = 7 cm**
5. 3.14 × (100 − 16) = **263.76 cm²**
6. 22/7 × 14 + 28 = 44 + 28 = **72 cm**
7. C = 220 cm → 220 × 400 = 88 000 cm = **880 m**
