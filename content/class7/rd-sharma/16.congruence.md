# 16. Congruence

## What congruent means

**Example:** two stamps from the same sheet — place one on the other and they match exactly.

- Two shapes are **congruent (≅)** if one fits exactly on the other: same shape **and** same size.
- Segments are congruent if they have the same length; angles if they have the same measure.

## Congruent triangles and matching parts

- △ABC ≅ △PQR means A ↔ P, B ↔ Q, C ↔ R. So AB = PQ, BC = QR, CA = RP and ∠A = ∠P, ∠B = ∠Q, ∠C = ∠R.
- **CPCT** = Corresponding Parts of Congruent Triangles are equal. Once you prove two triangles congruent, every matching side and angle is equal.

> [!WARNING]
> The **order of letters** matters. △ABC ≅ △PQR is not the same claim as △ABC ≅ △QPR.

## The four tests

You don't have to check all 6 parts. Any one of these is enough:

| Test | You need | Why it's enough |
|---|---|---|
| **SSS** | all 3 sides equal | three fixed sticks can only be joined into one triangle |
| **SAS** | 2 sides and the angle **between** them | fixing two arms and the gap between them fixes the third side |
| **ASA** | 2 angles and the side **between** them | the two angles fix the directions from each end; the lines meet at one point |
| **RHS** | right angle, hypotenuse, one side | Pythagoras then fixes the third side, so it becomes SSS |

- **AAS** also works: if two angles are equal, the third is too (angle sum 180°), so it turns into ASA.

> [!WARNING]
> **AAA is not a test.** Two triangles with angles 60°, 60°, 60° can be tiny or huge — same shape, different size.
> **SSA is not a test** either (the angle is not between the two sides) — two different triangles can be drawn.

## Worked example

**Example:** in △ABC, AB = AC, and AD is the median to BC. Show ∠B = ∠C.

- In △ABD and △ACD: AB = AC (given), BD = DC (D is the midpoint), AD = AD (common).
- So △ABD ≅ △ACD by **SSS**, and ∠B = ∠C by **CPCT**.

That's **why** the base angles of an isosceles triangle are equal.

## Quick practice

1. △DEF ≅ △XYZ. Which side equals EF?
2. Which test: AB = PQ, BC = QR, ∠B = ∠Q?
3. Which test: ∠A = ∠P, ∠B = ∠Q, AB = PQ?
4. Two right triangles have equal hypotenuses and one equal side. Congruent? By which test?
5. Why can't you use AAA to prove congruence?
6. △ABC ≅ △LMN, ∠A = 50°, ∠M = 70°. Find ∠C.
7. In △PQR and △STU, PQ = ST, QR = TU, PR = SU. Write the congruence.

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. **YZ**
2. **SAS** (∠B is between AB and BC)
3. **ASA**
4. **Yes, RHS**
5. Equal angles fix the shape, not the size.
6. ∠B = ∠M = 70° → ∠C = 180 − 50 − 70 = **60°**
7. **△PQR ≅ △STU** (SSS)

</details>
