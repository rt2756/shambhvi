# 5. Operations on Rational Numbers

These are the fraction rules from Chapter 2, plus the integer sign rules from Chapter 1. Nothing new — just both at once.

## Adding

**Example:** −3/4 + 5/6. LCM of 4 and 6 is 12 → −9/12 + 10/12 = **1/12**.

- **Rule:** write each in standard form (positive denominator), make the denominators equal with the LCM, add the numerators using integer rules.

> [!WARNING]
> Fix the sign first: 5/(−6) must become −5/6 before you find the LCM.

## Additive inverse (the negative)

- The **additive inverse** of a/b is −a/b, because they add to **0**: 2/7 + (−2/7) = 0.
- Inverse of −5/9 is **5/9**. Inverse of 0 is 0.

## Subtracting

**Rule:** a − b = a + (additive inverse of b). Subtracting is adding the opposite.

**Example:** 2/3 − (−1/4) = 2/3 + 1/4 = 8/12 + 3/12 = **11/12**.

**Why it works:** the same as with integers — taking away a negative is the same as adding a positive.

## Multiplying

**Example:** (−4/9) × (3/8) = (−4 × 3)/(9 × 8) = −12/72 = **−1/6**.

- **Rule:** numerator × numerator, denominator × denominator; the sign follows the integer rule (same signs → +, different → −). Cancel first when you can.

## Reciprocal (multiplicative inverse)

- The **reciprocal** of a/b is b/a, because a/b × b/a = **1**.
- Reciprocal of −3/7 is **−7/3** (the sign stays). 0 has **no** reciprocal (nothing × 0 gives 1).

> [!WARNING]
> The reciprocal keeps the sign; the additive inverse flips it. For −3/7: reciprocal = −7/3, additive inverse = 3/7.

## Dividing

**Rule:** a/b ÷ c/d = a/b × d/c — multiply by the reciprocal.

**Example:** (−5/6) ÷ (10/(−3)) = (−5/6) × (−3/10) = 15/60 = **1/4**.

**Why it works:** "÷ c/d" asks what you multiply c/d by to get a/b. Multiplying by d/c first undoes c/d (they make 1), so a/b × d/c is that number.

## Properties

| Property | + | − | × | ÷ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Closure | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✗ (can't ÷ 0) |
| Commutative | ✓ | ✗ | ✓ | ✗ |
| Associative | ✓ | ✗ | ✓ | ✗ |
| Identity | 0 | — | 1 | — |
| Inverse | −a/b | — | b/a (not for 0) | — |

- **Distributive:** a × (b + c) = a × b + a × c. Use it to save work:
  - −3/5 × 2/7 + (−3/5) × 5/7 = −3/5 × (2/7 + 5/7) = −3/5 × 1 = **−3/5**.

**Why the table has ✓ where it does:** + and × don't care about order (3 + 2 = 2 + 3), but − and ÷ do (3 − 2 ≠ 2 − 3).

## Quick practice

1. 5/(−8) + 3/4 = ?
2. Additive inverse of −11/15?
3. −2/5 − (−7/10) = ?
4. (−7/12) × (−18/35) = ?
5. Reciprocal of −1 ⅔?
6. (−9/16) ÷ (3/4) = ?
7. The product of two rationals is −8/15. One is 4/5. Find the other.

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. −5/8 + 6/8 = **1/8**
2. **11/15**
3. −4/10 + 7/10 = **3/10**
4. 126/420 = **3/10**
5. −5/3 → reciprocal **−3/5**
6. −9/16 × 4/3 = **−3/4**
7. −8/15 ÷ 4/5 = −8/15 × 5/4 = **−2/3**
