# 15. Properties of Triangles

## Kinds of triangles

| By sides | | By angles | |
|---|---|---|---|
| Equilateral | all 3 sides equal (all angles 60°) | Acute | all angles < 90° |
| Isosceles | 2 sides equal (the angles opposite them are equal) | Right | one angle = 90° |
| Scalene | no sides equal | Obtuse | one angle > 90° |

- **Median:** joins a vertex to the **midpoint** of the opposite side. **Altitude:** from a vertex, **perpendicular** to the opposite side.

## Angle sum = 180°

**Example:** two angles are 50° and 70°. Third = 180 − 50 − 70 = **60°**.

**Why it works:** draw a line through the top vertex parallel to the base. The two bottom angles reappear at the top as **alternate angles** (Chapter 14). The three angles now sit side by side on a straight line — so they add to 180°.

- A triangle can have **at most one** right or obtuse angle. **Why:** two angles of 90° or more already add to 180° or more, leaving nothing for the third.

## Exterior angle

- Extend one side; the angle outside is the **exterior angle**.
- **Exterior angle = sum of the two interior opposite angles.**
- Example: interior opposite angles 40° and 75° → exterior = **115°**.

**Why it works:** the exterior angle + the angle next to it = 180° (linear pair). The three interior angles also = 180°. Take away the shared angle from both: what's left is equal.

## Triangle inequality

- **Any two sides added together must be longer than the third side.**
- 3, 4, 8 → 3 + 4 = 7 < 8 → **not a triangle**. 5, 6, 9 → 5 + 6 = 11 > 9 ✓.

**Why it works:** the straight path from A to B is the shortest. Going A → C → B is a detour, so it must be longer than AB.

> [!TIP]
> You only need to check the two **shortest** sides against the longest one.

## Pythagoras' theorem (right triangles)

- In a right triangle, the side opposite the right angle is the **hypotenuse** (the longest side).
- **hypotenuse² = base² + perpendicular²**. Sides 3, 4 → hyp² = 9 + 16 = 25 → hyp = **5**.
- **Converse:** if a² + b² = c² for the sides, the triangle has a right angle. 5, 12, 13 → 25 + 144 = 169 ✓.

**Why it works (picture proof):** put four copies of the right triangle inside a big square of side (a + b), leaving a tilted square of side c in the middle. Big square = (a + b)² = 4 triangles + c². Rearrange the same four triangles into two rectangles; now the space left is a² + b². Same big square, same four triangles — so c² = a² + b².

> [!WARNING]
> The hypotenuse is always the **longest** side. To find a shorter side, **subtract**: 13² − 5² = 144 → 12.

## Quick practice

1. Angles of a triangle are in the ratio 2 : 3 : 4. Find them.
2. One angle of a right triangle is 35°. Find the third.
3. An exterior angle is 120°, one interior opposite angle is 50°. Find the other.
4. Can 4 cm, 6 cm, 11 cm form a triangle?
5. Legs 6 cm and 8 cm. Hypotenuse?
6. Hypotenuse 17 cm, one side 8 cm. The other side?
7. Is 7, 24, 25 a right triangle?

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. 180 ÷ 9 = 20 → **40°, 60°, 80°**
2. **55°**
3. **70°**
4. 4 + 6 = 10 < 11 → **no**
5. √(36 + 64) = **10 cm**
6. √(289 − 64) = √225 = **15 cm**
7. 49 + 576 = 625 = 25² → **yes**
