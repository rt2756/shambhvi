# 6. Exponents

## Base and exponent

**Example:** 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 = **2⁵** = 32. Here 2 is the **base** and 5 is the **exponent** (or power). Read it "2 to the power 5".

- aⁿ = a × a × … × a (n times). It's just short for repeated multiplication — the way 4 × 3 is short for 3 + 3 + 3 + 3.
- **Powers of a negative base:** (−2)³ = −8, (−2)⁴ = 16. **Even** power → +, **odd** power → −. **Why:** an even count of minus signs pair up and cancel (Chapter 1).

> [!WARNING]
> 2⁵ ≠ 2 × 5. And −2⁴ = −(2⁴) = −16, but (−2)⁴ = +16 — the bracket decides what is being powered.

## The laws (each one is just counting)

| Law | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Product | aᵐ × aⁿ = aᵐ⁺ⁿ | 3² × 3⁴ = 3⁶ |
| Quotient | aᵐ ÷ aⁿ = aᵐ⁻ⁿ | 5⁷ ÷ 5³ = 5⁴ |
| Power of a power | (aᵐ)ⁿ = aᵐⁿ | (2³)² = 2⁶ |
| Same power, multiply | aᵐ × bᵐ = (ab)ᵐ | 2³ × 5³ = 10³ |
| Same power, divide | aᵐ ÷ bᵐ = (a/b)ᵐ | 6⁴ ÷ 3⁴ = 2⁴ |
| Zero exponent | a⁰ = 1 (a ≠ 0) | 7⁰ = 1 |

**Why each law works:**
- **Product:** 3² × 3⁴ = (3·3) × (3·3·3·3) — count the 3s: 2 + 4 = **6**.
- **Quotient:** 5⁷ ÷ 5³ — write it as a fraction; three 5s on the bottom cancel three on top, leaving 7 − 3 = **4**.
- **Power of a power:** (2³)² = 2³ × 2³ — two groups of three 2s = 3 × 2 = **6** twos.
- **Same power:** 2³ × 5³ = (2·2·2)(5·5·5) — pair them up: (2·5)(2·5)(2·5) = 10³.
- **Zero exponent:** 7³ ÷ 7³ = 1 (anything ÷ itself), but by the quotient law it's 7³⁻³ = 7⁰. So 7⁰ must be **1**.

> [!WARNING]
> The laws only work with the **same base** (product/quotient) or the **same exponent** (pairing). 2³ × 3² is not 6⁵ — just work it out: 8 × 9 = 72.

## Using the laws

**Example:** simplify (2⁵ × 2³) ÷ 2⁴ = 2⁸ ÷ 2⁴ = 2⁴ = **16**.

**Example:** (3⁴ × 9) ÷ 27 → write everything as powers of 3: 3⁴ × 3² ÷ 3³ = 3⁶⁻³ = 3³ = **27**.

> [!TIP]
> Break every number into prime powers first (9 = 3², 27 = 3³, 8 = 2³, 16 = 2⁴, 125 = 5³). Then the laws do the rest.

## Expanded form and standard form

- **Expanded form with powers of 10:** 47 053 = 4 × 10⁴ + 7 × 10³ + 0 × 10² + 5 × 10¹ + 3 × 10⁰.
- **Standard form (scientific notation):** a number between 1 and 10, × a power of 10.
  - 5 900 000 = **5.9 × 10⁶** (the point moved 6 places left).
  - Distance Earth–Sun ≈ 150 000 000 km = **1.5 × 10⁸ km**.

**Why it helps:** huge numbers become short and easy to compare — just look at the power of 10 first.
