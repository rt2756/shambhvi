# 3. Decimals

## Place value

**Example:** 42.375 → 4 tens, 2 ones **.** 3 tenths, 7 hundredths, 5 thousandths.

| Tens | Ones | . | Tenths (1/10) | Hundredths (1/100) | Thousandths (1/1000) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | 2 | . | 3 | 7 | 5 |

**Why it works:** every place to the right is **10 times smaller** than the one before. The pattern of ones → tens → hundreds just carries on past the decimal point.

- **Decimal → fraction:** count the decimal places, put that many zeros under 1: 0.35 = 35/100 = **7/20**; 2.4 = 24/10 = **12/5**.
- **Fraction → decimal:** divide top by bottom, or make the bottom 10, 100, 1000: 3/4 = 75/100 = **0.75**; 1/8 = 0.125.

## Comparing

- Line up the decimal points, pad with zeros, then compare left to right: 0.5 vs 0.48 → 0.50 vs 0.48 → **0.5 is bigger**.

> [!WARNING]
> 0.48 is **not** bigger than 0.5 just because 48 > 5. Pad first: 50 hundredths beats 48 hundredths.

## Adding and subtracting

**Example:** 12.5 + 3.75 → 12.50 + 3.75 = **16.25**.

- **Rule:** line up the decimal points, fill gaps with zeros, then add or subtract like whole numbers.

**Why it works:** lining up the points puts tenths under tenths and hundredths under hundredths — you only add the same kind of thing.

## Multiplying

- **By 10, 100, 1000:** move the point right 1, 2, 3 places: 3.457 × 100 = **345.7**. **Why:** each place is worth 10 times more, so ×10 shifts every digit one place up.
- **Decimal × decimal:** multiply as whole numbers, then count the **total** decimal places in both numbers and put the point that many places from the right.
  - 1.2 × 0.03 → 12 × 3 = 36 → 1 + 2 = 3 places → **0.036**.

**Why it works:** 1.2 = 12/10 and 0.03 = 3/100. Multiplying gives 36/1000 — the 10 × 100 = 1000 is where the 3 places come from.

## Dividing

- **By 10, 100, 1000:** move the point **left**: 45.6 ÷ 100 = **0.456**.
- **Decimal ÷ whole number:** divide as usual, keep the point in the same column: 7.5 ÷ 3 = **2.5**.
- **Decimal ÷ decimal:** multiply **both** numbers by 10, 100… until the divisor is a whole number:
  - 2.56 ÷ 0.8 → 25.6 ÷ 8 = **3.2**.

**Why it works:** a division is a fraction, 2.56/0.8. Multiplying top and bottom by 10 doesn't change its value (it's ×10/10 = ×1), but now you divide by a whole number.

> [!TIP]
> Quick check: 0.8 is a bit under 1, so 2.56 ÷ 0.8 should be a bit **more** than 2.56. 3.2 ✓.

## Word problems

**Example:** 1 kg of apples costs ₹82.50. Cost of 3.4 kg? 82.5 × 3.4 → 825 × 34 = 28050 → 2 places → **₹280.50**.

## Quick practice

1. Write 0.625 as a fraction in lowest terms.
2. Write 7/25 as a decimal.
3. Arrange in descending order: 0.7, 0.07, 0.77, 0.707.
4. 25.07 − 9.6 = ?
5. 0.25 × 0.4 = ?
6. 0.9 ÷ 0.03 = ?
7. A car runs 14.5 km on 1 litre. How far on 6.4 litres?
