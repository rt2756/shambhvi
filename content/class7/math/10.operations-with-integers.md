## Recap: adding and subtracting integers

**Rakesh's puzzle:** two numbers add up to 25 and their difference (first − second) is 11. Try pairs that add to 25: (20, 5) has difference 15, (19, 6) has 13, **(18, 7) has 11** ✔. And for difference **−11**, just swap them: **(7, 18)**.

> [!TIP]
> **A shortcut for sum-and-difference puzzles:** first number = (sum + difference) ÷ 2, second = (sum − difference) ÷ 2.
> **Why:** adding "a + b" and "a − b" cancels b and gives 2a. Subtracting them cancels a and gives 2b.

**Book Figure it Out:**
- Sum 27, difference 9 → **18 and 9** · sum 4, difference 12 → **8 and −4** · sum 0, difference 10 → **5 and −5** · sum 0, difference −10 → **−5 and 5** · sum −7, difference −1 → **−4 and −3** · sum −7, difference −13 → **−10 and 3**.

### Carrom coin on a number line

A coin is struck left or right. Call **right +** and **left −**. Strike 5 right then 7 left: final position = **5 + (−7) = −2** (2 units left of 0).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **An integer carries two pieces of information:** its **magnitude** (how far) and its **sign** (which direction). After moves a and b, the position is **a + b**, whatever the directions.

- First move −4, final position 5 → second move = 5 − (−4) = **9**.
- Moves 1, −2, 3, −4, …, 9, −10 → pair them: (1 − 2) + (3 − 4) + … = five (−1)s = **−5**.

### The token model

**Green token = +1, red token = −1.** A green and a red together make a **zero pair** (they cancel).

**7 − 18:** we must remove 18 greens but have only 7. Add **11 zero pairs** (adding zero changes nothing), now remove 18 greens → **11 reds left = −11**.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Subtracting a number = adding its additive inverse.** 7 − 18 = 7 + (−18); 4 − (−12) = 4 + 12.
> **Why (tokens):** removing 18 greens leaves exactly the same pile as dropping in 18 reds — each red cancels one green.
> The **additive inverse** of a is written −a: −(18) = −18 and −(−18) = 18.

## Multiplying integers with tokens

Multiplication uses a **bag** that starts **empty** each time.
- **Positive multiplier → put tokens IN** that many times.
- **Negative multiplier → take tokens OUT** that many times.

| Calculation | What we do | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **4 × 2** | put in 2 greens, 4 times | 8 greens = **8** |
| **4 × (−2)** | put in 2 reds, 4 times | 8 reds = **−8** |
| **(−4) × 2** | take out 2 greens, 4 times — bag is empty, so first add 2 zero pairs each time, then remove the greens | 8 reds left = **−8** |
| **(−4) × (−2)** | take out 2 reds, 4 times — add zero pairs, remove the reds | 8 greens left = **+8** |

> [!NOTE]
> **Why do we remove greens in (−4) × 2?** The multiplicand is **+2**, i.e. two green tokens. The negative multiplier says "do the opposite of putting them in" — so we take greens out. Taking greens out of nothing leaves reds behind.

**Book Figure it Out (tokens):** 3 × (−2) = **−6** · (−5) × (−2) = **10** · (−4) × (−1) = **4** · (−7) × 3 = **−21**.
**If 123 × 456 = 56,088:** (−123) × 456 = **−56,088** · (−123) × (−456) = **56,088** · 123 × (−456) = **−56,088**.

### The pattern way — sign rules that must be true

Count down the multiplier and watch the product:

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| 3 × 3 = 9 | 3 × (−3) = −9 | |
| 2 × 3 = 6 | 2 × (−3) = −6 | |
| 1 × 3 = 3 | 1 × (−3) = −3 | |
| 0 × 3 = 0 | 0 × (−3) = 0 | |
| (−1) × 3 = **−3** | (−1) × (−3) = **3** | |
| (−2) × 3 = **−6** | (−2) × (−3) = **6** | |

In the first column the product **goes down by 3** each step, so after 0 it must continue to −3, −6. In the second column the product **goes up by 3** each step, so after 0 it must continue to +3, +6.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Sign rules for × (and ÷):**
> - positive × positive = **positive**
> - negative × negative = **positive**
> - positive × negative = **negative**, negative × positive = **negative**
> The **magnitude** is just the ordinary product of the magnitudes.
> **Why negative × negative is positive:** the pattern above forces it — and in tokens, taking away debts leaves you better off.

> [!NOTE]
> **Brahmagupta's rules (628 CE)**, with *dhana* (fortune) for positive and *ṛṇa* (debt) for negative: "The product or quotient of two fortunes is a fortune. Of two debts is a fortune. Of a debt and a fortune is a debt." This was the first time rules for multiplying and dividing negative numbers were written down.

**Book Figure it Out:** 4 × (−3) = **−12** · (−6) × (−3) = **18** · (−5) × (−1) = **5** · (−8) × 4 = **−32** · (−9) × 10 = **−90** · 10 × (−17) = **−170**.

### Multiplying by 1 and by −1

- **1 × a = a** for every integer (put a's tokens in once).
- **(−1) × a = −a** — the additive inverse. (−1) × 5 = −5, (−1) × (−5) = 5.

**Book:** the integer whose product with (−1) is 27 → **−27**; −31 → **31**; −1 → **1**; 1 → **−1**; 0 → **0**.

## Properties of integer multiplication

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Commutative:** a × b = b × a. (−30) × 12 = 12 × (−30) = **−360**.
> **Why:** the magnitude doesn't care about order, and the sign rule only asks "same signs or different?" — which doesn't change when you swap.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Associative:** (a × b) × c = a × (b × c).
> (5 × (−3)) × 4 = −15 × 4 = −60, and 5 × ((−3) × 4) = 5 × (−12) = −60, and (5 × 4) × (−3) = −60. **Any order, any grouping, same product.**

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Sign of a long product: count the negative numbers.** An **even** count → **positive**; an **odd** count → **negative**.
> **Why:** negatives cancel in pairs (− × − = +). (−1) × (−1) = 1; × (−1) again = −1; again = 1; again = −1 …
> (−2) × (−1) × (−5) × (−3): four negatives → positive → **30**.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Distributive:** a × (b + c) = a × b + a × c — still true with negatives.
> 5 × (4 + (−2)) = 5 × 2 = 10 and 5 × 4 + 5 × (−2) = 20 − 10 = 10 ✔. (−2) × (4 + (−3)) = −2 and (−8) + 6 = −2 ✔.
> **Why (tokens):** a 4 × (2 + (−3)) rectangle of tokens is a 4 × 2 green block next to a 4 × (−3) red block.

## Word problems with signs

**Mala's exam (book):** 50 questions, +5 for right, −2 for wrong. 30 right and 20 wrong → 30 × 5 + 20 × (−2) = 150 − 40 = **110**. Best possible: 50 × 5 = **250**; worst: 50 × (−2) = **−100**.

**Mine elevator (book):** moves 3 m per minute; **down = −3**.
- From ground (0), 1 hour down: 60 × (−3) = **−180** → 180 m below ground.
- From 15 m up, 45 minutes down: 15 + 45 × (−3) = 15 − 135 = **−120** → 120 m below ground.

> [!TIP]
> **Model the rate as a signed number** (−3 m/min, −5 °C/hour, +₹8 per bag), then **final = start + rate × time**.

**A magic grid (book):**

| | | | |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | −4 | 12 | −6 |
| −28 | 14 | −42 | 21 |
| 12 | −6 | 18 | −9 |
| 20 | −10 | 30 | −15 |

Circle a number, strike out its row and column, repeat until nothing is left, then multiply the four circled numbers. **You always get −30,240.**
**Why it's magic:** every entry is (a row number) × (a column number), with rows 2, −7, 3, 5 and columns 4, −2, 6, −3 (e.g. −42 = −7 × 6). Your four circles use each row once and each column once, so the product is always (2 × −7 × 3 × 5) × (4 × −2 × 6 × −3) = (−210) × 144 = −30,240.

## Dividing integers

Division undoes multiplication. **(−100) ÷ 25** asks "25 × ? = −100" → **−4**. **(−100) ÷ (−4)** asks "(−4) × ? = −100" → **25**. **50 ÷ (−25)** → **−2**.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Division uses the same sign rules as multiplication:** same signs → positive, different signs → negative.
> a ÷ (−b) = −(a ÷ b) · (−a) ÷ b = −(a ÷ b) · (−a) ÷ (−b) = a ÷ b.
> **Why:** a division is a multiplication question in disguise, so its answer must obey the multiplication sign rules.

**Book Figure it Out:**
1. 14 × (−15) = **−210** · (−16) × (−5) = **80** · 36 ÷ (−18) = **−2** · (−46) ÷ (−23) = **2**
2. **Freezing:** starts 32 °C, drops 5 °C per hour for 10 hours → 32 + 10 × (−5) = **−18 °C**.
3. **Cement:** white +₹8 per bag, grey −₹5 per bag.
   - 3,000 white and 5,000 grey: 24,000 − 25,000 = **−₹1,000 → a loss of ₹1,000**.
   - 6,400 grey bags lose 6,400 × 5 = ₹32,000 → white bags needed to break even: 32,000 ÷ 8 = **4,000**.
4. **Blanks:** (−3) × **(−9)** = 27 · 5 × **(−7)** = −35 · **7** × (−8) = −56 · **(−11)** × (−12) = 132 · **(−56)** ÷ (−8) = 7 · **(−132)** ÷ 12 = −11

## In a nutshell

- Integers have **size and direction**; right/up/profit = +, left/down/loss = −.
- **Subtracting = adding the inverse.**
- **× and ÷:** same signs → **+**, different signs → **−**.
- Many factors: **even number of negatives → +, odd → −**.
- **1 × a = a, (−1) × a = −a.**
- Multiplication of integers is **commutative, associative and distributive** over addition.
- Word problems: **final = start + (signed rate) × time**.

> [!WARNING]
> **Top traps:** (−4) × (−2) is +8, not −8 · (−1)⁵ is −1 (odd count) · 0 × anything = 0, and 0 is neither positive nor negative · (−46) ÷ (−23) = +2 · "a loss of ₹1,000" is −1000 · subtracting a negative adds: 4 − (−12) = 16.
