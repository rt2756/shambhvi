The year's maths follows the new NCERT book ***Ganita Prakash*** for Grade 7, which comes in **two parts**. Every chapter below has **detailed notes** on this page and a matching **4-level test** (Simple → Hard → Harder → Hardest) on the Tests page — same chapters, same order.

## Part 1

1. **Large Numbers Around Us** — lakh, crore, arab; Indian vs American number names; rounding and nearest neighbours; estimating; multiplication shortcuts; how many digits a product has.
2. **Arithmetic Expressions** — terms, brackets, comparing without calculating, removing brackets, the distributive property.
3. **A Peek Beyond the Point** — tenths, hundredths, thousandths; decimal place value; unit conversions; comparing, adding and subtracting decimals.
4. **Expressions using Letter-Numbers** — letters for numbers, formulas, like terms, simplifying, using algebra to prove patterns always hold.
5. **Parallel and Intersecting Lines** — linear pairs, vertically opposite angles, transversals, corresponding / alternate / interior angles.
6. **Number Play** — parity (odd and even), magic squares, Virahāṅka–Fibonacci numbers, cryptarithms.
7. **A Tale of Three Intersecting Lines** — constructing triangles, the triangle inequality, angle sum 180°, exterior angles, altitudes, types of triangles.
8. **Working with Fractions** — multiplying and dividing fractions, reciprocals, Brahmagupta's rules, problems from old Indian texts.

## Part 2

9. **Geometric Twins** *(Part 2, Ch 1)* — congruence; SSS, SAS, ASA, AAS, RHS; why AAA and SSA fail; isosceles and equilateral triangle angles.
10. **Operations with Integers** *(Part 2, Ch 2)* — multiplying and dividing integers (tokens and patterns), sign rules, properties.
11. **Finding Common Ground** *(Part 2, Ch 3)* — prime factorisation, HCF and LCM, and HCF × LCM = product.
12. **Another Peek Beyond the Point** *(Part 2, Ch 4)* — multiplying and dividing decimals, never-ending decimals, the leap-year rules.
13. **Connecting the Dots…** *(Part 2, Ch 5)* — mean, median, outliers, dot plots, double bar graphs, reading data.
14. **Constructions and Tilings** *(Part 2, Ch 6)* — ruler-and-compass constructions (perpendicular bisector, angle bisector, copying angles, 60°, hexagon); tiling.
15. **Finding the Unknown** *(Part 2, Ch 7)* — equations, solving by doing the same to both sides, word problems, Brahmagupta's formula.

> [!TIP]
> **Chapters that build on each other:** 3 → 12 (decimals), 2 → 4 → 15 (expressions → equations), 5 → 7 → 9 → 14 (lines → triangles → congruence → constructions), 8 uses the fraction ideas from Class 6, and 10 uses the integer ideas from Class 6.

> [!NOTE]
> **Stuck on a chapter?** Its foundation is usually one class below — see the **Class 6** map (Prime Time → Ch 11, Fractions → Ch 8, Integers → Ch 10, Playing with Constructions → Ch 7 and 14).
