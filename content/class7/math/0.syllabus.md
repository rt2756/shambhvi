# 📖 Syllabus map — Class 7 Maths

<!-- Site's own 13-chapter organisation (from ToStudy.md — the school follows the older
     NCERT Class 7 maths syllabus). Every chapter below has full notes AND practice questions. -->

Everything for the year, in the order the panels below follow. **Every chapter has full notes and a matching practice set** on the Questions page.

1. ✅ **Numbers** — integers and their signs, factors & multiples, primes, HCF & LCM, BODMAS.
2. ✅ **Fractions & decimals** — all four operations with both, and the place-value thinking behind them.
3. ✅ **Geometry** — angles, shapes, 3D solids and nets, symmetry.
4. ✅ **Algebra** — expressions and like terms, simple equations, ratio & proportion.
5. ✅ **Data handling** — tally marks, bar graphs, mean/median/mode/range, probability.
6. ✅ **Measurements** — length, weight, capacity, time, and the big number names.
7. ✅ **Calculations** — tables to 20, squares to 25, cubes to 10, with shortcuts to rebuild any of them.
8. ✅ **Lines & angles** — complementary/supplementary pairs, linear pairs, parallel lines and transversals.
9. ✅ **Triangles** — angle sum, exterior angle, triangle inequality, Pythagoras.
10. ✅ **Comparing quantities** — percentages, profit & loss, simple interest.
11. ✅ **Rational numbers** — p/q numbers, standard form, comparing and all four operations.
12. ✅ **Perimeter & area** — squares, rectangles, parallelograms, triangles and circles; path problems.
13. ✅ **Exponents & powers** — the laws of exponents and standard form for huge numbers.

> Stuck on a chapter? Its foundation is usually one class below — check the **Class 6** map (Prime Time → Ch 1, Fractions → Ch 2, Perimeter and Area → Ch 12, Symmetry → Ch 3).
