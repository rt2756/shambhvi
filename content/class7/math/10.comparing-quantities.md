# 10. Comparing quantities

## Percentage = per hundred

- **%** means "out of 100": `45% = 45/100 = 0.45`.
- **Fraction → %:** multiply by 100 → `3/4 = 3/4 × 100% = 75%`.
- **Decimal → %:** multiply by 100 → `0.35 = 35%` · **% → decimal:** divide by 100 → `8% = 0.08`.
- **% → fraction:** put it over 100, then simplify → `20% = 20/100 = 1/5`.
- **Ratio → %:** each share ÷ total parts × 100 → split `3 : 1` → `3/4 = 75%` and `1/4 = 25%`.
- All the parts of a whole add to **100%**.

> [!TIP]
> Know by heart: `1/2 = 50%` · `1/4 = 25%` · `3/4 = 75%` · `1/5 = 20%` · `1/10 = 10%` · `1/100 = 1%`.

## Using percentages

- **x% of a quantity:** `x/100 × quantity` → 15% of 200 = `15/100 × 200 = 30`.
- **A is what % of B?** → `A/B × 100%` → 12 marks out of 50 = `12/50 × 100% = 24%`.
- **% increase or decrease = change ÷ original × 100%** — the base is always the **original** value.
  - Example: price goes ₹40 → ₹50 → change = 10 → `10/40 × 100% = 25%` increase.

## Profit & loss

- **CP** = cost price (what it cost) · **SP** = selling price (what it sold for).
- SP > CP → **profit = SP − CP** · SP < CP → **loss = CP − SP**.
- **Profit% = profit/CP × 100** · **Loss% = loss/CP × 100**.
- Example: bought at ₹250, sold at ₹325 → profit = ₹75 → profit% = `75/250 × 100 = 30%`.

## Simple interest (SI)

- **P** = principal (money borrowed) · **R** = rate % per year · **T** = time in **years**.
- **SI = P × R × T ÷ 100** · **Amount = P + SI**.
- Example: ₹5000 at 8% per year for 2 years → SI = `5000 × 8 × 2 ÷ 100` = ₹800 → amount = ₹5800.

> [!WARNING]
> Profit % and loss % are always on the **CP**, never the SP · in % change, divide by the **original** value · T must be in years (6 months = `1/2` year).
