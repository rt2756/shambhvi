## Quick recap: decimals are fractions with 10, 100, 1000 … below

**27.53** = 2 tens + 7 ones + 5 tenths + 3 hundredths.

| Fraction | As a sum | Decimal |
|---|---|---|
| 254/1000 | 2/10 + 5/100 + 4/1000 | **0.254** |
| 50 g = 50/1000 kg | 5/100 | **0.05 kg** |
| 250 g = 250/1000 kg | 2/10 + 5/100 | **0.25 kg** |
| 25 g | 2/100 + 5/1000 | **0.025 kg** |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **To divide by 10, 100, 1000 …, move the decimal point LEFT one place for each zero** (put in zeros at the front if needed).
> 123 ÷ 10 = 12.3 · 24 ÷ 100 = 0.24 · 678 ÷ 1000 = 0.678 · 12 ÷ 1000 = **0.012** · 12345 ÷ 1000 = 12.345
> **Why:** dividing by 10 makes every digit worth ten times less — each digit slides one place to the right, which is the same as the point moving one place left.
> **Multiplying** by 10, 100, 1000 moves the point **right**: 0.306 × 100 = 30.6.

## Multiplying decimals

**Arshad buys 5 pens at ₹9.5 each.** 9.5 × 5 = 9.5 + 9.5 + 9.5 + 9.5 + 9.5 = **₹47.5**. Or as fractions: 95/10 × 5 = 475/10 = 47.5.

**A car does 12.5 km per litre. How far on 7.5 litres?** 12.5 × 7.5 = 125/10 × 75/10 = 9375/100 = **93.75 km**.

**Ajay walks 827 m to school and back, 6 days a week.** 0.827 × 2 = 1.654 km a day; × 6 = **9.924 km** a week.

**Rectangle 5.7 cm × 13.3 cm:** 57/10 × 133/10 = 7581/100 = **75.81 sq cm**.

| Calculation | Decimal places in the two numbers | In the product |
|---|---|---|
| 9.5 × 5 = 47.5 | 1 + 0 | 1 |
| 12.5 × 7.5 = 93.75 | 1 + 1 | 2 |
| 1.64 × 6 = 9.84 | 2 + 0 | 2 |
| 5.7 × 13.35 = 76.095 | 1 + 2 | 3 |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **To multiply decimals: ignore the points, multiply as whole numbers, then give the answer as many decimal places as the two numbers have together.**
> 596 × 248 = 1,47,808 → **5.96 × 24.8 = 147.808** (2 + 1 = 3 places). 58 × 124 = 7192 → **5.8 × 1.24 = 7.192**.
> **Why:** each decimal is a fraction over 10, 100, 1000 … Multiplying fractions multiplies the denominators, so the zeros add up: 100 × 10 = 1000 → 3 decimal places.

> [!WARNING]
> **Count places in the question, not in the answer.** 0.432 × 0.23: 432 × 23 = 9936, and 3 + 2 = 5 places → **0.09936** (put a zero in front to make 5 places).

**Can a product of decimals be a whole number?** Yes: 0.5 × 4 = 2, and 2.5 × 0.4 = 1. The extra zero at the end "uses up" a decimal place.

### Is the product bigger or smaller?

Exactly as with fractions:

| Numbers | Example | Product |
|---|---|---|
| both greater than 1 | 3.4 × 6.5 = 22.1 | bigger than both |
| both between 0 and 1 | 0.75 × 0.4 = 0.3 | smaller than both |
| one each | 0.75 × 5 = 3.75 | in between |

### Whole numbers that don't divide exactly

**Neenu shares 29 m of ribbon with Anu:** each gets 14 m with 1 m left over; half of that is 1/2 = 5/10 = 0.5 → **14.5 m**.
**Among 4 friends:** 29/4 — make the denominator 100: × 25 → 725/100 = **7.25 m**.

> [!TIP]
> **Fraction → decimal:** if you can turn the denominator into 10, 100, 1000 … (multiply top and bottom by the same number), the decimal is easy. 2/5 = 4/10 = **0.4** · 13/4 = 325/100 = **3.25** · 4/50 = 8/100 = **0.08** · 5/8 = 625/1000 = **0.625**.

### Long division keeps going past the point

**1325 ÷ 4.** Thousands, hundreds, tens, ones as usual: 331 with **1 one** left over. Now **regroup 1 one as 10 tenths**: 10 tenths ÷ 4 = 2 tenths, 2 tenths left. Regroup as **20 hundredths**: ÷ 4 = 5 hundredths, nothing left. So **1325 ÷ 4 = 331.25** ✔ (check: 1325 × 25/100 = 33125/100).

**237 ÷ 8:** 23 tens ÷ 8 = 2 (7 left) → 77 ones ÷ 8 = 9 (5 left) → **point** → 50 tenths ÷ 8 = 6 (2 left) → 20 hundredths ÷ 8 = 2 (4 left) → 40 thousandths ÷ 8 = 5 → **29.625**.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Long division with decimals: when you run out of ones, regroup the remainder into tenths (put the decimal point in the quotient there), then tenths into hundredths, and so on.**
> **Why:** 1 one = 10 tenths, 1 tenth = 10 hundredths — exactly like "1 hundred = 10 tens" in ordinary long division.

**A decimal dividend:** 9.5 kg of sugar in 4 bags → 9.5 ÷ 4 = **2.375 kg**. 0.06 ÷ 5 = **0.012**.

### Dividing BY a decimal

**Ravi rides 126 km in 2.5 hours.** Speed = 126 ÷ 2.5. Multiply both by 10: **1260 ÷ 25 = 50.4 km/h**.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **To divide by a decimal, multiply BOTH numbers by 10, 100, 1000 … until the divisor is a whole number — then divide as usual.**
> 4.68 ÷ 0.13 = 468 ÷ 13 = **36**.
> **Why it's allowed:** a ÷ b is the fraction a/b, and multiplying top and bottom of a fraction by the same number doesn't change its value. (Also: "how many 0.13s fit in 4.68" is the same as "how many 13s fit in 468".)

**Given 756 ÷ 36 = 21:** 75.6 ÷ 3.6 = **21** · 7.56 ÷ 0.36 = **21** · 756 ÷ 0.36 = **2100** · 75.6 ÷ 360 = **0.21** · 7560 ÷ 3.6 = **2100**.

### Does this ever end?

**10 ÷ 3:** each step leaves remainder 1, regroup, divide, remainder 1 again… **3.333…** forever. Some divisions **never end**.

**1 ÷ 7 = 0.142857 142857 …** The remainders go 1, 3, 2, 6, 4, 5, 1, … — when a remainder repeats, everything after repeats too.

> [!NOTE]
> **Why the digits must repeat:** dividing by 7, the remainder is always 0–6. If it's never 0, then within 6 steps some remainder must come back — and from then on the division is a re-run.
> **Magic number 142857:** × 2 = 285714, × 3 = 428571, × 4 = 571428, × 5 = 714285, × 6 = 857142 — the same digits going round! × 7 = **999999**. Another such "cyclic number" comes from 1 ÷ 17. Whether there are infinitely many is **Artin's conjecture (1927) — still unsolved.**

**When does a fraction's decimal end?** 1/2 = 0.5, 1/4 = 0.25, 1/8 = 0.125, 1/16 = 0.0625, **1/32 = 0.03125**; 1/5 = 0.2, 1/25 = 0.04, … **1/3125 = 0.00032**.
**Why 2 and 5 are special:** 10 = 2 × 5. A denominator made only of 2s and 5s can be topped up to a power of 10 (1/32 = 3125/100000), so the decimal ends. A 3 or 7 in the denominator can never become part of a power of 10.

### Bigger or smaller quotient?

128 ÷ 4 = 32 (smaller than 128), but **128 ÷ 0.4 = 320** (bigger!).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Dividing by a number less than 1 gives a bigger answer; dividing by a number greater than 1 gives a smaller one.** (Dividing by 0.4 = multiplying by 10/4 = 2.5.)

## Look before you leap — the calendar

The Earth takes **365.2422 days** to go round the Sun, but a calendar year has 365. The leftover 0.2422 day seems tiny — but after 100 years: 0.2422 × 100 = **24.22 days**! The seasons would drift.

| Rule | Days in 100 years | Days in 1000 years | Earth's actual |
|---|---|---|---|
| 365 every year | 36,500 | 3,65,000 | 36,524.22 / 3,65,242.2 |
| + leap year every 4th year | **36,525** (100 × 365 + 25) | — | over by 0.78 |
| …but not in years divisible by 100 | **36,524** (24 × 366 + 76 × 365) | 3,65,240 | short by 2.2 in 1000 |
| …except years divisible by 400 | — | **3,65,242** | short by only 0.2 |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Leap year rule:** a year is a leap year (366 days, with 29 February) if it is **divisible by 4**, **except** century years, which must be **divisible by 400**. 2024 ✔ · 1900 ✘ · 2000 ✔.
> **Why each exception:** +1 every 4 years adds 0.25 per year — slightly too much (0.2422 needed). Dropping 1 every 100 years corrects to 0.24; adding 1 back every 400 years gives 0.2425 — very close.

In 1000 years with the full rule: 2 years divisible by 400 (366 days), 8 more century years (365), 240 other multiples of 4 (366), 750 ordinary → 750 × 365 + 240 × 366 + 8 × 365 + 2 × 366 = **3,65,242 days**.

> [!TIP]
> **Over 10,000 years:** every 400 years has 146,097 days, so 10,000 years = 25 × 146,097 = 36,52,425 days, against the Earth's 36,52,422 — about **3 days** too many.

## In a nutshell

- ÷ 10, 100, 1000 → point moves **left**; × → point moves **right**.
- **Multiply:** multiply as whole numbers; decimal places in the answer = **total** in the question.
- **Divide by a whole number:** long division, regroup ones → tenths → hundredths, placing the point as you cross.
- **Divide by a decimal:** scale both numbers until the divisor is whole.
- × or ÷ by a number **below 1** works the "opposite" way to × or ÷ by a number above 1.
- Some divisions **never end** (10 ÷ 3, 1 ÷ 7); they **repeat**.
- Leap years: **÷ 4, except centuries unless ÷ 400**.

> [!WARNING]
> **Top traps:** 0.432 × 0.23 = 0.09936 (5 places, not 0.9936) · 12 ÷ 1000 = 0.012 · when dividing by a decimal, scale **both** numbers · 128 ÷ 0.4 is bigger than 128 · 1900 was not a leap year · 56.50 and 56.5 are equal.
