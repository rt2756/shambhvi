# 4. Algebra

## Expressions — the vocabulary

- A **variable** is a letter standing for an unknown number (x, y, n); a **constant** is a fixed number (4, −7).
- An **expression** joins constants and variables with + − × ÷: `2x + 5`, `3y² − 4y + 7`.
- **Terms** are the parts being added: the terms of `3y² − 4y + 7` are `3y²`, `−4y` and `7` — the sign travels with its term.
- **Coefficient** = the number multiplying the variable: in `−4y` it is **−4**.
- **Like terms** have exactly the **same variable part** (`2x` and `−5x` · `3xy` and `7xy`); **unlike terms** don't (`2x` and `2x²`).
- Count the terms: **monomial** (1 term: `7x`) · **binomial** (2 terms: `x + 4`) · **trinomial** (3 terms: `x² + 2x + 1`).

## Working with expressions

- **Add or subtract only like terms** — add the coefficients, keep the variable part: `5x + 3x = 8x` · `7xy − 2xy = 5xy` · `2x + 3y` stays as it is.
- Subtracting a bracket **flips every sign inside**: `(5x + 4) − (2x − 3) = 5x + 4 − 2x + 3 = 3x + 7`.
- **Value of an expression:** replace each variable with its number, then calculate: at `x = −2`, `x² + 2x + 1 = 4 − 4 + 1 = 1`.
- **Rules as formulas:** perimeter of a square = `4s` · nth term of 2, 4, 6, 8, … is `2n` · of 3, 5, 7, … is `2n + 1`.

> [!WARNING]
> `x + x = 2x` but `x × x = x²` · when subtracting a bracket, change **every** sign inside — not just the first one.

## Simple equations

- An **equation** says two expressions are equal: LHS = RHS (`2x + 3 = 11`). The value of x that makes it true is the **solution**.
- **Balance rule:** an equation stays true if you do the **same thing to both sides** (add, subtract, multiply, divide).
- **Transposing** (the shortcut): move a term to the other side of `=` and flip its job — + becomes −, × becomes ÷.
  - `2x + 3 = 11` → `2x = 11 − 3 = 8` → `x = 8 ÷ 2 = 4`.
- **Check** by putting the answer back: `2 × 4 + 3 = 11` ✓.
- **Words → equation:** "5 added to 3 times a number gives 26" → `3x + 5 = 26` → x = 7.

> [!TIP]
> Undo in reverse order: clear the + or − first, then the × or ÷.

## Ratio & proportion

- **Ratio** compares two amounts by division, written a : b (same units). Equivalent ratios work like equivalent fractions.
- **Proportion:** a : b = c : d (2 : 3 = 4 : 6); the product of the extremes = the product of the means.
- **Unitary method:** find the value of 1, then multiply (5 pens ₹50 → 1 pen ₹10 → 8 pens ₹80).

> [!WARNING]
> Make the units the same before writing a ratio · order matters (2 : 3 is not 3 : 2).
