# ✳️ Multiplication tables (1 to 20)

A **times table** is just **repeated addition**. The table of 7 is 7, then 7 + 7, then
7 + 7 + 7 … so each step goes **up by 7**. If you learn to *build* a row this way, you're
never truly stuck — even if you forget one, you can add your way to it.

**How to read the grid:** find the number on the **left**, then slide across to the
**× column** along the top. Where they meet is the answer — so row **7**, column **8** is
`7 × 8 = 56`. Each whole **row is one table**: read row 7 straight across and you are
reciting the table of 7.

> [!TIP]
> **Read every row out loud, top to bottom:** "seven ones are seven, seven twos are
> fourteen…". The tricky rows are usually **6× to 9×** and **13× to 19×** — give those
> extra time.

> [!NOTE]
> **Why the shortcuts work**
> - **× 10** — just add a zero: `14 × 10 = 140`.
> - **× 9** — one group less than × 10: `7 × 9 = 7 × 10 − 7 = 70 − 7 = 63`.
> - **× 5** — half of × 10: `8 × 5 = (8 × 10) ÷ 2 = 40`.
> - **× 11 (up to 9)** — just double the digit: `11 × 6 = 66`.
> - **Forgot one?** Add one more group to the one before it: if `7 × 6 = 42`, then
>   `7 × 7 = 42 + 7 = 49`.
