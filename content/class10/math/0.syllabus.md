# 📖 Syllabus map — Mathematics (Class 10)

<!-- Chapter list verified against ncert_books/class_10/maths.pdf (NCERT, rationalised edition — 14 chapters). -->

The whole year at a glance — NCERT **Class 10 Mathematics**, the board-exam year (14 chapters).

1. **Real Numbers** — the Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic (every number's unique prime fingerprint) and irrational numbers.
2. **Polynomials** — zeroes of a polynomial, what they look like on a graph, and how they relate to the coefficients.
3. **Pair of Linear Equations in Two Variables** — solving two equations together, graphically and algebraically.
4. **Quadratic Equations** — ax² + bx + c = 0: solving by factorisation and the formula, and what the roots tell you.
5. **Arithmetic Progressions** — sequences that grow by a fixed step, and the shortcut for adding them up.
6. **Triangles** — similarity: same shape, different size, and the ratios that follow.
7. **Coordinate Geometry** — distance between points, section formula — geometry done with algebra.
8. **Introduction to Trigonometry** — sin, cos and tan: ratios that turn angles into lengths, plus the identities.
9. **Some Applications of Trigonometry** — heights and distances you can't measure directly.
10. **Circles** — tangents to a circle and their properties.
11. **Areas Related to Circles** — sectors, segments and the areas of circular shapes.
12. **Surface Areas and Volumes** — solids and combinations of solids, inside and out.
13. **Statistics** — mean, median and mode for **grouped** data.
14. **Probability** — the formal, theoretical treatment of chance.

> [!TIP]
> The Class 7 chapters aren't decoration for this year: comparing quantities → Ch 1 & 13, algebra → Ch 2–4, triangles → Ch 6 & 8, perimeter–area → Ch 11–12, data handling → Ch 13–14.
