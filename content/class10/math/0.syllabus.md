# 📖 Syllabus map — Mathematics (Class 10)

<!-- Chapter list verified against ncert_books/class_10/maths.pdf (NCERT, rationalised edition, Reprint 2026-27 — 14 chapters). -->

The whole year at a glance — NCERT **Class 10 Mathematics**, the board-exam year (14 chapters).

**Every chapter has detailed notes (with the book's examples and exercises worked) and four self-marking tests — Simple, Hard, Harder, Hardest — on the Tests page.**

1. **Real Numbers** — the Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic (every number's unique prime fingerprint), HCF and LCM, and proving √2, √3, √5 irrational.
2. **Polynomials** — zeroes of a polynomial, what they look like on a graph, and how their sum and product come from the coefficients.
3. **Pair of Linear Equations in Two Variables** — two equations together: graphs, the ratio test, substitution and elimination.
4. **Quadratic Equations** — ax² + bx + c = 0: factorisation, the quadratic formula, and what the discriminant tells you.
5. **Arithmetic Progressions** — lists that grow by a fixed step: the nth term and the sum of n terms.
6. **Triangles** — similarity: same shape, different size; the Basic Proportionality Theorem; AA, SSS and SAS.
7. **Coordinate Geometry** — the distance formula and the section formula: geometry done with algebra.
8. **Introduction to Trigonometry** — sin, cos and tan, their values at 0°, 30°, 45°, 60°, 90°, and the identities.
9. **Some Applications of Trigonometry** — heights and distances with angles of elevation and depression.
10. **Circles** — tangents: perpendicular to the radius, and equal from an outside point.
11. **Areas Related to Circles** — sectors, segments and arc lengths.
12. **Surface Areas and Volumes** — combinations of cubes, cylinders, cones, spheres and hemispheres.
13. **Statistics** — mean, mode and median of **grouped** data.
14. **Probability** — the theoretical approach: coins, dice, cards.

> [!NOTE]
> **What the rationalised book left out** (so it isn't in these notes either): Euclid's division lemma, the division algorithm for polynomials, cross-multiplication, completing the square as a method, areas of similar triangles and the Pythagoras proofs, area of a triangle from coordinates, complementary-angle ratios, frustums and conversion of solids, and ogives. The two appendices (Proofs in Mathematics, Mathematical Modelling) aren't examined.

> [!TIP]
> Earlier classes feed straight into this year: number work → Ch 1 · algebra → Ch 2–5 · triangles and Pythagoras → Ch 6–9 · circles and mensuration → Ch 10–12 · data handling → Ch 13–14.
