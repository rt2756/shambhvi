# 📖 Syllabus map — Science (Class 10)

<!-- Chapter list verified against ncert_books/class_10/science.pdf (NCERT, rationalised edition — 13 chapters). -->

The whole year at a glance — NCERT **Class 10 Science**, the board-exam year (13 chapters, three streams woven together).

**Every chapter has full notes and a matching practice set.**

## Chemistry

1. ✅ **Chemical Reactions and Equations** — writing and balancing equations; the main types of reaction.
2. ✅ **Acids, Bases and Salts** — the Class 7 acid/base story made quantitative: the pH scale and important salts.
3. ✅ **Metals and Non-metals** — properties, reactivity series, and how metals are extracted.
4. ✅ **Carbon and its Compounds** — why carbon makes so many compounds; chains, bonds and functional groups.

## Biology

5. ✅ **Life Processes** — nutrition, respiration, transport and excretion: what keeps a body running.
6. ✅ **Control and Coordination** — nerves and hormones as the body's messaging systems.
7. ✅ **How do Organisms Reproduce?** — asexual and sexual reproduction across the living world.
8. ✅ **Heredity** — how traits pass from parents to offspring; Mendel's rules.

## Physics

9. ✅ **Light — Reflection and Refraction** — mirrors and lenses, image formation and the ray rules.
10. ✅ **The Human Eye and the Colourful World** — how the eye focuses, its defects, and why the sky is blue.
11. ✅ **Electricity** — current, potential difference, resistance and Ohm's law (Class 7's circuits, now with numbers).
12. ✅ **Magnetic Effects of Electric Current** — magnetic fields, motors and induction.

## Environment

13. ✅ **Our Environment** — ecosystems, food chains, and the human impact on them.
