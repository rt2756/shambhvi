The whole year at a glance — NCERT's **Ganita Prakash** for Class 8 comes in two parts. The chapters are numbered **1–14 straight through** here, so Part II's Chapter 1 appears as Chapter 8.

**Every chapter has full notes and a matching practice set.**

## Part I

1. ✅ **A Square and A Cube** — square numbers and cube numbers, and the roots that undo them.
2. ✅ **Power Play** — exponents: short, unambiguous ways to write and read very large numbers.
3. ✅ **A Story of Numbers** — how the idea of *writing numbers* evolved across the world into our modern place-value system.
4. ✅ **Quadrilaterals** — special four-sided figures, their properties and problems built on them.
5. ✅ **Number Play** — number properties through puzzles: sums of consecutive numbers, multiples, visual proofs.
6. ✅ **We Distribute, Yet Things Multiply** — algebra and the **distributive law**: using letters to prove claims, not just state them.
7. ✅ **Proportional Reasoning — 1** — comparing quantities the multiplicative way: ratios and proportion in daily life.

## Part II

8. ✅ **Fractions in Disguise** — **percentages** and their many applications (a fraction wearing a costume).
9. ✅ **The Baudhāyana–Pythagoras Theorem** — the relationship between the side lengths of a right triangle — with its Indian history.
10. ✅ **Proportional Reasoning — 2** — continues Chapter 7: pie charts and **inverse** proportion.
11. ✅ **Exploring Some Geometric Themes** — fractals (self-similar shapes) and ways of visualising 3D solids.
12. ✅ **Tales by Dots and Lines** — a fresh look at the **arithmetic mean**, plus line graphs for visualising data.
13. ✅ **Algebra Play** — modelling situations with algebra; explaining number tricks by proving why they work.
14. ✅ **Area** — deeper into areas of triangles and quadrilaterals.

> [!TIP]
> Part I Ch 1–2 grow straight out of Class 7's squares/cubes and exponents chapters; Part II Ch 1–2 grow out of comparing quantities and triangles. The ladder is continuous.
