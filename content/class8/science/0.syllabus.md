# 📖 Syllabus map — Curiosity (Class 8)

<!-- Chapter list verified against ncert_books/class_8/science.pdf (NCERT Curiosity, 2025-26). -->

The whole year at a glance — NCERT's **Curiosity** (Class 8), the next step after the Class 7 book.

**Every chapter has full notes and a matching practice set.**

1. ✅ **Exploring the Investigative World of Science** — how scientists investigate (intro chapter).
2. ✅ **The Invisible Living World: Beyond Our Naked Eye** — microorganisms: the living world the microscope opened up.
3. ✅ **Health: The Ultimate Treasure** — staying healthy: nutrition, hygiene, disease and prevention.
4. ✅ **Electricity: Magnetic and Heating Effects** — what a current *does*: heating wires and making magnets (continues Class 7's circuits).
5. ✅ **Exploring Forces** — pushes, pulls, friction and the ways forces act.
6. ✅ **Pressure, Winds, Storms, and Cyclones** — air pressure and the weather it drives.
7. ✅ **Particulate Nature of Matter** — matter is made of particles — the idea behind solids, liquids and gases.
8. ✅ **Nature of Matter: Elements, Compounds, and Mixtures** — the chemist's three families of stuff.
9. ✅ **The Amazing World of Solutes, Solvents, and Solutions** — what dissolves, in what, and how much.
10. ✅ **Light: Mirrors and Lenses** — reflection and refraction: how images form.
11. ✅ **Keeping Time with the Skies** — day, month and year: timekeeping from the Sun and Moon.
12. ✅ **How Nature Works in Harmony** — ecosystems: living and non-living working together.
13. ✅ **Our Home: Earth, a Unique Life Sustaining Planet** — why Earth, alone among planets we know, supports life.
