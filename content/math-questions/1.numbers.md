Calculate **4 − 9**.

> [!ANSWER]
> **Mix.** `+` on the left: `4 − 9`.
>
> **Smaller − Larger** → do `9 − 4 = 5`, then put a `−` at the end → **−5**.

At dawn the temperature was **−4°C**. By afternoon it had risen by **11°C**, and by night it fell by **6°C**. What was the night temperature?

> [!ANSWER]
> Start `−4`, rise `+11`, fall `−6`.
>
> **Step 1:** `−4 + 11` → `+` on the left `11 − 4` → **7** (Larger − Smaller).
>
> **Step 2:** `7 − 6` → **Larger − Smaller** → **1**.
>
> Night temperature = **1°C**.

A diver is **15 m below sea level (−15 m)**. She rises **9 m**. What is her new depth?

> [!ANSWER]
> Below sea level is negative: `−15 + 9`.
>
> **Mix** → `+` on the left `9 − 15` → **Smaller − Larger** → `15 − 9 = 6`, then `−` → **−6**.
>
> New depth = **−6 m** (6 m below sea level).

A bank account has **₹200**. A payment of **₹350** is made from it. What is the new balance?

> [!ANSWER]
> Money going out is subtraction: `200 − 350`.
>
> **Mix** → `+` on the left `200 − 350` → **Smaller − Larger** → `350 − 200 = 150`, then `−` → **−150**.
>
> Balance = **−₹150** (₹150 overdrawn).

The temperature falls **3°C every hour**. How much does it change over **5 hours**?

> [!ANSWER]
> A fall each hour is `−3`, for `5` hours: `(−3) × 5`.
>
> **Size:** `3 × 5 = 15`. **Sign:** **1 minus** → odd → **−**.
>
> Change = **−15°C** (15°C colder).

A shopkeeper makes a **loss of ₹240**, shared **equally among 4 partners**. What is each partner's share?

> [!ANSWER]
> A loss is `−240`, split between `4`: `(−240) ÷ 4`.
>
> **Size:** `240 ÷ 4 = 60`. **Sign:** **1 minus** → odd → **−**.
>
> Each share = **−₹60** (a loss of ₹60 each).

A diver descends at **8 m per minute**. Taking depth below the surface as negative, what is her position after **6 minutes**?

> [!ANSWER]
> Going down each minute is `−8`, for `6` minutes: `(−8) × 6`.
>
> **Size:** `8 × 6 = 48`. **Sign:** **1 minus** → odd → **−**.
>
> Position = **−48 m** (48 m below the surface).
