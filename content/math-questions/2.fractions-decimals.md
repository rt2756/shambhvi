<!-- ===== Place value & expanded form ===== -->

In the number **4.73**, what is the **place value of the 7**?

> [!ANSWER]
> The first place to the **right** of the decimal point is **tenths**.
>
> So the 7 means **7 tenths = 7/10**.
>
> **Why:** every step right of the point divides by 10 — ones → tenths → hundredths → …

---

Write **6.259** in **expanded form**.

> [!ANSWER]
> Read the places right of the point: 2 = tenths, 5 = hundredths, 9 = thousandths.
>
> `6.259 = 6 + 2/10 + 5/100 + 9/1000`

---

Write **4 + 7/10 + 3/100** as a single decimal.

> [!ANSWER]
> 4 ones, 7 tenths, 3 hundredths → fill each place in order:
>
> **4.73**

---

What is the **place value of the 8** in **12.048**?

> [!ANSWER]
> Count the places right of the point: 0 = tenths, 4 = hundredths, **8 = thousandths**.
>
> So the 8 means **8 thousandths = 8/1000**.

<!-- ===== Comparing decimals ===== -->

Which is greater, **0.5** or **0.45**?

> [!ANSWER]
> **Don't count digits.** Line up the points and add a helper zero: write `0.5` as `0.50`.
>
> Now compare **50 hundredths** with **45 hundredths** → `0.50 > 0.45`, so **0.5 is greater**.
>
> **Why the zero is safe:** a zero at the *end* of a decimal doesn't change its value (`0.5 = 0.50`).

---

Which is greater, **0.7** or **0.68**?

> [!ANSWER]
> Helper zero: write `0.7` as `0.70`.
>
> `70` hundredths vs `68` hundredths → `0.70 > 0.68`, so **0.7 is greater**.

---

Put the correct sign (`<` or `>`): **2.5 ___ 2.45**

> [!ANSWER]
> Whole parts are equal (both 2), so compare the decimal parts. Write `2.5` as `2.50`.
>
> `50 > 45` → **2.5 > 2.45**

---

Arrange in **ascending order**: **0.3, 0.03, 0.33, 0.303**

> [!ANSWER]
> Give them all the **same number of decimal places** with helper zeros (thousandths):
>
> `0.300, 0.030, 0.330, 0.303`
>
> Compare as whole numbers of thousandths: `030 < 300 < 303 < 330`.
>
> Ascending → **0.03, 0.3, 0.303, 0.33**

---

<!-- ===== Multiplying decimals ===== -->

Find **3.45 × 10**.

> [!ANSWER]
> **× 10** → slide the point **right** by **1** place (one zero):
>
> `3.45 → ` **34.5**

---

Find **0.5 × 1000**.

> [!ANSWER]
> **× 1000** → slide the point **right** by **3** places (three zeros), filling with zeros:
>
> `0.5 → 5. → 50. → ` **500**

---

Find **1.2 × 0.3**.

> [!ANSWER]
> **Multiply ignoring the points:** `12 × 3 = 36`.
>
> **Count decimal digits in both numbers:** `1.2` has 1, `0.3` has 1 → `1 + 1 = 2` digits in the answer.
>
> Place the point so there are 2 decimal digits → **0.36**.

---

Find **2.5 × 1.25**.

> [!ANSWER]
> Ignore the points: `25 × 125 = 3125`.
>
> Decimal digits: `2.5` has 1, `1.25` has 2 → `1 + 2 = 3` digits.
>
> Point so there are 3 decimal digits → **3.125**.

---

Find **0.4 × 0.6**.

> [!ANSWER]
> Ignore the points: `4 × 6 = 24`.
>
> Decimal digits: `0.4` has 1, `0.6` has 1 → `2` digits → **0.24**.
>
> (Note it's *smaller* than both — see the "less than 1" questions below.)

---

Apples cost **₹48.50 per kg**. What do **3 kg** cost?

> [!ANSWER]
> `48.50 × 3`. Ignore the point: `4850 × 3 = 14550`.
>
> `48.50` has 2 decimal digits → the answer has 2 → **145.50**.
>
> Cost = **₹145.50**.

<!-- ===== Dividing decimals ===== -->

Find **31.5 ÷ 10**.

> [!ANSWER]
> **÷ 10** → slide the point **left** by **1** place:
>
> `31.5 → ` **3.15**

---

Find **31.5 ÷ 100**.

> [!ANSWER]
> **÷ 100** → slide the point **left** by **2** places (fill with a zero):
>
> `31.5 → 3.15 → ` **0.315**

---

Find **8.4 ÷ 4**.

> [!ANSWER]
> Dividing by a **whole number**: divide normally, and the decimal point goes **straight up**.
>
> `84 ÷ 4 = 21`, point straight up → **2.1**

---

Find **7.75 ÷ 0.25**.

> [!ANSWER]
> Dividing by a **decimal**: slide **both** points right until the divisor is a whole number. Move 2 places each:
>
> `7.75 ÷ 0.25 = 775 ÷ 25 = ` **31**

---

Find **4.5 ÷ 0.5**.

> [!ANSWER]
> Slide **both** points right by 1 so the divisor is whole:
>
> `4.5 ÷ 0.5 = 45 ÷ 5 = ` **9**

---

**6 friends** share **₹27.60** equally. How much does each get?

> [!ANSWER]
> Equal sharing is division by a whole number: `27.60 ÷ 6`, point straight up.
>
> `2760 ÷ 6 = 460` → **4.60**
>
> Each gets **₹4.60**.

---

<!-- ===== ×/÷ by a number less than 1 ===== -->

Without full working, is **8 × 0.5** **bigger or smaller** than 8? Then find it.

> [!ANSWER]
> `0.5` is **less than 1**, so multiplying by it makes the answer **smaller**.
>
> `8 × 0.5 = ` **4** — and 4 is indeed smaller than 8. ✓
>
> **Why:** `× 0.5` means "take half of," which shrinks the number.

---

Is **8 ÷ 0.5** **bigger or smaller** than 8? Then find it.

> [!ANSWER]
> Dividing by a number **less than 1** makes the answer **bigger**.
>
> `8 ÷ 0.5 = 80 ÷ 5 = ` **16** — bigger than 8. ✓
>
> **Why:** "how many halves fit in 8?" — twice as many as wholes, so the answer grows.
