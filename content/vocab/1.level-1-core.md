# Level 1 — Everyday words to own now

---

### curious

*adjective*

> [!ANSWER]
> Wanting to know or learn about something.
> *Example:* She was so **curious** about the locked room that she couldn't stop asking questions.
> *Word family:* curiosity, curiously.

---

### generous

*adjective*

> [!ANSWER]
> Happy to give or share more than is expected.
> *Example:* It was **generous** of him to share his lunch with the new student.
> *Opposite:* selfish, stingy.

---

### fragile

*adjective*

> [!ANSWER]
> Easily broken or damaged.
> *Example:* Handle the glass ornaments carefully — they are very **fragile**.
> *Opposite:* sturdy, tough.

---

### ancient

*adjective*

> [!ANSWER]
> Belonging to the very distant past; extremely old.
> *Example:* The **ancient** fort had stood for over a thousand years.
> *Opposite:* modern, recent.

---

### gradual

*adjective*

> [!ANSWER]
> Happening slowly, in small steps.
> *Example:* There was a **gradual** improvement in her handwriting over the year.
> *Opposite:* sudden, abrupt.

---

### sincere

*adjective*

> [!ANSWER]
> Honest and truly meant; not pretending.
> *Example:* He gave a **sincere** apology and really meant it.
> *Word family:* sincerely, sincerity.

---

### weary

*adjective*

> [!ANSWER]
> Very tired, especially after effort.
> *Example:* The **weary** travellers fell asleep the moment they sat down.
> *Similar:* exhausted, fatigued.

---

### vivid

*adjective*

> [!ANSWER]
> Very bright and clear; easy to picture in the mind.
> *Example:* She painted a **vivid** sunset in shades of orange and pink.
> *Similar:* bright, striking.

---

### cautious

*adjective*

> [!ANSWER]
> Careful to avoid danger or mistakes.
> *Example:* Be **cautious** while crossing the busy road.
> *Word family:* caution, cautiously.

---

### reliable

*adjective*

> [!ANSWER]
> Able to be trusted or depended on.
> *Example:* My friend is **reliable** — she always keeps her promises.
> *Opposite:* unreliable.

---

### eager

*adjective*

> [!ANSWER]
> Very keen and excited to do something.
> *Example:* The puppy was **eager** to go for a walk and wagged its tail.
> *Similar:* keen, enthusiastic.

---

### scarce

*adjective*

> [!ANSWER]
> Not easy to find; not enough to meet the need.
> *Example:* Clean water becomes **scarce** during a long dry season.
> *Opposite:* plentiful, abundant.
