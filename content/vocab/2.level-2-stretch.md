# Level 2 — Stretch words

---

### immense

*adjective*

> [!ANSWER]
> Extremely large in size or amount.
> *Example:* From the hilltop, the **immense** valley stretched as far as she could see.
> *Similar:* vast, enormous.

---

### remarkable

*adjective*

> [!ANSWER]
> So unusual or impressive that it is worth noticing.
> *Example:* Solving the puzzle in one minute was a **remarkable** feat.
> *Similar:* extraordinary, striking.

---

### reluctant

*adjective*

> [!ANSWER]
> Unwilling; hesitating to do something.
> *Example:* He was **reluctant** to lend his new bicycle to anyone.
> *Opposite:* willing, eager.

---

### abundant

*adjective*

> [!ANSWER]
> Present in large amounts; more than enough.
> *Example:* After the good monsoon, food was **abundant** in the village.
> *Opposite:* scarce.

---

### courageous

*adjective*

> [!ANSWER]
> Brave; willing to face danger or difficulty.
> *Example:* The **courageous** firefighter rushed in to save the trapped kitten.
> *Word family:* courage, courageously.

---

### fascinate

*verb*

> [!ANSWER]
> To attract and hold someone's interest completely.
> *Example:* Stories about space **fascinate** her, and she reads about them for hours.
> *Word family:* fascinating, fascination.

---

### anxious

*adjective*

> [!ANSWER]
> Worried or nervous about what might happen.
> *Example:* She felt **anxious** before her first stage performance.
> *Similar:* worried, uneasy.

---

### persuade

*verb*

> [!ANSWER]
> To make someone agree by giving good reasons.
> *Example:* He managed to **persuade** his parents to let him join the trip.
> *Word family:* persuasion, persuasive.

---

### peculiar

*adjective*

> [!ANSWER]
> Strange or unusual in a way that stands out.
> *Example:* There was a **peculiar** smell coming from the science lab.
> *Similar:* odd, curious.

---

### mischievous

*adjective*

> [!ANSWER]
> Playfully causing small trouble or mischief.
> *Example:* The **mischievous** monkey snatched the tourist's cap and ran off.
> *Word family:* mischief.

---

### gratitude

*noun*

> [!ANSWER]
> The feeling of being thankful.
> *Example:* She wrote a letter full of **gratitude** to her teacher.
> *Similar:* thankfulness, appreciation.

---

### hesitate

*verb*

> [!ANSWER]
> To pause before acting because you are unsure.
> *Example:* Don't **hesitate** to ask for help if a question is hard.
> *Word family:* hesitation, hesitant.
