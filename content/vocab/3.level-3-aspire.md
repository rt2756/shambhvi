# Level 3 — Aspire words

---

### inevitable

*adjective*

> [!ANSWER]
> Certain to happen; impossible to avoid.
> *Example:* With dark clouds gathering, rain seemed **inevitable**.
> *Similar:* unavoidable, certain.

---

### meticulous

*adjective*

> [!ANSWER]
> Very careful and precise about small details.
> *Example:* She kept **meticulous** notes, neatly labelling every diagram.
> *Similar:* thorough, careful.

---

### resilient

*adjective*

> [!ANSWER]
> Able to recover quickly from difficulty.
> *Example:* He is **resilient** — after losing the match, he trained even harder.
> *Word family:* resilience.

---

### benevolent

*adjective*

> [!ANSWER]
> Kind, generous, and wishing to do good.
> *Example:* The **benevolent** king built schools and hospitals for the poor.
> *Opposite:* cruel, selfish.

---

### diligent

*adjective*

> [!ANSWER]
> Hardworking and careful in your work.
> *Example:* A **diligent** student revises a little every day instead of all at once.
> *Word family:* diligence, diligently.

---

### eloquent

*adjective*

> [!ANSWER]
> Speaking or writing fluently and convincingly.
> *Example:* Her **eloquent** speech moved the whole audience.
> *Word family:* eloquence.

---

### profound

*adjective*

> [!ANSWER]
> Very deep — in meaning, thought, or feeling.
> *Example:* The book had a **profound** effect on how she saw the world.
> *Similar:* deep, intense.

---

### scrutinize

*verb*

> [!ANSWER]
> To examine something very closely and carefully.
> *Example:* The detective **scrutinized** every clue before deciding.
> *Word family:* scrutiny.

---

### candid

*adjective*

> [!ANSWER]
> Honest and straightforward, even about difficult things.
> *Example:* She was **candid** about her mistake and promised to fix it.
> *Similar:* frank, honest.

---

### prudent

*adjective*

> [!ANSWER]
> Wise and careful when making decisions.
> *Example:* It is **prudent** to save a little money for emergencies.
> *Word family:* prudence.
