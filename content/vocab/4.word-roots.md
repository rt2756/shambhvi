# Word roots — learn one, unlock many

---

### -bio- &nbsp; (life)

> [!ANSWER]
> From Greek *bios* = life.
> *Words:* **biology** (study of life), **biography** (story of someone's life), **biosphere** (zone of life on Earth).
> *Example:* A **biography** tells the true story of a person's life.

---

### -geo- &nbsp; (earth)

> [!ANSWER]
> From Greek *gē* = earth.
> *Words:* **geography** (writing about the earth), **geology** (study of rocks/earth), **geometry** (measuring the earth — shapes & space).
> *Example:* In **geography** we learn about mountains, rivers, and climates.

---

### -tele- &nbsp; (far, distant)

> [!ANSWER]
> From Greek *tēle* = far off.
> *Words:* **telephone** (sound from afar), **television** (seeing from afar), **telescope** (seeing far things).
> *Example:* A **telescope** lets you see stars that are very far away.

---

### -port- &nbsp; (carry)

> [!ANSWER]
> From Latin *portare* = to carry.
> *Words:* **transport** (carry across), **export** (carry out of a country), **portable** (able to be carried).
> *Example:* A **portable** speaker is small and easy to carry around.

---

### -dict- &nbsp; (say, speak)

> [!ANSWER]
> From Latin *dicere* = to say.
> *Words:* **dictionary** (book of what words say), **predict** (say before), **dictate** (say aloud to be written).
> *Example:* Weather scientists try to **predict** — say beforehand — if it will rain.

---

### -spect- &nbsp; (look)

> [!ANSWER]
> From Latin *spectare* = to look.
> *Words:* **inspect** (look into), **spectator** (one who looks on), **respect** (look at with regard).
> *Example:* A **spectator** is someone who watches a game.

---

### -aqua- &nbsp; (water)

> [!ANSWER]
> From Latin *aqua* = water.
> *Words:* **aquarium** (water tank for fish), **aquatic** (living in water), **aqueduct** (channel that carries water).
> *Example:* Fish, frogs, and turtles are **aquatic** animals.

---

### -graph- / -graphy- &nbsp; (write, draw)

> [!ANSWER]
> From Greek *graphein* = to write or draw.
> *Words:* **autograph** (self-written signature), **paragraph** (a written block of text), **photograph** (drawn with light).
> *Example:* She asked the cricketer for his **autograph**.

---

### -vis- / -vid- &nbsp; (see)

> [!ANSWER]
> From Latin *videre* = to see.
> *Words:* **visible** (able to be seen), **vision** (the act of seeing), **video** (moving pictures to see), **evident** (clearly seen).
> *Example:* On a clear night, thousands of stars are **visible**.

---

### pre- &nbsp; (before) — *prefix*

> [!ANSWER]
> A prefix meaning *before*.
> *Words:* **preview** (see before), **prepare** (get ready before), **predict** (say before).
> *Example:* A movie **preview** shows you scenes before the film releases.

---

### trans- &nbsp; (across) — *prefix*

> [!ANSWER]
> A prefix meaning *across* or *through*.
> *Words:* **transport** (carry across), **translate** (carry meaning across languages), **transfer** (move across).
> *Example:* To **translate** is to carry a sentence across from one language to another.

---

### -able &nbsp; (able to be) — *suffix*

> [!ANSWER]
> A suffix meaning *able to be* or *capable of*.
> *Words:* **readable** (able to be read), **portable** (able to be carried), **comfortable** (giving comfort).
> *Example:* Her neat handwriting is very **readable**.
