# 📖 Syllabus map — Exploration (Class 9)

<!-- Chapter list verified against ncert_books/class_9/science.pdf (NCERT "Exploration", new edition). -->

The whole year at a glance — NCERT's **Exploration** (Class 9), where the *Curiosity* series hands over to secondary science.

1. **Exploration: Entering the World of Secondary Science** — how science gets more precise from here (intro chapter).
2. **Cell: The Building Block of Life** — every living thing is built from cells, and what's inside one.
3. **Tissues in Action** — cells of the same job working as a team, in plants and animals.
4. **Describing Motion Around Us** — distance, displacement, speed, velocity, acceleration and their graphs.
5. **Exploring Mixtures and their Separation** — solutions, suspensions, colloids and how to separate each.
6. **How Forces Affect Motion** — Newton's laws: what a force actually *does* to movement.
7. **Work, Energy, and Simple Machines** — work in the physics sense, forms of energy, and machines that trade force for distance.
8. **Journey Inside the Atom** — protons, neutrons, electrons and how they're arranged.
9. **Atomic Foundations of Matter** — atoms and molecules: the counting rules behind chemistry.
10. **Sound Waves: Characteristics and Applications** — how sound travels, pitch, loudness, echo and ultrasound.
11. **Reproduction: How Life Continues** — how living things make more of their kind.
12. **Patterns in Life: Diversity and Classification** — sorting the living world into groups that reflect real relationships.
13. **Earth as a System: Energy, Matter, and Life** — Earth's spheres and cycles working as one connected system.
