# 📖 Syllabus map — Mathematics (Class 9)

<!-- Chapter list verified against ncert_books/class_9/maths.pdf (NCERT, new 2026 edition — 8 chapters). -->

The whole year at a glance — the **new NCERT Class 9 Mathematics** (2026 edition, 8 chapters).

1. **Orienting Yourself: The Use of Coordinates** — locating points with number pairs: the coordinate plane.
2. **Introduction to Linear Polynomials** — expressions of the form ax + b, and what their graphs look like.
3. **The World of Numbers** — the full number family, including numbers that *aren't* fractions (irrationals like √2).
4. **Exploring Algebraic Identities** — the famous expansions ((a+b)², (a−b)², a²−b²…) and why they always work.
5. **I'm Up and Down, and Round and Round** — the geometry of **circles**: the shapes nature draws everywhere.
6. **Measuring Space: Perimeter and Area** — perimeter and area at full depth, for triangles and beyond.
7. **The Mathematics of Maybe: Introduction to Probability** — chance made precise (grows out of Class 7's probability).
8. **Predicting What Comes Next: Exploring Sequences and Progressions** — patterns that continue forever, and the rules that generate them.

> [!NOTE]
> Class 9 switched to a new NCERT book in 2026 — older guides describe a 12-chapter book (Euclid, Heron's formula, surface areas…). This map follows the **current** book on the shelf.
