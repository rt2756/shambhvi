# 🎸 Week 5 — Power chords and palm muting

**Goal:** play **power chords** on the low strings, move them around the neck, **palm-mute** them, and
use a little **distortion** for the first time. Learn the G major scale shape.

**Read first:** theory lessons **8** (the major scale) and **9** (how chords are built).

## 🎚️ Tune (3 min)

All six strings, finishing each one by tuning **up** to the note.

## 🔥 Warm-up (5 min)

1. Finger stretches.
2. One-minute changes **Am ↔ E** — write the number down.

## 🛠️ Technique (10 min)

### A. Your first power chords — 3 min

**Finger 1** on the root (low E string), **finger 3** on the A string, **2 frets higher**. Pick only those
two strings.

```
   E5         G5         A5
e|-----|    |-----|    |-----|
B|-----|    |-----|    |-----|
G|-----|    |-----|    |-----|
D|-----|    |-----|    |-----|
A|-2---|    |-5---|    |-7---|
E|-0---|    |-3---|    |-5---|
```

(E5 uses the open low E as its root, so only finger 1 on the A string, fret 2.)

**Why only two strings?** A power chord is just root + 5th (lesson 9). Any other string ringing adds a
note that doesn't belong. Let the side of finger 1 rest lightly on the D string to keep it quiet.

### B. Turn on a little distortion — now you've earned it

Switch to the **bridge pickup**, turn the amp's **gain** up to about halfway, and play G5.

**Why only now?** Distortion makes everything louder and longer — including mistakes and stray strings.
After four weeks of clean playing, your fingers are tidy enough that distortion shows off the chord
instead of the mess. And power chords are built for it: the 3 : 2 ratio stays clear under distortion (lesson 9).

> [!WARNING]
> Keep the **amp volume** sensible. Distortion sounds louder than it is, and your ears are worth more
> than the sound.

### C. Palm muting — 4 min

Rest the **side of your picking hand** (the fleshy edge, below the little finger) **on the strings, right
where they meet the bridge**. Pick E5 with **all down-strokes**, eighth notes, at **60 BPM**:

```
   PM - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -|
e|---------|---------|---------|---------|
B|---------|---------|---------|---------|
G|---------|---------|---------|---------|
D|---------|---------|---------|---------|
A|-2-2-2-2-|-5-5-5-5-|-7-7-7-7-|-5-5-5-5-|
E|-0-0-0-0-|-3-3-3-3-|-5-5-5-5-|-3-3-3-3-|
    E5         G5        A5        G5
```

Count "**1 & 2 &**" — two chugs per click. Play the riff four times through.

**Why does muting sound "chunky"?** Your palm stops the strings ringing long and soaks up the bright
high overtones. You hear a short, low, punchy note — the sound of almost all rock rhythm guitar.

> [!TIP]
> Slide the palm forward until the note goes dead, then back until it **just** has a pitch again.
> That sweet spot moves when you change chords — keep the hand anchored on the bridge.

### D. Moving the shape — 3 min

The shape never changes; only the fret does. The **fret of the root names the chord** — use the low E
string notes from lesson 3:

| Root fret (low E) | 0 | 1 | 3 | 5 | 7 | 8 | 10 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Power chord | E5 | F5 | G5 | A5 | B5 | C5 | D5 |

Call out a chord name, then find and play it. Do 10.

## 🎵 Notes & tunes (7 min)

### E. The G major scale — 3 min

```
e|-----------------|
B|-----------------|
G|-----------------|
D|-----------2-4-5-|
A|-----2-3-5-------|
E|-3-5-------------|
   G A B C D E F♯G
```

Up and down, **alternate picking**, one note per click at 60 BPM. Say the note names on the way up and
**Sa Re Ga Ma Pa Dha Ni Sa** on the way down.

### F. *Frère Jacques* — 4 min

A round (one person starts, the next begins 2 bars later). Back to the clean sound for this one.

```
Frère Jacques, Frère Jacques,    Dormez-vous? Dormez-vous?
e|-----0---|-----0---|-0-1-3-|-0-1-3-|
B|-1-3---1-|-1-3---1-|-------|-------|
G|---------|---------|-------|-------|
D|---------|---------|-------|-------|
A|---------|---------|-------|-------|
E|---------|---------|-------|-------|

Sonnez les matines, sonnez les matines,    Ding dang dong, ding dang dong
e|-3-5-3-1-0---|-3-5-3-1-0---|-------|-------|
B|-----------1-|-----------1-|-1---1-|-1---1-|
G|-------------|-------------|---0---|---0---|
D|-------------|-------------|-------|-------|
A|-------------|-------------|-------|-------|
E|-------------|-------------|-------|-------|
```

Fret 5 on the high e: use **finger 4**, stretching from your normal place.

**Why does a round work?** The tune is built from the C chord's notes (C, E, G) at the strong beats, so
when two people play different bars at the same time, their notes still fit together.

## 🎉 Fun (5 min)

Distortion on. Make up your own riff using only E5, G5 and A5, palm-muted and open. Try ending on E5.
