# 🎸 Week 2 — Up and down, across the strings

**Goal:** pick **down and up** smoothly, move the spider down to frets 1–4, learn the notes on
the A string, and play two tunes that cross three strings.

**Read first:** theory lesson **2** (the musical alphabet). Look back at lessons 3 and 4.

## 🎚️ Tune (3 min)

Same as Week 1 — all six strings, always finishing by tuning **up** to the note.

## 🔥 Warm-up (5 min)

1. Finger stretches, as in Week 1.
2. The Week 1 spider (frets 5–8) across all six strings, once, at 60 BPM.

## 🛠️ Technique (10 min)

### A. Alternate picking — 4 min

Now pick **down, up, down, up** (written **⊓ V ⊓ V**). Two notes per metronome click, at
**60 BPM**. Count "**1 & 2 & 3 & 4 &**" — down on the number, up on the "&".

```
e|-0-0-0-0-0-0-0-0-|-----------------|
B|-----------------|-0-0-0-0-0-0-0-0-|
G|-----------------|-----------------|
D|-----------------|-----------------|
A|-----------------|-----------------|
E|-----------------|-----------------|
```

Do it on every string, thin to thick and back.

**Why alternate?** With down-strokes only, the pick has to travel back up *without* playing —
a wasted move. Alternate picking uses **both** directions, so you can play twice as many notes
with the same hand movement. Almost all fast guitar playing is built on it.

> [!TIP]
> Down on the beat, up between beats — always. If you keep that rule, your picking hand
> becomes a clock, and your rhythm gets steady on its own.

### B. The spider, now at frets 1–4 — 6 min

Same exercise, **one finger per fret**, but now at frets **1, 2, 3, 4** and with **alternate
picking**. Go up all six strings, then come back down **4-3-2-1**.

```
Going up:
e|---------|---------|---------|
B|---------|---------|---------|
G|---------|---------|---------|
D|---------|---------|-1-2-3-4-|
A|---------|-1-2-3-4-|---------|
E|-1-2-3-4-|---------|---------|

e|---------|---------|-1-2-3-4-|
B|---------|-1-2-3-4-|---------|
G|-1-2-3-4-|---------|---------|
D|---------|---------|---------|
A|---------|---------|---------|
E|---------|---------|---------|

Coming down:
e|-4-3-2-1-|---------|---------|
B|---------|-4-3-2-1-|---------|
G|---------|---------|-4-3-2-1-|
D|---------|---------|---------|
A|---------|---------|---------|
E|---------|---------|---------|

e|---------|---------|---------|
B|---------|---------|---------|
G|---------|---------|---------|
D|-4-3-2-1-|---------|---------|
A|---------|-4-3-2-1-|---------|
E|---------|---------|-4-3-2-1-|
```

Start at **60 BPM, one note per click**. When it is clean 3 times in a row, go up 5 BPM.

**Why come back down?** Going up, each new finger *adds* to the string. Coming down, each
finger has to *lift off* cleanly so the note below it sounds — a different skill. Lifting
neatly is what makes pull-offs and fast runs work later.

> [!WARNING]
> Frets 1–4 are wider than 5–8, so the stretch is bigger. If your hand hurts, go back to frets
> 5–8 for that day. Don't force the stretch — it comes with time.

## 🎵 Notes & tunes (7 min)

### C. Say the notes on the A string — 1 min

```
e|-------------------|
B|-------------------|
G|-------------------|
D|-------------------|
A|-0-2-3-5-7-8-10-12-|
E|-------------------|
```

A – B – C – D – E – F – G – A. Here the one-fret jumps (**B → C** and **E → F**) are in different
places from the low E string — but they are **the same two pairs**.

**Why do they move?** The string starts on a different letter, so the B–C and E–F pairs come up
at a different point in the count. The rule from lesson 2 never changes; only where you start
does.

### D. *Twinkle Twinkle Little Star* — 3 min

Three strings now: **G, B and high e**. Fret 1 → finger 1, fret 2 → finger 2, fret 3 → finger 3.

```
Twinkle twinkle little star, how I wonder what you are
e|---------0-0---|---------------|
B|-----3-3-----3-|-1-1-0-0-------|
G|-0-0-----------|---------2-2-0-|
D|---------------|---------------|
A|---------------|---------------|
E|---------------|---------------|

Up above the world so high, like a diamond in the sky
e|---------------|---------------|
B|-3-3-1-1-0-0---|-3-3-1-1-0-0---|
G|-------------2-|-------------2-|
D|---------------|---------------|
A|---------------|---------------|
E|---------------|---------------|
```

Then play the first line again to finish. The notes are **G G D D E E D · C C B B A A G**.

### E. *Ode to Joy* (Beethoven) — 3 min

```
e|-0-0-1-3-|-3-1-0---|-------0-|-0-----|
B|---------|-------3-|-1-1-3---|---3-3-|
G|---------|---------|---------|-------|
D|---------|---------|---------|-------|
A|---------|---------|---------|-------|
E|---------|---------|---------|-------|

e|-0-0-1-3-|-3-1-0---|-------0-|-------|
B|---------|-------3-|-1-1-3---|-3-1-1-|
G|---------|---------|---------|-------|
D|---------|---------|---------|-------|
A|---------|---------|---------|-------|
E|---------|---------|---------|-------|
```

Notes: **E E F G · G F E D · C C D E · E D D** — then the same again, ending **D C C**.
The last note of each line is **held longer** — listen to the tune once before you play it.

**Why listen first?** TAB shows *where* to play but not *how long* each note lasts (lesson 4).
Your ear fills in the rhythm until you learn to read it in lesson 5.

## 🎉 Fun (5 min)

Make up your own tune using only the **G, B and e strings, frets 0–3**. Try ending it on the
open G — it will sound "finished". (You will learn *why* in lesson 8.)
