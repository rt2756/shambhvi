# 🎸 Week 10 — Raags on the guitar

**Goal:** play **Raag Bhupali** and **Raag Yaman** with Sa = C, play **Alankar 2**, and play a short **Bhupali
tune** — while singing the sargam.

**Read first:** theory lesson **14** (sargam ↔ Western & Indian tunes).

## 🎚️ Tune (3 min)

All six strings, finishing each one by tuning **up** to the note.

## 🔥 Warm-up (5 min)

1. Finger stretches.
2. **Alankar 1** from Week 6, up and down, singing the sargam.

## 🛠️ Technique (10 min)

### A. Alankar 2 — jumping in 3rds — 5 min

**S G, R M, G P, M D, P N, D S'** — each pair skips one note. Sa = C, on the lesson 8 shape:

```
  SG    RM    GP    MD    PN    DS'
e|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
B|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
G|-----|-----|-----|---2-|---4-|-2-5-|
D|---2-|---3-|-2-5-|-3---|-5---|-----|
A|-3---|-5---|-----|-----|-----|-----|
E|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
```

And down: **S' D, N P, D M, P G, M R, G S**:

```
  S'D   NP    DM    PG    MR    GS
e|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
B|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
G|-5-2-|-4---|-2---|-----|-----|-----|
D|-----|---5-|---3-|-5-2-|-3---|-2---|
A|-----|-----|-----|-----|---5-|---3-|
E|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
```

**Why practise skips?** Tunes rarely move only step by step. Jumping a 3rd (lesson 7) trains your fingers to land
on a note **two places** away without sliding through the one in between — and your ear to hear that distance.

### B. Raag Yaman — aaroh and avroh — 5 min

```
e|---------------|-----------------|
B|---------------|-----------------|
G|---------2-4-5-|-5-4-2-----------|
D|-----2-4-------|-------5-4-2-----|
A|-2-5-----------|-------------5-3-|
E|---------------|-----------------|
```

Up: **,N R G M' D N S'** · Down: **S' N D P M' G R S**

D string fret 4 is **teevra Ma (F♯)** — the note that gives Yaman its glow.

**Why does teevra Ma sound "bright"?** Ma is normally a perfect 4th above Sa. Raising it by a half step makes it
lean upward toward Pa, a half step away — like the 7th leaning toward Sa (lesson 8). That upward lean is what
makes Yaman feel lifted and shining.

## 🎵 Notes & tunes (7 min)

### C. Raag Bhupali — aaroh and avroh — 2 min

**S R G P D S'** and back. Sa = C: C D E G A C.

```
e|-------------|-------------|
B|-------------|-------------|
G|---------2-5-|-5-2---------|
D|-----2-5-----|-----5-2-----|
A|-3-5---------|---------5-3-|
E|-------------|-------------|
```

Up: **S R G P D S'** · Down: **S' D P G R S**

### D. A short Bhupali tune — 5 min

A tune written for this page, using only Bhupali's five notes. Sing the sargam first, then play it.

```
 S R G    G P G R    S R G P    D P G
 S R G P  D P G R    S R G R    S ,D S
```

```
e|-------|---------|---------|-------|
B|-------|---------|---------|-------|
G|-------|---------|---------|-2-----|
D|-----2-|-2-5-2---|-----2-5-|---5-2-|
A|-3-5---|-------5-|-3-5-----|-------|
E|-------|---------|---------|-------|

e|---------|---------|---------|-------|
B|---------|---------|---------|-------|
G|-----2-5-|-2-------|---------|-------|
D|-2-5-----|---5-2---|-----2---|-------|
A|---------|-------5-|-3-5---5-|-3-0-3-|
E|---------|---------|---------|-------|
```

The last bar dips to **,D** — the lower Dha — on the **open A string**, then returns to Sa.

**Why does dipping below Sa and coming back sound so final?** You step away from home and return in a tiny loop —
your ear hears the return as the end of the journey. Many Bhupali phrases end exactly like this.

> [!TIP]
> Get someone to hold a **C5 power chord** (C + G = Sa + Pa) ringing while you play — that's your
> drone, like a tanpura holding Sa and Pa.

## 🎉 Fun (5 min)

Make up your own Bhupali tune: only S R G P D, start and end on Sa. Because there are no half steps (lesson 13),
almost anything you play will sound good.
