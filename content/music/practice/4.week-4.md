# 🎸 Week 4 — The E chord and up-strums

**Goal:** add the **E major** chord, change smoothly between Em, Am and E, add **up-strums** to your
rhythm, and play a tune in **3/4** time.

**Read first:** theory lessons **6** (time signatures & tempo) and **7** (intervals).

## 🎚️ Tune (3 min)

All six strings, finishing each one by tuning **up** to the note.

## 🔥 Warm-up (5 min)

1. Finger stretches.
2. One-minute changes **Em ↔ Am** (from Week 3) — write down the number.

## 🛠️ Technique (10 min)

### A. The E major chord — 3 min

```
   E (major)               Em (for comparison)
   E A D G B e             E A D G B e
   o       o o             o     o o o
   ===========             ===========
1  | | | 1 | |          1  | | | | | |
   -----------             -----------
2  | 2 3 | | |          2  | 2 3 | | |
   -----------             -----------
3  | | | | | |          3  | | | | | |
```

**E is just Em plus finger 1** on the G string, fret 1. Do the roll call (pick each string alone) until
all six ring.

**Why does one finger change sad to happy?** The open G string is the note **G**. Fret 1 makes it
**G♯**. From E, G is a **minor 3rd** (3 half steps) and G♯ is a **major 3rd** (4 half steps) —
lesson 7. That one half step is the whole difference between a minor (sad) and a major (bright) chord.
Lesson 9 explains this fully.

### B. Am → E: the same shape, one string over — 2 min

Look closely: **Am** and **E** use the **same finger shape**. For E, just move the whole shape
**one string toward the ceiling** (toward the thick strings).

| | Finger 1 | Finger 2 | Finger 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Am** | B string, fret 1 | D string, fret 2 | G string, fret 2 |
| **E** | G string, fret 1 | A string, fret 2 | D string, fret 2 |

Practise lifting the shape **as one piece** and putting it down one string over. One-minute changes
**Am ↔ E** — write the number down.

**Why does the same shape give a different chord?** The shape sets the **distances** between the
notes (lesson 7). Moved one string over, it starts from a new note — E instead of A. But in Am, finger 1
sits on the B string; in E, it sits on the G string. Crossing the **G–B gap** (only 4 half steps, not 5)
shifts that one note by a half step — and that half step is exactly what turns minor (A C E) into
major (E G♯ B).

### C. Up-strums: down, down-up — 5 min

New pattern at **60 BPM**. Down on the numbers, up on the "&" (lesson 5):

```
Count:   1       2   &   3       4   &
Strum:   ↓       ↓   ↑   ↓       ↓   ↑
```

Keep the hand moving **down-up all the time**, like a pendulum. On "1 &" and "3 &" the hand still
goes up, but **misses** the strings.

**Why keep moving on the missed strums?** If the hand stops and starts, the timing wobbles. A hand
that never stops lands every strum exactly on time — you only choose whether it touches the strings.

Play it on these progressions, one bar each:

```
| Em      | Am      | Em      | Am      |
| Am      | E       | Am      | E       |
| Em      | E       | Am      | E       |
```

> [!TIP]
> Up-strums only need to catch the **top 3 or 4 strings**. They're meant to be lighter than the
> down-strums — that's what gives the rhythm its bounce.

## 🎵 Notes & tunes (7 min)

### D. *Happy Birthday* — in 3/4 time

*Happy Birthday* is in **3/4**: count "**1** 2 3, **1** 2 3" (lesson 6). It **starts on beat 3** — the
first "Hap-py" is a pick-up before bar 1.

```
Happy birthday to you,            Happy birthday to you
e|-------------|-------------|
B|---------1-0-|---------3-1-|
G|-0-0-2-0-----|-0-0-2-0-----|
D|-------------|-------------|
A|-------------|-------------|
E|-------------|-------------|

Happy birthday dear ___,          Happy birthday to you!
e|-----3-0-------|-1-1-0-------|
B|---------1-0---|-------1-3-1-|
G|-0-0---------2-|-------------|
D|---------------|-------------|
A|---------------|-------------|
E|---------------|-------------|
```

Notes: **G G A G C B · G G A G D C · G G G E C B A · F F E C D C**.

**Why does "dear" jump so high?** It is the octave of the first note — G on the open G string, then
G again at fret 3 of the high e (lesson 7). A big jump like that makes the most important line stand out.

## 🎉 Fun (5 min)

Strum **E** loud with a little gain, then **Em**, then E again. Listen to the change from bright to
dark — that's one fret doing all of it.
