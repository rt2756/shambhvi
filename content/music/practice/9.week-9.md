# 🎸 Week 9 — Your first whole song

**Goal:** play a **complete song** from start to finish without stopping — *Amazing Grace*, in 3/4, with G, C
and D — and learn **hammer-ons and pull-offs**, the first lead-guitar moves.

**Read first:** look back at lessons **6** (3/4 time), **11** (keys) and **12** (the waltz strum).

## 🎚️ Tune (3 min)

All six strings, finishing each one by tuning **up** to the note.

## 🔥 Warm-up (5 min)

1. Finger stretches.
2. One-minute changes **G ↔ C** and **G ↔ D** — write the numbers down.

## 🛠️ Technique (10 min)

### A. Hammer-ons — 4 min

Pick fret 5, then **slam** finger 3 onto fret 7 on the same string — **don't pick again**.

```
e|-5h7-5h7-|
B|-5h7-5h7-|
G|-5h7-5h7-|
D|-5h7-5h7-|
A|-5h7-5h7-|
E|-5h7-5h7-|
```

Go across all six strings. Both notes should be **equally loud**.

**Why does slamming the finger make a sound?** Hitting the string hard against the fret makes it shake — a
tiny "pluck" with the fretting finger. The note at fret 7 then rings on its own.

### B. Pull-offs — 4 min

Put finger 1 on fret 5 **and** finger 3 on fret 7. Pick, then **flick** finger 3 off **sideways** (toward the floor)
so fret 5 sounds.

```
e|-7p5-7p5-|
B|-7p5-7p5-|
G|-7p5-7p5-|
D|-7p5-7p5-|
A|-7p5-7p5-|
E|-7p5-7p5-|
```

**Why flick sideways, not lift straight up?** Lifting straight up just stops the note. Flicking catches the string
on the way off — a tiny pluck — so the lower note rings. And finger 1 must already be in place, or the open
string sounds instead.

### C. Combine them — 2 min

```
e|-5h7p5-----|
B|-------8p5-|
G|-----------|
D|-----------|
A|-----------|
E|-----------|
```

Pick **once** per string: the hammer and pull do the rest. These notes are all from **Box 1** (lesson 13).

## 🎵 Notes & tunes (7 min)

### D. *Amazing Grace* — the whole song

A hymn from 1779, in **3/4**. Use the waltz strum **↓ . ↓ ↑ ↓ ↑** (lesson 12), one bar per chord. "A-" at the
start is the **pick-up** — sing it before you strum.

```
(A-)  ma-zing   grace how   sweet the   sound, that
      | G         | G         | C         | G         |

      saved a     wretch like me—         ——— I
      | G         | G         | D         | D         |

      once was    lost but    now am      found, was
      | G         | G         | C         | G         |

      blind but   now I       see.        ———
      | G         | D         | G         | G         |
```

Sing or hum the tune while you strum — or ask someone to sing.

> [!TIP]
> **The golden rule of playing a song: never stop.** If you miss a chord, keep the strumming hand moving and
> catch the **next** one. A listener barely notices a wrong chord, but they always notice the music stopping.

**Why keep going?** In real music, the beat doesn't wait. Practising "stop and fix" teaches your hands to stop
every time something goes wrong. Practising "keep going" teaches them to recover — which is what performing needs.
Fix the hard bar **separately** afterwards.

## 🎉 Fun (5 min)

Play *Amazing Grace* once **clean** on the neck pickup, then once with a little gain and the **everyday strum** in
4/4 — a different feel for the same chords. Which do you like better?
