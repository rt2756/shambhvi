# Measurements
## Length

- Millimetre (mm)
- Centimetre (cm)
  - **1 cm = 10 mm**
- Decimetre (dm)
  - **1 dm = 10 cm**
- Metre (m)
  - **1 m = 10 dm**
  - **1 m = 100 cm**
- Decametre (dam)
  - **1 dam = 10 m**
- Hectometre (hm)
  - **1hm = 10m**
- Kilometre (km)
  - **1 km = 1000 m**

## Weight

- Milligram (mg)
- Gram (g)
  - **1g = 1000 mg**
- Kilogram (kg)
  - **1kg = 1000 g**

## Capacity

- Millilitre (ml)
- Litre (L)
  - **1L = 1000ml**
