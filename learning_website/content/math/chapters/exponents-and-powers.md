# Exponents and Powers

$a^n$ = $a$ multiplied by itself $n$ times · $a$ = base, $n$ = exponent · $2^3=8$, $5^4=625$
**Signs:** $(+)^n$ → + · $(-)^n$ → even $n$ gives +, odd $n$ gives − · $(-2)^4=16$, $(-2)^3=-8$

## Laws (same base)

- Product: $a^m\times a^n=a^{m+n}$ · $2^3\times2^4=2^7$
- Quotient: $\dfrac{a^m}{a^n}=a^{m-n}$ · $\dfrac{5^7}{5^2}=5^5$
- Power of a power: $(a^m)^n=a^{mn}$ · $(3^2)^4=3^8$
- Power of a product: $(ab)^n=a^n b^n$
- Zero exponent: $a^0=1$ ($a\ne0$)

## Standard form

- $k\times10^n$, with $1\le k<10$ · e.g. $38{,}000{,}000=3.8\times10^7$
- Compare: bigger exponent wins; tie → compare $k$

**⚠️** $2^3=6$ ✗ (=8) · multiply → **add** exponents (not multiply) · $a^0=1$ not 0 · $(-3)^2=9$ not −9
