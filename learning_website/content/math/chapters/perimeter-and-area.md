# Perimeter and Area

**Perimeter** = distance around (length units) · **Area** = surface covered (square units)

## Formulas

- **Square** side $s$: P $=4s$ · A $=s^2$
- **Rectangle** $l,b$: P $=2(l+b)$ · A $=l\times b$
- **Parallelogram**: A $=b\times h$ ($h$ = perpendicular height)
- **Triangle**: A $=\frac12\times b\times h$
- **Circle** radius $r$: Circumference $=2\pi r$ · Area $=\pi r^2$ · $d=2r$ · $\pi\approx\frac{22}{7}\approx3.14$

## Unit conversion

- $1$ m $=100$ cm → $1$ m² $=10000$ cm²
- $1$ km² $=10^6$ m² · $1$ cm² $=100$ mm²

## Quick example

- Circle $r=35$ cm → A $=\frac{22}{7}\times35\times35=3850$ cm²

**⚠️** perimeter (cm) vs area (cm²) units · height must be **perpendicular**, not slant · don't drop the $\frac12$ in triangle area · in $\pi r^2$ use radius, $r=\frac{d}{2}$
