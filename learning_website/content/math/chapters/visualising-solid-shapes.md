# Visualising Solid Shapes

**2D** = flat (length, breadth) · **3D** = solid (length, breadth, height)
**Face** = flat surface · **Edge** = where 2 faces meet · **Vertex** = corner point

## Faces / Edges / Vertices

| Solid | F | E | V |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cube / Cuboid | 6 | 12 | 8 |
| Triangular prism | 5 | 9 | 6 |
| Square pyramid | 5 | 8 | 5 |
| Tetrahedron | 4 | 6 | 4 |
| Cylinder | 2 flat + 1 curved | 2 | 0 |
| Cone | 1 flat + 1 curved | 1 | 1 |
| Sphere | 1 curved | 0 | 0 |

## Nets & views

- **Net** = unfolded flat shape that folds into the solid (a cube has 11 nets)
- **Oblique sketch** = freehand · **Isometric** = on dot paper, true scale
- **Views**: top / front / side · cylinder upright → top circle, front rectangle

**⚠️** curved surface ≠ flat face · the line where faces meet = edge (not vertex) · net (unfolded) ≠ view (2D projection)
