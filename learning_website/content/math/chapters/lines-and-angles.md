# Lines and Angles

**Types:** acute $<90°$ · right $=90°$ · obtuse $90°$–$180°$ · straight $=180°$ · reflex $180°$–$360°$

![Angles diagram](/images/angles-diagram.png)

## Angle pairs

- **Complementary** sum $=90°$ · e.g. $35°+55°$
- **Supplementary** sum $=180°$ · e.g. $110°+70°$
- **Adjacent** = common vertex + common arm
- **Linear pair** = adjacent, outer arms form a straight line → supplementary
- **Vertically opposite** (two lines cross) → **equal** · e.g. $40°$ opposite $40°$

## Transversal cuts two ∥ lines

- **Corresponding** → equal
- **Alternate interior** → equal
- **Co-interior** (same side) → supplementary ($180°$)

![placeholder: two parallel lines cut by a transversal, 8 angles labeled](/images/lines-and-angles-1.png)

## Test for parallel

Lines are ∥ if **any one** holds: corresponding equal / alternate equal / co-interior sum $180°$

**⚠️** C before S → 90 before 180 · "linear pair" only if outer arms form a straight line · angle equalities hold **only** when the lines are parallel
