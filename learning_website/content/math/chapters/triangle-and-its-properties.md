# The Triangle and Its Properties

**By sides:** scalene (none equal) · isosceles (2 equal) · equilateral (all equal)
**By angles:** acute · right · obtuse

## Lines in a triangle

- **Median** = vertex → midpoint of opposite side · 3 medians meet at the centroid
- **Altitude** = perpendicular from a vertex to the opposite side

## Key properties

- **Angle sum** $=180°$ · e.g. $50°+70°+? \Rightarrow ?=60°$
- **Exterior angle** = sum of the two opposite interior angles · e.g. $50°+70°=120°$
- **Equilateral** → all angles $60°$ · **Isosceles** → angles opposite equal sides are equal
- **Triangle inequality:** sum of any two sides > third side · 3,4,8 ✗ ($3+4<8$) · 5,6,7 ✓

## Pythagoras (right triangle only)

- $a^2+b^2=c^2$, where $c$ = hypotenuse (longest side, opposite the right angle)
- e.g. $3^2+4^2=9+16=25=5^2$ · reverse also proves a right angle

**⚠️** angles always sum $180°$ · median ≠ altitude · Pythagoras only for right triangles · must **square**: $3+4\ne5$, it's $3^2+4^2=5^2$
