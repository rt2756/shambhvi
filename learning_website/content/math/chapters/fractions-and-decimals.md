# Fractions and Decimals

**Proper** num < den ($\frac{3}{5}$) · **Improper** num ≥ den ($\frac{7}{4}$) · **Mixed** whole+frac ($2\frac14$)
Improper ↔ Mixed: $\frac{11}{4}=2\frac34$ · $2\frac34=\frac{2\times4+3}{4}=\frac{11}{4}$

## Multiply

- Fraction × whole: $3\times\frac{2}{5}=\frac{6}{5}$
- Fraction × fraction: tops×tops, bottoms×bottoms → $\frac{2}{3}\times\frac{4}{5}=\frac{8}{15}$
- **"of" means ×** · e.g. $\frac12$ of 18 $=9$

## Divide

- Multiply by the **reciprocal** → $\frac{2}{3}\div\frac{4}{5}=\frac{2}{3}\times\frac{5}{4}=\frac{5}{6}$
- Reciprocal of $\frac{7}{3}$ is $\frac{3}{7}$

## Decimals

- Place value: $4.732 = 4+\frac{7}{10}+\frac{3}{100}+\frac{2}{1000}$
- Compare: line up points, pad zeros → $0.45 > 0.40$
- Frac→dec: divide → $\frac{3}{4}=0.75$ · Dec→frac: over power of 10 → $0.75=\frac{75}{100}=\frac34$
- × by 10/100/1000 → point moves **right** 1/2/3 · ÷ → point moves **left**
- dec × dec: total decimal places = sum of both · e.g. $0.3\times0.04=0.012$ (3 places)
- ÷ by a decimal: make divisor whole → $\frac{2.5}{0.5}=\frac{25}{5}=5$

**⚠️** $\frac23+\frac45=\frac68$ ✗ (need common denominator) · ÷ flips to ×, don't multiply directly · $0.5\times0.5=2.5$ ✗ (=0.25) · $0.5<0.45$ ✗ ($0.50>0.45$)
