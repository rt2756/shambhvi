# Comparing Quantities

**Ratio** $a:b$ (same units) · equivalent: ×/÷ both terms by the same number
**Proportion** $a:b=c:d \Rightarrow a\times d=b\times c$
**Unitary method:** find 1 unit → multiply · e.g. 5 books ₹150 → 1 = ₹30 → 8 = ₹240

## Percentage

- $x\% = \frac{x}{100}$ · frac→%: ×100 · dec→%: point right 2
- $x\%$ of $N = \frac{x}{100}\times N$ · e.g. $25\%$ of ₹400 = ₹100

## Profit / Loss (always on CP)

- Profit $= SP-CP$ · Loss $= CP-SP$
- Profit% $= \dfrac{\text{Profit}}{CP}\times100$ · e.g. CP ₹2000, SP ₹2500 → 25%

## Simple Interest

- $SI = \dfrac{P\times R\times T}{100}$ · Amount $= P+SI$ · **T in years**
- e.g. ₹5000, 8%, 2 yr → $SI=$ ₹800, Amount ₹5800

**⚠️** % always on **CP** not SP · convert to same units before a ratio · 6 months = 0.5 yr in SI
