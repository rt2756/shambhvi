# Symmetry

**Line of symmetry** = divides a shape into mirror-image halves

## Lines of symmetry

- Regular polygon with $n$ sides → **$n$ lines**
- Equilateral △ 3 · Square 4 · Reg. pentagon 5 · Reg. hexagon 6 · Circle ∞

## Rotational symmetry

- Looks the same after a turn $<360°$ about a fixed **centre**
- **Angle of rotation** = smallest turn that matches
- **Order** $=\dfrac{360°}{\text{angle}}$
- Equilateral △: $120°$, order 3 · Square: $90°$, order 4 · Hexagon: $60°$, order 6
- Letter S → order 2, no line · Letter A → 1 line, order 1

**⚠️** rectangle has **2** lines, not 4 (diagonals aren't lines of symmetry) · order 1 = no rotational symmetry · centre ≠ vertex
