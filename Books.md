# Shambhvi's Books — Class 7

Reference list of the books Shambhvi studies from. The NCERT textbooks are downloaded and
kept locally; the supplementary titles are commercial books (buy / borrow legally).

## School textbooks — NCERT (downloaded)

Stored locally in `ncert_books/` (gitignored; full catalogue with page counts in
`ncert_books/INDEX.md`). The library covers **classes 5–10**, core academic subjects.
**Class 7** specifically:

| Subject | Book | File |
|---|---|---|
| Mathematics | Ganita Prakash, Part I & II | `ncert_books/class_7/maths_part1.pdf`, `maths_part2.pdf` |
| Science | Curiosity | `ncert_books/class_7/science.pdf` |
| Social Science | Exploring Society: India and Beyond, Part I & II | `ncert_books/class_7/social-science_part1.pdf`, `social-science_part2.pdf` |
| English | Poorvi | `ncert_books/class_7/english.pdf` |

Classes 5, 6, 8, 9, 10 (same core subjects) are also downloaded for reference.

## Supplementary book — Class 7

- **R.D. Sharma — *Mathematics for Class 7*** (Dhanpat Rai Publications). Extra maths practice
  alongside NCERT; same concepts, more problems.
  *Commercial / copyrighted — not downloaded. Buy (~₹300–550) or borrow.*

## General English resources (not school-issued)

- **Word Power Made Easy — Norman Lewis.** Vocabulary building.
  *Commercial — buy (~₹150–250) or borrow on archive.org.*
- **Wren & Martin — *High School English Grammar & Composition*** (S. Chand). Grammar reference.
  *Commercial — buy (~₹200–400) or borrow on archive.org.*
