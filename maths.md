# NUMBERS

## 1. Types of Numbers

- **Natural numbers**: 1, 2, 3, … — counting numbers, start at **1**
- **Whole numbers**: 0, 1, 2, 3, … — natural numbers **+ 0**
- **Integers**: …, −3, −2, −1, 0, 1, 2, 3, … — negatives + 0 + positives
- **Positive** > 0 · **Negative** < 0 · **0 is neither** positive nor negative
- **Even**: ends in 0, 2, 4, 6, 8 (divisible by 2) · **Odd**: ends in 1, 3, 5, 7, 9
- **Prime** (positive integers only): exactly **2 factors** — 1 and itself → 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, …
- **Composite**: more than 2 factors → 4, 6, 8, 9, 10, …
- **1** = neither prime nor composite (only 1 factor) · **2** = the only even prime

**⚠️** 1 is not prime · not every odd number is prime (9, 15, 21, 25 are composite) · 2 is even **and** prime

## 2. Factors & Multiples

- **Factor / divisor**: divides a number exactly (remainder 0). e.g. 4 is a factor of 12.
  - 1 is a factor of every number · every number is a factor of itself · factors are **finite**
  - Factors of 24 → 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24
- **Multiple**: a number × 1, 2, 3, … e.g. multiples of 5 → 5, 10, 15, … · multiples are **infinite**
- **Common factors** = factors of both. e.g. 14 & 36 → 1, 2
- **Common multiples** = multiples of both. e.g. 3 & 5 → 15, 30, 45, … · the smallest one = **LCM**
- **Twin primes** = primes differing by 2 → (3,5), (5,7), (11,13), (17,19), …
- **Co-prime** = only common factor is 1 → 4 & 9, 18 & 35
  - need not be prime themselves · any two different primes are co-prime
  - co-prime → first common multiple = their **product** (3 & 5 → 15)

### Prime factorisation
- Write a number as a product of primes. 56 = 2 × 2 × 2 × 7 · 36 = 2 × 2 × 3 × 3
- Unique (order doesn't matter) · 1 has none
- **Check divisibility:** A is divisible by B if **all** of B's prime factors (with repeats) are inside A.
  - 168 = 2×2×2×3×7 is divisible by 12 = 2×2×3 ✓
  - 42 = 2×3×7 is **not** divisible by 12 ✗ (12 needs two 2s, 42 has one)

### Divisibility tests
- **by 2** → last digit 0, 2, 4, 6, 8
- **by 5** → last digit 0 or 5
- **by 10** → last digit 0
- **by 4** → last **two** digits divisible by 4 (8536 ✓ because 36 ÷ 4)
- **by 8** → last **three** digits divisible by 8 (8560 ✓ because 560 ÷ 8)

**⚠️** factors **divide** the number, multiples are **made by multiplying** it · repeated prime factors matter for divisibility

## 3. Integers — NCERT Ch 1

**Integer** = …, −3, −2, −1, 0, 1, 2, 3, … (no fractions/decimals). 0 is neither + nor −.

**Order:** further right on the number line = bigger → −2 > −5 · every negative < every positive.

### Addition
- Same sign → add values, keep the sign · e.g. (−4) + (−3) = −7
- Different signs → subtract smaller from larger, keep the larger's sign · e.g. 7 + (−2) = 5
- a + 0 = a

### Subtraction
- a − b = a + (−b) · two minuses make a plus · e.g. 4 − (−3) = 4 + 3 = 7

### Multiply & divide — signs
- Same signs → **+** · different signs → **−**
- (−6) × 4 = −24 · (−7) × (−3) = 21 · (−24) ÷ 6 = −4
- Negatives in a product: odd count → −, even count → + · e.g. (−2)^3 = −8

### Properties (hold for + and ×, not − or ÷)
- Closure · Commutative a + b = b + a · Associative (a + b) + c = a + (b + c)
- Identity a + 0 = a, a × 1 = a · Zero a × 0 = 0 · Distributive a(b + c) = ab + ac
- Shortcut · e.g. 7 × 99 = 7 × (100 − 1) = 693

**⚠️** −3 > −8 (not −8 > −3) · 4 − (−3) = 7 · (−2)^3 = −8 (not +8) · 0 ÷ 7 = 0 but 7 ÷ 0 is undefined

## 4. Exponents & Powers — NCERT Ch 11

a^n = a multiplied by itself n times · a = **base**, n = **exponent/power** · 2^3 = 8, 5^4 = 625

**Signs:** (+)^n → + · (−)^n → + if n is even, − if n is odd · (−2)^4 = 16, (−2)^3 = −8

### Laws (same base)
- Product: a^m × a^n = a^(m+n) · 2^3 × 2^4 = 2^7
- Quotient: a^m ÷ a^n = a^(m−n) · 5^7 ÷ 5^2 = 5^5
- Power of a power: (a^m)^n = a^(m×n) · (3^2)^4 = 3^8
- Power of a product: (a × b)^n = a^n × b^n
- Zero exponent: a^0 = 1 (a ≠ 0)

### Standard form & large numbers
- k × 10^n, with 1 ≤ k < 10 · e.g. 38,000,000 = 3.8 × 10^7
- Compare: bigger exponent wins; tie → compare k
- Hundred 10^2 · Thousand 10^3 · Million 10^6 · Billion 10^9 · Trillion 10^12

**⚠️** 2^3 = 8 (not 6) · multiply same base → **add** exponents · a^0 = 1 (not 0) · (−3)^2 = 9 (not −9)

## 5. Fractions & Decimals — NCERT Ch 2

**Fraction** = part of a whole. In 3/4: 3 = **numerator**, 4 = **denominator**.

- **Proper** num < den, value 0–1 (4/7) · **Improper** num ≥ den, value ≥ 1 (7/4) · **Mixed** whole + fraction (2 1/4)
- **Like** = same denominator (2/7, 5/7) · **Unlike** = different (2/3, 1/6)
- **Equivalent** = same value; × or ÷ num & den by the same number (1/2 = 2/4 = 3/6)

### Convert & simplify
- Improper ↔ Mixed: 11/4 = 2 3/4 (quotient + remainder/divisor) · 2 3/4 = (4×2 + 3)/4 = 11/4
- Simplest form: divide num & den by the same number. 75/100 = 3/4 (÷25) · 6/12 = 1/2

### Compare
- Like → compare numerators (5/9 > 3/9)
- Unlike → make denominators equal using LCM. 2/3 = 8/12, 3/4 = 9/12 → 2/3 < 3/4

### Add / subtract
- Like → add/subtract numerators (3/4 + 2/4 = 5/4 = 1 1/4)
- Unlike → make denominators equal first (1/4 + 1/6 = 3/12 + 2/12 = 5/12)

### Multiply / divide
- Multiply: tops × tops, bottoms × bottoms → 2/3 × 4/5 = 8/15 · fraction × whole: 3 × 2/5 = 6/5
- **"of" means ×** → 1/2 of 18 = 9
- Divide: × the **reciprocal** → 2/3 ÷ 4/5 = 2/3 × 5/4 = 5/6 · reciprocal of 7/3 is 3/7

### Decimals
- Place value: 4.732 = 4 + 7/10 + 3/100 + 2/1000
- Compare: line up the point, pad zeros → 0.45 > 0.40
- Fraction → decimal: divide (3/4 = 0.75) · decimal → fraction: over a power of 10 (0.75 = 75/100 = 3/4 · 0.5643 = 5643/10000)
- × by 10/100/1000 → point moves **right** 1/2/3 places · ÷ → point moves **left**
- decimal × decimal: total decimal places = sum of both (0.3 × 0.04 = 0.012, 3 places)
- ÷ by a decimal: make the divisor whole first (2.5 ÷ 0.5 = 25 ÷ 5 = 5)

**⚠️** 2/3 + 4/5 ≠ 6/8 (need a common denominator) · to divide, flip to × · 0.5 × 0.5 = 0.25 (not 2.5) · 0.50 > 0.45

## 6. Rational Numbers — NCERT Ch 8

**Rational number** = p/q, where p and q are integers and **q ≠ 0** · e.g. 3/4, −5/2, 7 = 7/1, 0 = 0/1
- Every integer is rational · same sign → positive, different sign → negative

### Standard form
- Denominator **positive**, fully simplified
- −8/12 → −2/3 · 4/−6 → −4/6 → −2/3

### Compare
- Same denominator → compare numerators (2/7 < 5/7)
- Different → make denominators equal via LCM (2/3 vs 3/4 → 8/12 vs 9/12)
- Any negative < any positive

### Operations
- ± : same denominator → ± numerators; different → LCM first (1/4 + 1/6 = 3/12 + 2/12 = 5/12)
- × : tops × tops, bottoms × bottoms (2/3 × −4/5 = −8/15)
- ÷ : × the reciprocal (2/3 ÷ 4/5 = 2/3 × 5/4 = 5/6)
- Sign rules are the same as for integers

**⚠️** denominator is never 0 · −3/4 = 3/−4 (both equal −3/4) · add only after denominators are equal · compare by **value**, not digit size

# ALGEBRA

## 7. Algebraic Expressions — NCERT Ch 10

- **Variable** = a letter standing for a number · **Constant** = a fixed number
- **Term** = numbers × variables (4x, −3xy, 7) · **Coefficient** = the numeric factor (in −3xy it is −3)
- **Expression** = terms joined by + or −

### Like vs unlike terms
- **Like** = same variables, same powers (5x & −2x ✓ · 3xy & 7xy ✓)
- **Unlike** = 4x & 4y, or 5x & 5x^2 · **only like terms combine**

### Types (by number of terms)
- Monomial 1 (7xy) · Binomial 2 (4x + 3) · Trinomial 3 (x^2 − 5x + 6)

### Add / subtract
- Combine like terms' coefficients → (3x + 2y) + (5x − y) = 8x + y
- Subtraction flips **every** sign → (7x + 3) − (2x − 4) = 5x + 7

### Value (substitution)
- 4x − 5 at x = 3 → 4(3) − 5 = 7 · x^2 − 2x + 1 at x = −2 → 4 + 4 + 1 = 9
- n-th term: multiples of 3 → 3n · even → 2n · odd → 2n − 1

**⚠️** 3x + 4y ≠ 7xy (unlike) · −(2x − 4) = −2x + 4 (not −2x − 4) · x^2 and x are unlike · coefficient of −3xy is −3

## 8. Simple Equations — NCERT Ch 4

- **Variable** = the unknown (x) · **Equation** = two expressions joined by = (think of a balance scale)
- **From words:** pick a variable, translate "is/equals" → =. "3 more than twice the age is 19" → 2x + 3 = 19

### Balance method (do the same operation to both sides)
- x − 5 = 12 → add 5 → x = 17
- 4x = 20 → ÷ 4 → x = 5
- 3x + 7 = 22 → −7 → 3x = 15 → ÷ 3 → x = 5

### Transposition (move a term across =, flip its operation)
- + 5 becomes − 5 · × 3 becomes ÷ 3
- 3x + 7 = 22 → 3x = 22 − 7 = 15 → x = 15/3 = 5
- **Check:** put the answer back; both sides must match

**⚠️** do the **same** thing to both sides · transposing 4x = 20 gives x = 20 ÷ 4 (not 20 − 4) · never ÷ by 0 · always verify by substituting back

# RATIO & PERCENTAGE

## 9. Comparing Quantities — NCERT Ch 7

- **Ratio** a : b (same units). Equivalent ratios: × or ÷ both terms by the same number.
- **Proportion** a : b = c : d → a × d = b × c
- **Unitary method:** find the value of 1 unit, then multiply. 5 books ₹150 → 1 = ₹30 → 8 = ₹240

### Percentage
- x% = x/100 · fraction → %: × 100 · decimal → %: move point right 2 places
- x% of N = (x/100) × N → 25% of ₹400 = ₹100

### Profit / Loss (always calculated on CP)
- CP = cost price, SP = selling price
- Profit = SP − CP · Loss = CP − SP
- Profit% = (Profit / CP) × 100 → CP ₹2000, SP ₹2500 → 25%

### Simple Interest
- SI = (P × R × T) / 100 · Amount = P + SI · **T is in years**
- e.g. ₹5000, 8%, 2 years → SI = ₹800, Amount = ₹5800

**⚠️** percentage is always on **CP**, not SP · convert to the same units before making a ratio · 6 months = 0.5 year in SI

# GEOMETRY

## 10. Lines & Angles — NCERT Ch 5

**Angle types:** acute < 90° · right = 90° · obtuse 90°–180° · straight = 180° · reflex 180°–360°
- **Perpendicular** = two lines meeting at 90°
- **Clock:** 30° per hour, 6° per minute, 6° per second (360° ÷ 12 = 30°; 360° ÷ 60 = 6°)

### Angle pairs
- **Complementary** → sum = 90° (35° + 55°)
- **Supplementary** → sum = 180° (110° + 70°)
- **Adjacent** = common vertex + common arm
- **Linear pair** = adjacent, outer arms form a straight line → supplementary
- **Vertically opposite** (two lines cross) → **equal** (40° opposite 40°)

### Transversal cutting two parallel lines
- **Corresponding** → equal · **Alternate interior** → equal · **Co-interior** (same side) → supplementary (180°)
- **Test for parallel:** lines are parallel if any one holds — corresponding equal / alternate equal / co-interior sum 180°

**⚠️** Complementary before Supplementary → 90 before 180 · "linear pair" only if outer arms form a straight line · angle equalities hold **only** when the lines are parallel

## 11. Polygons & Shapes

- **Triangle** (3 sides, 3 corners): equilateral (all sides & angles equal) · isosceles (2 equal) · scalene (none equal) · right-angled (one 90° angle)
- **Square**: 4 equal sides, 4 right angles, opposite sides parallel
- **Rectangle**: 4 right angles, opposite sides equal & parallel
- **Rhombus** (leaning square): 4 equal sides, no right angles, opposite sides parallel
- **Parallelogram** (leaning rectangle): opposite sides equal & parallel, no right angle
- **Regular polygon** = all sides equal **AND** all angles equal. Angle turned at the centre = 360° ÷ n (pentagon 72°, hexagon 60°)
- Sides → name: Pentagon 5 · Hexagon 6 · Septagon 7 · Octagon 8 · Nonagon 9 · Decagon 10

**⚠️** "regular" needs **both** equal sides and equal angles · a rhombus is not a square (no right angles)

## 12. The Triangle & Its Properties — NCERT Ch 6

**By sides:** scalene (none equal) · isosceles (2 equal) · equilateral (all equal)
**By angles:** acute · right · obtuse

### Lines in a triangle
- **Median** = vertex → midpoint of the opposite side · 3 medians meet at the **centroid**
- **Altitude** = perpendicular from a vertex to the opposite side

### Key properties
- **Angle sum = 180°** → 50° + 70° + ? → ? = 60°
- **Exterior angle** = sum of the two opposite interior angles → 50° + 70° = 120°
- **Equilateral** → all angles 60° · **Isosceles** → angles opposite equal sides are equal
- **Triangle inequality:** sum of any two sides > the third side. 3, 4, 8 ✗ (3 + 4 < 8) · 5, 6, 7 ✓

### Pythagoras (right triangle only)
- a^2 + b^2 = c^2, where c = hypotenuse (longest side, opposite the right angle)
- e.g. 3^2 + 4^2 = 9 + 16 = 25 = 5^2 · the reverse also proves a right angle

**⚠️** angles always sum to 180° · median ≠ altitude · Pythagoras only for right triangles · you must **square**: 3 + 4 ≠ 5, it's 3^2 + 4^2 = 5^2

## 13. Symmetry — NCERT Ch 12

- **Line of symmetry** = divides a shape into mirror-image halves
- Regular polygon with n sides → **n lines**. Equilateral △ 3 · Square 4 · Regular pentagon 5 · Regular hexagon 6 · Circle infinite

### Rotational symmetry
- Looks the same after a turn of less than 360° about a fixed **centre**
- **Angle of rotation** = smallest turn that matches · **Order** = 360° ÷ angle
- Equilateral △ → 120°, order 3 · Square → 90°, order 4 · Hexagon → 60°, order 6
- Letter S → order 2, no line · Letter A → 1 line, order 1

**⚠️** a rectangle has **2** lines of symmetry, not 4 (diagonals are not lines of symmetry) · order 1 = no rotational symmetry · centre ≠ vertex

## 14. Visualising Solid Shapes — NCERT Ch 13

- **2D** = flat (length, breadth) · **3D** = solid (length, breadth, height)
- **Face** = flat surface · **Edge** = where 2 faces meet · **Vertex** = corner point

| Solid | Faces | Edges | Vertices |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cube / Cuboid | 6 | 12 | 8 |
| Triangular prism | 5 | 9 | 6 |
| Square pyramid | 5 | 8 | 5 |
| Tetrahedron | 4 | 6 | 4 |
| Cylinder | 2 flat + 1 curved | 2 | 0 |
| Cone | 1 flat + 1 curved | 1 | 1 |
| Sphere | 1 curved | 0 | 0 |

- **Net** = an unfolded flat shape that folds back into the solid (a cube has 11 nets)
- **Oblique sketch** = freehand · **Isometric sketch** = on dot paper, true scale
- **Views:** top / front / side. A cylinder standing up → top = circle, front = rectangle.

**⚠️** a curved surface is not a flat face · the line where faces meet is an edge (not a vertex) · a net (unfolded) is not a view (2D projection)

# MENSURATION

## 15. Perimeter & Area — NCERT Ch 9

**Perimeter** = distance around (length units) · **Area** = surface covered (square units)

### Formulas
- **Square** (side s): P = 4s · A = s^2
- **Rectangle** (length l, breadth b): P = 2(l + b) · A = l × b
- **Parallelogram**: A = b × h (h = perpendicular height)
- **Triangle**: A = 1/2 × b × h
- **Circle** (radius r): Circumference = 2 × π × r · Area = π × r^2 · diameter d = 2r · π ≈ 22/7 ≈ 3.14
- e.g. circle r = 35 cm → A = 22/7 × 35 × 35 = 3850 cm²

### Units
- Length: 1 cm = 10 mm · 1 m = 100 cm · 1 km = 1000 m
- Weight: 1 g = 1000 mg · 1 kg = 1000 g
- Capacity: 1 L = 1000 ml
- Area: 1 m² = 10000 cm² · 1 km² = 1,000,000 m² · 1 cm² = 100 mm²

**⚠️** perimeter is in cm, area is in cm² · height must be **perpendicular**, not the slant side · don't drop the 1/2 in triangle area · in π × r^2 use the **radius** (r = d/2)

# DATA

## 16. Data Handling — NCERT Ch 3

- **Data** = collected numbers/facts · **Frequency** = how many times a value occurs

### Representative values
- **Mean** = sum ÷ count → 8, 7, 9, 6, 10 → 40/5 = 8
- **Median** = middle of the **sorted** data → 6, 7, 8, 9, 10 → 8 · even count → average of the two middle values
- **Mode** = most frequent value → 5, 7, 5, 8, 5 → 5 · a list can have none or several modes

### Bar graphs
- Equal-width bars, height = frequency · a **double bar graph** compares two groups
- Always label both axes and write the scale

### Probability
- P(event) = favourable outcomes ÷ total outcomes · always between 0 and 1
- 0 = impossible · 1 = certain
- Coin: P(heads) = 1/2 · Die: P(4) = 1/6, P(even) = 3/6 = 1/2

**⚠️** mean ≠ median (median needs sorting first) · mode = **most** frequent · P is never > 1 · a bar graph with no scale is meaningless
